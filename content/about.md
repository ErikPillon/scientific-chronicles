Science history is usually told as a list of dates and dead names. It shouldn't be. It's thousands of years of curious, stubborn, occasionally reckless people arguing with reality until it gave up a secret.

Scientific Chronicles is our attempt to make that history feel alive again: pick any day of the year and see who was born, who died, and what got discovered, invented, or blown up in the process.

## How we build each entry

Every birth, death, and breakthrough is cross-checked against primary and reputable secondary sources before it goes live — we'd rather cut a good anecdote than publish a shaky one. Found something that looks off? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll fix it.

## Where to go from here

Poke around, click a random day, and see what kind of world-altering mischief was happening while the rest of the world was fast asleep.
