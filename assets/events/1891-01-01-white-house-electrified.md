Imagine living in the late 19th century, where gas lamps still flickered in stately homes. Then, boom! Electricity arrives. In 1891, President Benjamin Harrison was the first to bask in the glow of electric lights in the White House. While it was a huge step forward, Harrison and his wife were apparently a bit wary of flicking the switches themselves, fearing electric shocks! So, the staff were often left to illuminate and darken the rooms. A true testament to the early days of electric power – revolutionary, but still a little mysterious for those at the top. It certainly lit up the political stage, literally!
