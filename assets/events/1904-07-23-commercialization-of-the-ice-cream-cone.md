Picture this: It's the 1904 St. Louis World's Fair, it's hot, and an ice cream vendor runs out of dishes. Right next door, a Syrian waffle vendor, Ernest Hamwi (or possibly Charles R. Menches, depending on who you ask), steps up, rolling his crisp waffles into cones. Voila! The ice cream cone was born, marrying two delicious treats and eliminating the need for plates. This ingenious (and deliciously practical) solution revolutionized how we eat ice cream, making it perfectly portable and delightfully edible. It's a sweet tale of innovation under pressure that continues to bring joy to sticky-fingered enthusiasts everywhere.
