Forget Jules Verne, Simon Lake was the real Captain Nemo! While others were tinkering with subs primarily for military use, Lake built the *Argonaut I* in 1897 with a different goal: underwater exploration and salvage. This wasn't just a bathtub toy; it was the first submarine proven capable of operating successfully in the *open ocean*. It featured wheels for rolling on the seabed and an airlock for divers to exit and work underwater, essentially making it a true underwater workhorse. Lake's *Argonaut* proved that submarines weren't just for war; they could be vital tools for exploration, industry, and perhaps even unlocking the ocean's secrets. He literally opened up a whole new world beneath the waves.
