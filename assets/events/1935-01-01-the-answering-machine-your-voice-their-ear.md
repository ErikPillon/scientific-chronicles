Before you could just swipe to silence a call, there was a revolutionary gadget that promised to catch your messages when you were out: the answering machine! While various pioneers tinkered with early concepts, Austrian inventor William Muller created a functional recording device for telephone calls in 1935, though it wasn't exactly pocket-sized. Fast forward to the 1950s, and Japanese inventor Kazuo Hashimoto brought us the 'Ansafone,' arguably the first commercially successful answering machine. It meant no more frantic dashes to the phone or missed opportunities. These clunky, tape-driven marvels laid the groundwork for modern voicemail, giving us the freedom to miss a call without missing the message. So, next time you hear 'Please leave a message after the tone,' give a little nod to these unsung heroes of telecommunication!
