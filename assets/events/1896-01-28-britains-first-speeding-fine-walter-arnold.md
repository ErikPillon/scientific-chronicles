Ever complained about a speeding ticket? Spare a thought for Walter Arnold. On January 28, 1896, Mr. Arnold earned the dubious distinction of receiving Britain's first-ever speeding fine. He was caught by a policeman (who had to chase him on a bicycle, mind you!) in Paddock Wood, Kent, driving his Benz at 8 mph – four times the legal speed limit of 2 mph! Yes, you read that right, 2 mph. He was fined one shilling plus costs, a hefty sum for the day. This incident, just a few months before the infamous "Emancipation Run" that raised the speed limit to 14 mph, hilariously highlights the early, somewhat absurd, attempts to regulate a technology that was still finding its feet (or wheels).
