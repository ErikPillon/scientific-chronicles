Before apps and ATMs, banks still wanted to make things easier for their clientele. The 'autobank' concept, most famously realized as the drive-thru bank, was a game-changer for car-obsessed America in the early 20th century. Imagine not even having to park your car to deposit a check or grab some cash! The first recognized drive-thru bank opened in St. Louis, Missouri, in 1930, marking a significant shift in customer service and accessibility. It was all about convenience, changing how people interacted with their money, and adding a dash of automotive flair to finance, making banking as quick as a pit stop. This innovation underscored a growing trend in urban planning and commercial design, adapting services to the burgeoning car culture.
