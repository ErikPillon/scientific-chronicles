Talk about a cosmic road trip! NASA's Dawn spacecraft made history on March 6, 2015, by successfully entering orbit around Ceres, the largest object in the asteroid belt and officially a dwarf planet. But here's the kicker: Dawn had already orbited Vesta, another massive asteroid, from 2011 to 2012! This made it the first-ever probe to orbit two different celestial bodies beyond Earth. Using its super-efficient ion propulsion system, Dawn meticulously mapped Ceres, revealing bright spots (later identified as salts) and evidence of cryovolcanism. It was like having a personal tour guide to the early solar system, giving us unprecedented views and data on these mysterious rocky and icy worlds. Dawn truly opened our eyes to the hidden gems of the asteroid belt!
