After inventing a practical, long-lasting incandescent light bulb, Thomas Edison wasn't just content with the science; he wanted to electrify the world! So, in 1878, with support from powerful financiers like J.P. Morgan, he founded the Edison Electric Light Company. This wasn't just a bulb-making factory; it was the blueprint for the entire electrical utility industry. From power generation to distribution and, of course, the bulbs themselves, this company laid the wires for modern living. It was the spark that ignited the Age of Electricity, proving that brilliant inventions need brilliant business to truly shine.
