Long car journeys in the UK used to mean packing a thermos and a picnic, or risking starvation. That all changed on November 2, 1959, with the grand opening of Watford Gap services on the newly minted M1 motorway. This wasn't just a petrol station; it was a revolution for weary travelers! With its restaurant, shops, and — gasp — proper toilets, it offered a much-needed pit stop. Though often mocked for its culinary delights (or lack thereof), Watford Gap became an iconic landmark, symbolizing the dawn of the motorway age and forever changing the British road trip experience, one questionable sausage roll at a time.
