Before smartphones and smart speakers, how did people wake up? Well, they had alarm clocks, but not always the handy, adjustable kind we know. While ancient Greeks had water-powered alarms and medieval monks had mechanical bells, the truly modern, adjustable mechanical alarm clock arrived thanks to Antoine Redier in France, who patented his design in 1847. No longer just a fixed wake-up call, Redier's innovation meant you could finally set your wake-up time with precision, transforming personal punctuality. It was a small but mighty invention that helped usher in the era of industrial work schedules and a more regimented daily life. Say goodbye to sleeping in and hello to the relentless march of time, perfectly punctuated by a delightful (or dreadful) morning clang!
