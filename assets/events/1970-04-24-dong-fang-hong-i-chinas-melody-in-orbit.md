On April 24, 1970, China proudly declared its entry into the space race with the launch of its first artificial satellite, Dong Fang Hong I (meaning 'The East is Red'). Unlike other nations' silent orbiters, this 173-kilogram trailblazer didn't just whizz around; it broadcast the patriotic anthem 'The East is Red' for eager listeners on Earth. Orchestrated by pioneers like Qian Xuesen, this monumental feat showcased China's growing scientific and technological prowess, proving that even a nation in turmoil could reach for the stars. It was a literal mic drop in space!
