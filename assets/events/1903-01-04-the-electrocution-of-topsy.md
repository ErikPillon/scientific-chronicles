In one of history's most disturbing spectacles, Topsy, a circus elephant deemed a danger, was publicly electrocuted at Coney Island in 1903. This wasn't just an execution; it was a grim demonstration, secretly filmed by Edison Manufacturing and exploited in the infamous 'War of the Currents.' Thomas Edison, a proponent of direct current (DC), sought to discredit alternating current (AC) by portraying it as deadly, using Topsy's death as shocking (literally!) 'proof.' While Edison wasn't directly involved in the execution, his company's filming amplified the tragedy, solidifying Topsy's sad legacy as a pawn in an electrical rivalry.
