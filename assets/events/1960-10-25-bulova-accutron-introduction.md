Forget ticking, the Bulova Accutron hummed! Launched on October 25, 1960, this wasn't just another fancy wristwatch; it was the world's first fully electronic watch. Swiss engineer Max Hetzel’s ingenious design replaced the traditional balance wheel with a miniature tuning fork, vibrating at a precise 360 Hz. This innovation made the Accutron incredibly accurate, guaranteeing accuracy to within one minute a month. It was so reliable that NASA used its movements in satellites and for timing equipment on early space missions. The Accutron's distinctive hum became its signature, making it a symbol of mid-century technological prowess and a must-have for anyone serious about punctuality, or just really cool gadgets. It truly set the standard for electronic timekeeping for decades.
