Before drones and satellites, getting a picture from the sky was quite the endeavor! The very first successful aerial photograph was taken on October 13, 1858, by French photographer and balloonist Gaspard-Félix Tournachon, better known as Nadar. He ascended over Petit-Bicêtre, near Paris, in his hot air balloon, 'Le Géant,' with a camera in tow. His pioneering image, a clear shot of the Parisian landscape from hundreds of meters up, was a monumental achievement in both photography and aeronautics. While his initial photographs have since been lost, the event itself was widely reported and recognized as a significant breakthrough. It wasn't just a cool trick; it paved the way for aerial reconnaissance, mapping, and eventually, the way we see and understand our planet from above. Nadar literally gave us a new perspective.
