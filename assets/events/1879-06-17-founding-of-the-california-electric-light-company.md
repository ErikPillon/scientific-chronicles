Imagine a world lit only by gas lamps and candles, then suddenly, bright electric light! That's what the California Electric Light Company, incorporated on June 17, 1879, brought to the West Coast. Founded by George H. Roe, this wasn't just another business venture; it was a pioneering effort in a brand-new, transformative technology. They were the first electric utility in the United States to purchase Edison dynamos, and within months, they were supplying power to businesses in San Francisco, illuminating streets and factories. Their innovative spirit helped establish the infrastructure for electric power distribution, paving the way for the widespread adoption of electricity throughout California. It was the spark that truly ignited the Golden State's embrace of modern power, forever changing its cities and industries.
