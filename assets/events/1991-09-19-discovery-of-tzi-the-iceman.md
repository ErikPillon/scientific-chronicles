The year was 1991, and two German hikers were just enjoying a stroll through the Ötztal Alps when BAM! They stumbled upon a remarkably preserved corpse sticking out of the melting ice. This wasn't just any old body; it was 'Ötzi,' the Iceman, a Neolithic dude from about 3300 BCE who'd been chilling (literally) in his icy tomb for over 5,300 years. His discovery was a massive deal for archaeologists, giving us an unprecedented peek into Copper Age life, from his clothes and tools to his last meal and even his tattoos. Forget time travel – Ötzi's well-preserved remains offer a direct link to a bygone era, proving that sometimes, the best historical finds are just waiting for a good thaw.
