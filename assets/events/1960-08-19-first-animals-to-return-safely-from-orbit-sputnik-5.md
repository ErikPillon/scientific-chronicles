Before Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space, the Soviets sent some furry pioneers. On August 19, 1960, the Sputnik 5 mission launched with a motley crew: two dogs named Belka and Strelka, a rabbit, 40 mice, 2 rats, and a whole bunch of plants and insects. After orbiting Earth 17 times, they all returned safely – a monumental success! This mission was a critical precursor to human spaceflight, proving that living organisms could survive the rigors of launch, orbit, and reentry. Belka and Strelka became instant global celebrities, proving that when it comes to space exploration, every small step (or paw print!) is a giant leap.
