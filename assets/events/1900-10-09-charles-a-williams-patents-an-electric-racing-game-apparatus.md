Long before you were glued to your console racing virtual cars, Charles A. Williams was cooking up some analog fun! On October 9, 1900, he secured US Patent 659,207 for an "Electric Racing Game Apparatus." This wasn't just a toy; it was an early precursor to what we now know as slot cars. His patent described a track with electric conductors and miniature vehicles powered by them, allowing for competitive racing. It perfectly captured the early 20th-century fascination with electricity and speed, proving that even a century ago, folks loved a good race – especially when it came with a bit of electric zest!
