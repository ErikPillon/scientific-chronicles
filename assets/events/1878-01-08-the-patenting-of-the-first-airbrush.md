Before the airbrush, achieving smooth gradients and fine details in art and photography retouching was a painstaking process. Enter Francis Edgar Stanley and Abner Peeler! Stanley is often credited with the first spray painting device, and Peeler patented the "paint distributor" or "atomizer" in 1878, which is recognized as the first true airbrush. Peeler's device used a foot-operated compressor to spray liquid color, allowing artists to create incredibly smooth blends and precise lines that brushes simply couldn't replicate. It wasn't just for art; it quickly became indispensable for photo retouching, commercial illustration, and even early cosmetics, forever changing how detailed imagery could be produced.
