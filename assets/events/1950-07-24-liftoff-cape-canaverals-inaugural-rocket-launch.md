Long before tourists flocked to watch shuttle launches, on July 24, 1950, Cape Canaveral's launch pads saw their very first action. A two-stage Bumper 8 rocket, a souped-up V-2 missile with a WAC Corporal rocket perched on top, screamed into the Florida sky. This wasn't some flashy space mission aiming for the stars; it was a test, primarily to study high-altitude flight and propel the US's fledgling rocket program forward. While it didn't carry any humans or satellites, this launch from a desolate strip of sand marked the true beginning of Cape Canaveral as the epicentre of America's space dreams.
