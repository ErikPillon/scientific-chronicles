On May 21, 2021, a single page, handwritten letter penned by none other than Albert Einstein, containing his iconic E=mc² equation, went under the hammer for a jaw-dropping $1.2 million. This isn't just any old scribbled note; dating back to 1946, the letter was addressed to Polish-American physicist Ludwik Silberstein, who had posed questions challenging Einstein's theories. It's a fantastic, albeit expensive, window into the mind of a genius at work, reminding us that some insights are literally priceless – or at least, million-dollar-pricey. Clearly, some 'doodles' are worth more than a gallery of masterpieces.
