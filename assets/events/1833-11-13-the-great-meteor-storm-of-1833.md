On the chilly night of November 13, 1833, the heavens put on a show for the ages across North America. The Leonids meteor shower erupted into a storm so intense, it reportedly rained down thousands of meteors per minute, startling people awake and inspiring awe and fear in equal measure. This wasn't just any light show; it was the first widely and accurately recorded meteor shower in U.S. history, prompting serious scientific study. Yale professor Denison Olmsted was one of the first to deduce that the meteors originated from space, not the Earth's atmosphere. Before this, many thought meteors were simply atmospheric phenomena. The 1833 Leonids proved them wonderfully wrong, kickstarting a new era of meteor science and reminding everyone that space is full of surprises!
