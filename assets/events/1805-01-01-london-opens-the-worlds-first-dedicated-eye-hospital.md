Before 1805, if your peepers were in trouble, you were pretty much out of luck or at the mercy of general surgeons. But that all changed when John Cunningham Saunders, a visionary surgeon, founded the London Eye Infirmary in 1805 (which later became the famous Moorfields Eye Hospital). This wasn't just another clinic; it was the world's *first* dedicated hospital solely for treating eye diseases, a revolutionary idea! It brought specialized care to the masses, turning ophthalmology into a distinct medical field and setting the standard for eye care globally. Talk about having a clear vision!
