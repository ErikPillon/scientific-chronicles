In the early 1960s, space was primarily the domain of superpowers. But a determined group of ham radio operators in California had other ideas. Calling themselves Project OSCAR (Orbiting Satellite Carrying Amateur Radio), they secretly designed and built a tiny satellite in a garage. On December 12, 1961, OSCAR 1 hitched a ride aboard a U.S. Air Force rocket, becoming the world's very first privately-built satellite! For 22 days, it broadcast a simple 'HI' in Morse code to ground stations worldwide, proving that space wasn't just for governments. This incredible grassroots achievement inspired a generation of amateur radio enthusiasts and laid the groundwork for today's booming private space industry. It showed that with enough ingenuity and a bit of radio magic, anyone could send their dreams into orbit. Take that, big government budgets!
