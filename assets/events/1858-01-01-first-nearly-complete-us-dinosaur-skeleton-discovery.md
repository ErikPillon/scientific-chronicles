Before 'Jurassic Park,' there was a New Jersey marl pit! In 1858, William Parker Foulke was digging around in Haddonfield when he stumbled upon some seriously massive bones. He called in paleontologist Joseph Leidy, who quickly realized these weren't just any old bones – they belonged to a gigantic, bipedal creature. Leidy named it *Hadrosaurus foulkii*, the first nearly complete dinosaur skeleton discovered in the United States. This wasn't just a fossil find; it was a revelation that proved dinosaurs were real, massive, and once walked (or waddled) right across America. It kicked off the Great Dinosaur Rush and ignited a nation's fascination with prehistoric giants!
