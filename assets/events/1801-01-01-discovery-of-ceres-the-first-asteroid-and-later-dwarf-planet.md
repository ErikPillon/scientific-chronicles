On New Year's Day, 1801, Italian astronomer Giuseppe Piazzi was meticulously mapping stars from Sicily when he stumbled upon a faint, moving object. He initially thought it was a comet, but it turned out to be something much more significant: Ceres, the first-ever asteroid discovered, orbiting between Mars and Jupiter. For decades, it was even considered a planet! Fast forward to 2006, and Ceres got a promotion (or demotion, depending on your view) to 'dwarf planet.' It's the largest object in the main asteroid belt, a true pioneer in our understanding of our solar system's lesser-known residents.
