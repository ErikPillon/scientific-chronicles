Before 1904, shaving was a perilous affair involving open blades and a prayer. Enter King C. Gillette, a man who clearly thought, "There has to be a better way!" He wasn't the first to think of a safety razor, but his design, patented on November 15, 1904, changed the game. Gillette's genius was in creating a thin, disposable, double-edged blade that could be held securely in a reusable handle. This wasn't just about safety; it was about convenience and affordability, democratizing the daily ritual of shaving and making those morning routines a lot less likely to end in a bloodbath. It kicked off a disposable product revolution and cemented Gillette's name (and face!) into history, one clean-shaven mug at a time.
