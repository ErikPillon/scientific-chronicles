Picture this: 1965, chemist James M. Schlatter is trying to create a new ulcer drug. He accidentally licks his finger (oops, probably don't do that in a lab!) and tastes something incredibly sweet. Turns out, he'd synthesized aspartame, a compound hundreds of times sweeter than sugar with virtually no calories. This happy accident gave us one of the most popular artificial sweeteners, revolutionizing the diet food and drink industry. From diet sodas to sugar-free gum, aspartame became a household name, proving that sometimes, the best discoveries are made when you're looking for something else entirely... and maybe a little bit careless with lab safety.
