A momentous merger on April 15, 1892, brought together Edison General Electric Company and Thomson-Houston Electric Company, sparking the creation of General Electric. This wasn't just another corporate handshake; it consolidated some of the brightest minds and most crucial patents in electricity, setting the stage for GE to light up homes, power factories, and innovate everything from appliances to jet engines. It was the moment a vision for electrified living really started to glow, shaping much of the modern world we now take for granted.
