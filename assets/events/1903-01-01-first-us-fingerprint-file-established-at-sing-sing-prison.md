Forget grainy mugshots; the early 20th century brought a revolutionary new way to identify criminals: fingerprints! In 1903, Sing Sing Prison in New York became the first U.S. institution to establish a systematic fingerprint file, applying the Henry Classification System – a method already gaining traction globally. This wasn't just about catching bad guys; it was a massive leap forward for forensic science, providing an undeniable, unique identifier for every individual. No more mistaken identities or getting away with a new alias; your prints told the undeniable truth. This move set the stage for modern criminal identification across the nation, making 'leaving your mark' a whole lot more incriminating.
