On April 15, 1964, the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel officially opened, instantly becoming hailed as one of the 'Seven Wonders of the Modern World' by many. This colossal 17.6-mile-long (28.3 km) structure isn't just a bridge; it's a seamless fusion of bridges, tunnels, artificial islands, and causeways that spans the mouth of the Chesapeake Bay, linking Virginia's Eastern Shore with its mainland. It was an audacious feat of civil engineering, dramatically cutting travel times and transforming commerce for the region, proving that with enough concrete, ingenuity, and a hefty budget, even the most ambitious aquatic crossings are possible. It truly revolutionized travel along the East Coast!
