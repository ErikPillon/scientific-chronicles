Not content with just lighting up homes, Thomas Edison also set his sights on electrifying transportation. On May 13, 1880, at his Menlo Park laboratory, he unveiled and successfully ran his first electric passenger train. This wasn't just a toy; it was a full-sized locomotive pulling several cars, powered by a third rail system. Spectators were amazed as it zipped along a short track, proving that electric power could move heavy loads efficiently. It was an early, pivotal step towards the electric railways and subways that crisscross our world today, showing Edison was a true visionary in every sense.
