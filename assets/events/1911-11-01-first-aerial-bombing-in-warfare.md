In a chilling precursor to modern aerial warfare, Italian pilot Lieutenant Giulio Gavotti conducted the first-ever air bombing during the Italo-Turkish War. Flying a primitive Etrich Taube monoplane over Ain Zara and Tagiura (modern-day Libya), Gavotti casually dropped four small bombs by hand onto Turkish positions. While the bombs caused little damage, the psychological impact was immense, proving that the sky was no longer a sanctuary. It marked a grim turning point, demonstrating the airplane's potential as a weapon and ushering in an entirely new dimension of conflict.
