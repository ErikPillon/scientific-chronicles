Imagine using extreme cold to treat what ails you – that's cryosurgery! While ancient Egyptians dabbled with cold for wounds, it wasn't until Dr. Irving S. Cooper got really innovative in the early 1960s that modern cryosurgery took off. He developed probes using liquid nitrogen, turning freezing into a precise surgical tool. Suddenly, tumors and chronic pain could be tackled with less invasion, proving that sometimes, the coldest approach is the coolest solution for targeted treatments.
