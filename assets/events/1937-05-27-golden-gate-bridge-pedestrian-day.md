Imagine strolling across one of the world's most iconic bridges before a single car had crossed it! That's exactly what happened on May 27, 1937, when San Francisco's Golden Gate Bridge opened its mighty span to pedestrians only for a grand 15 hours. A whopping 200,000 people paid 25 cents each to be among the first to experience the breathtaking views and the sheer scale of this engineering marvel. It was a massive civic celebration, a chance for ordinary folks to touch history and claim a piece of the 'Bridge That Couldn't Be Built' before traffic roared to life the very next day. A truly golden moment in architectural history!
