On May 18, 1969, Apollo 10 launched, basically a full-dress rehearsal for the actual moon landing. Astronauts Tom Stafford, John Young, and Gene Cernan flew all the way to the moon, and Stafford and Cernan even took the Lunar Module, affectionately nicknamed 'Snoopy,' for a spin, descending to within a mere 9 miles of the lunar surface! They meticulously scoped out the landing site for Apollo 11, took crucial photographs, and tested all the systems, making sure everything was tip-top for the big one. The only thing they didn't do was land. NASA famously put just enough fuel in Snoopy's ascent stage to prevent any eager astronauts from trying a premature landing. Talk about restraint! It was a nail-biting, successful precursor that paved the way for Neil and Buzz just a couple of months later.
