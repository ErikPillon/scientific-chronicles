Imagine being so fed up with broken dishes that you invent a machine to wash them for you – that was Josephine Cochrane in a nutshell! This American socialite, not a trained engineer, created a mechanical dishwasher that actually worked. On December 28, 1886, she patented her ingenious design, which used a wheel that rotated dishes through jets of water. Her invention wasn't just a clever gadget; it was a game-changer, especially for commercial kitchens and later, for homes. Cochrane's frustration led to a practical solution that saved countless hours and dishes, cementing her legacy as a pioneer in domestic technology and proving that innovation can spring from the most unexpected places. She truly cleaned up in the world of invention!
