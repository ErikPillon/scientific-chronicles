Imagine thinking the entire universe was just our Milky Way, only for some dude with a telescope to blow your mind. Edwin Hubble, peering through the 100-inch Hooker telescope at Mount Wilson, meticulously observed distant 'spiral nebulae.' In 1925, he proved they were actually other galaxies, far, far away! But he didn't stop there. By 1929, working with Milton Humason, he showed that the farther away these galaxies were, the faster they seemed to be moving away from us (redshift). This wasn't just a discovery; it was a cosmic mic drop, proving the universe is expanding and kickstarting modern cosmology. Suddenly, our universe was unimaginably vaster and far more dynamic than anyone had dared to dream.
