Long before panda cams and international diplomacy by bear, there was Su-Lin. On December 18, 1936, this fluffy, wide-eyed giant panda cub arrived in San Francisco, brought from China by adventurer Ruth Harkness. Su-Lin wasn't just an animal; she was a celebrity, capturing the hearts of Americans and becoming the first live panda to reside in the U.S. Her arrival wasn't just a cute story; it marked a turning point in Western awareness of these enigmatic creatures, inspiring both awe and, eventually, significant conservation efforts. From a furry ambassador to a symbol of species survival, Su-Lin started it all, proving that sometimes, the biggest impact can come in the most adorable package.
