Back in 1801, astronomer Giuseppe Piazzi was busy charting stars when he stumbled upon something unexpected between Mars and Jupiter – a moving object! He initially thought it was a comet, but it turned out to be the very first 'asteroid' (or minor planet), which he named Ceres. This discovery completely rattled the astronomical community, as they had expected to find a full-sized planet there. Ceres, now classified as a dwarf planet and the largest object in the asteroid belt, kicked off a whole new era of understanding the small, rocky leftovers of solar system formation. It proved the cosmos had more surprises in store than previously imagined!
