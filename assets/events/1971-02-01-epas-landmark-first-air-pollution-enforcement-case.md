Freshly formed and eager to tackle America's mounting environmental woes, the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) didn't waste much time. While an exact single 'first injunction' is tough to pinpoint due to ongoing legal actions, one of its earliest and most significant air pollution enforcement actions came in February 1971. The EPA filed a lawsuit against Armco Steel Corp. under the newly enacted Clean Air Act, seeking to compel the company to reduce its emissions. This wasn't just a friendly chat; it was a clear signal that the EPA meant business, setting the precedent for aggressive federal action to protect the air we breathe. Pollution, meet your match!
