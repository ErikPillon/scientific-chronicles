Released on June 5, 1977, the Apple II wasn't just another computer; it was a game-changer that helped kickstart the personal computer revolution. Designed primarily by the legendary Steve Wozniak, with Steve Jobs handling the marketing and overall vision, this machine was famous for its vivid color graphics (a huge deal at the time!), its expandability with eight internal expansion slots, and its approachable design. Unlike its more hobbyist predecessors, the Apple II was ready to run right out of the box, making it accessible to a much broader audience, from schools to small businesses and homes. It wasn't cheap, but it offered unprecedented power and user-friendliness, solidifying Apple's place as a major player and showing the world that computers weren't just for big corporations anymore. It practically put a computer on every desk... well, eventually.
