Before cell phones, Granville T. Woods, the self-taught wizard of invention, literally kept trains from crashing into each other with his groundbreaking Synchronous Multiplex Railway Telegraph. Patented in 1887, this ingenious device allowed train dispatchers to communicate with moving trains and, crucially, for trains to talk to each other. Imagine a world where trains operate without real-time communication – chaos! Woods's invention was a game-changer, making rail travel safer and more efficient. He basically laid the groundwork for modern train communication, proving that brilliance isn't confined to formal education.
