Before Harvey Hubbell, getting electricity to an appliance often meant a permanent, often dangerous, wire connection. But in 1904, Hubbell changed everything with his brilliant patents for a detachable electric plug and a matching wall receptacle (like US Patent 774250A). Suddenly, you could plug in your toaster, then unplug it and use your vacuum cleaner in the same spot! This seemingly simple invention was a monumental step for domestic convenience and electrical safety, allowing people to easily move and use their ever-growing collection of household appliances. Hubbell's ingenuity literally gave us the power to move around with our gadgets.
