Before the 1920s, car rides were often bone-jarring affairs, thanks to high-pressure, narrow tires. Enter the "balloon tyre"—a brilliant bit of engineering that was wider, had thinner walls, and ran at much lower pressure. Goodyear's "Super-twist" cords in 1923 helped usher in this new era. This wasn't just about comfort (though passengers surely rejoiced!); these plump puppies also offered better grip and handling, especially on the less-than-perfect roads of the era. It was a revolutionary upgrade that transformed the driving experience, making road trips feel less like a wrestling match and more like a leisurely cruise. Suddenly, your car was floating on air, literally and figuratively!
