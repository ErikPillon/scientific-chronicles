If you're looking for Astatine, you better have a good magnifying glass and a particle accelerator! This super-rare, highly radioactive element was first synthesized in 1940 by Dale R. Corson, Kenneth R. MacKenzie, and Emilio Segrè at the University of California, Berkeley. They bombarded bismuth with alpha particles to create this elusive element. Astatine (from Greek 'astatos', meaning unstable) is so unstable and decays so rapidly that only tiny amounts exist on Earth at any given time, making it the rarest naturally occurring element. Its discovery filled a gap in the periodic table and expanded our understanding of heavy radioactive elements.
