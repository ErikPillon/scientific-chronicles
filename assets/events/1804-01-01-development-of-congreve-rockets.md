Before the age of guided missiles, there were Congreve rockets – and they were quite a spectacle! Developed by British artillery officer William Congreve the Younger in the early 19th century (around 1804), these solid-propellant rockets weren't exactly precision weapons, but they were incredibly effective at causing chaos and setting things ablaze. Inspired by Indian Mysorean rockets, Congreve refined the design, making them a significant (and noisy!) addition to the British arsenal during the Napoleonic Wars and the War of 1812. They might not have been subtle, but they certainly made a statement, 'the rockets' red glare' and all!
