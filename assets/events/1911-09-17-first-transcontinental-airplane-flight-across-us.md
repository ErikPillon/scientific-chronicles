In an era when planes were flimsy contraptions held together by wood and fabric, Calbraith Perry Rodgers decided to fly one *across the entire United States*. Starting on September 17, 1911, and finishing on November 5, 1911, Rodgers navigated his "Vin Fiz Flyer" (named after a grape soda sponsor!) from Sheepshead Bay, New York, to Long Beach, California. This wasn't a non-stop journey; it involved 70 landings, numerous crashes, and frequent repairs. He literally rebuilt the plane multiple times! His incredible, often perilous, 49-day adventure proved the potential of aviation, captivating the nation and earning him a place in the annals of airborne daring.
