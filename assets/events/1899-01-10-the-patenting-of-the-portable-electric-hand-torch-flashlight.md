Stumbling in the dark became a lot less common after 1899, thanks to the official patent for the 'Electric Hand Torch' – what we now fondly call the flashlight! While earlier versions flickered about, the portable, battery-powered device as we recognize it was patented by inventor David Misell, who worked with the American Electrical Novelty and Manufacturing Company (later Eveready). This wasn't just a fancy candle; it was a revolution in portable illumination. Imagine a world where you didn't need a lantern, a match, or a servant with a torch to see where you were going at night! This ingenious blend of a battery, a small bulb, and a reflector quickly became indispensable for everyone, literally putting power and light into people's hands. It lit up countless dark corners and remains a staple of modern life.
