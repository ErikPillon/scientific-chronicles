Feeling a bit rough after a big meal or a late night? In 1931, Miles Laboratories unleashed a bubbling savior upon the world: Alka-Seltzer! The idea was simple but genius: combine aspirin (for pain), sodium bicarbonate (an antacid), and citric acid. Drop it in water, and *fizz* – you had a rapidly dissolving, effervescent concoction designed to make you feel human again. It was a multi-symptom hero before multi-symptom heroes were cool, quickly becoming a staple in medicine cabinets across America. Its catchy jingles and memorable commercials ('Plop, plop, fizz, fizz, oh what a relief it is!') cemented its place in popular culture. Who knew a little bit of fizz could bring so much relief (and brand recognition)?
