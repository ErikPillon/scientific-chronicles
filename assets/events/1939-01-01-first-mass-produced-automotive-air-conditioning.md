Before 1939, road trips often involved sweating it out with the windows down, hoping for a breeze. Enter Chrysler, who decided enough was enough and offered the very first factory-installed car air conditioning system, dubbed "Airtemp," as an option on their 1939 models! Sure, it was expensive (costing about a quarter of the car's price!), took up half the trunk, and you had to stop the engine to turn it off, but it was revolutionary. Suddenly, long drives in summer became a *lot* more pleasant. This innovation, though initially a luxury, paved the way for the ubiquitous air conditioning systems we take for granted today, making hot climates manageable and forever changing the experience of driving. It was a cool invention that truly helped cars become more comfortable living spaces.
