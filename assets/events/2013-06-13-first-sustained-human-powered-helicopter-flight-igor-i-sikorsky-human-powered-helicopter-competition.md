If you thought human-powered flight was hard, try doing it vertically! For over 30 years, the American Helicopter Society's Igor I. Sikorsky Human Powered Helicopter Competition lay unclaimed, a testament to its immense difficulty. The challenge: a helicopter powered solely by a human, remaining airborne for 60 seconds and reaching a height of 3 meters within a 10x10 meter area. Many tried, many failed. Then, on June 13, 2013, AeroVelo, a team of Canadian engineers led by Todd Reichert and Cameron Robertson, shattered the impossible with their colossal quad-rotor contraption, the 'Atlas.' Pedaled by Reichert himself, this engineering marvel briefly hovered, fulfilling the prize requirements and making history. It was a dizzying, delicate dance of power, weight, and aerodynamic perfection, proving once again that human ingenuity knows no bounds.
