Ever wished your bed was pre-warmed on a frosty evening? Before the electric blanket, people resorted to hot water bottles, warmed bricks, or just shivering. The real game-changer came in 1930 when American inventor Sidney I. Russell patented what was deemed the first 'therapeutically useful' electric blanket. Imagine the relief! Instead of just warming up *before* bed, you could maintain a perfect temperature all night long. While early models might have raised an eyebrow or two regarding safety, Russell's ingenious integration of heating elements into fabric quickly evolved into a beloved household staple. This invention didn't just chase away the cold; it truly zapped away winter blues, making bedtime a much cozier affair for millions.
