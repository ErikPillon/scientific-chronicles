Before social media influencers tried to sell you artisanal gelato, ice cream had to make its debut the old-fashioned way: a newspaper ad! On May 12, 1777, the New York Gazette featured what's widely considered the first American advertisement for ice cream. Confectioner Philip Lenzi announced he had 'ice cream' available almost daily, hinting at its novelty and luxury status. Imagine a time when ice cream was so rare, it needed an introduction! This wasn't just about a tasty treat; it was a subtle sign of colonial prosperity and changing culinary tastes, moving beyond basic sustenance to indulgent desserts. It paved the way for ice cream to become the beloved American staple it is today – thanks to a well-placed classified ad!
