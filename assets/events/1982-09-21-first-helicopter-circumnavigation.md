Who needs wings when you've got rotors? In a feat of incredible endurance and aviation skill, Ross Perot Jr. and Jay W. Coburn completed the first helicopter circumnavigation of the globe. Taking off from Dallas, Texas, on September 1, 1982, and landing back there on September 21, they completed the journey in a speedy 29 days, 3 hours, and 8 minutes. Their trusty steed was a Bell 206L-1 LongRanger II named 'Spirit of Texas.' This wasn't just a joyride; it pushed the boundaries of helicopter reliability and long-distance flight, proving these versatile machines could conquer the world, one rotating blade at a time. It was a true 'Top Gun' moment for the rotary-wing world!
