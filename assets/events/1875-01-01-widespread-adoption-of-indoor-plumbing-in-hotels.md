Before the late 19th century, even fancy hotels often meant shared facilities down the hall. Not exactly glamorous, right? Then came the revolution: indoor bathrooms, right in your room! While earlier establishments like Boston's Tremont House (1829) had some private water closets, it was grand hotels like the rebuilt Palmer House in Chicago (1875) that truly embraced and popularized the idea of private, en-suite indoor bathrooms for many of their guests. This wasn't just about comfort; it was a massive leap for sanitation and changed hospitality forever, leading to the swanky private amenities we expect today. No more dashing down corridors in your nightgown – just the sweet, sweet sound of your own flushing toilet!
