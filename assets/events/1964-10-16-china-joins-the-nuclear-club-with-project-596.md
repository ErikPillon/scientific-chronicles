On October 16, 1964, a massive explosion rocked the Lop Nur test site in Xinjiang, China, sending a mushroom cloud sky-high and altering the global geopolitical landscape forever. This was 'Project 596,' China's first successful test of an atomic bomb, making it the fifth nation to possess nuclear weapons. Led by brilliant scientists like Qian Sanqiang, this monumental achievement was a testament to China's rapid scientific and technological advancement, proving its determination to secure its place on the world stage without relying on foreign powers. Suddenly, the world got a lot more interesting (and a little scarier).
