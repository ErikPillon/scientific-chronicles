For centuries, getting to Prince Edward Island meant a ferry ride or, if you were feeling adventurous, walking across the ice. That all changed on May 31, 1997, with the grand opening of the Confederation Bridge! Spanning 12.9 kilometers (8 miles) across the Northumberland Strait, this colossal engineering feat connected the island to mainland New Brunswick, replacing the fickle ferries. It's not just long; it's a marvel of marine engineering, designed to withstand harsh winter ice conditions. A true 'Fixed Link,' it dramatically reshaped the island's economy and lifestyle, proving that sometimes, you *can* build a bridge to paradise.
