On December 5, 1996, General Motors made history by delivering the first leases of its EV1, marking the debut of the modern era's first mass-produced electric car from a major automaker. This sleek, two-seater coupé, with its aerodynamic design and quiet operation, was a bold experiment in sustainable transportation. Available only by lease in select markets, the EV1 was a technological marvel, showcasing the viability of electric propulsion long before Tesla was even a twinkle in Elon Musk's eye. While its story eventually ended with a controversial recall, the EV1 undeniably paved the way and proved that electric vehicles weren't just a futuristic fantasy, but a practical reality. It truly charged up the conversation about green cars!
