In a remarkable feat of archaeological detective work and conservation, the world got its first proper glimpse of Pharaoh Khufu's (Cheops') second solar boat on June 23, 2011. This isn't just any old boat; it's a 4,500-year-old vessel, intended to carry the pharaoh's soul through the afterlife. Discovered in 1987, buried alongside the Great Pyramid in Giza, this second boat was found disassembled into over 1,200 pieces within a sealed pit. Unlike its fully restored sister ship, this one required painstaking excavation by a joint Japanese-Egyptian team, followed by years of meticulous conservation and reassembly. The 'viewing' wasn't just a casual glance; it was a testament to the incredible effort involved in piecing together this ancient jigsaw puzzle, preserving a crucial piece of Egyptian funerary beliefs and boat-building prowess for future generations to marvel at. What a journey for a boat built for the ultimate journey!
