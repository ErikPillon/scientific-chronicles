Forget the Masters, this was literally out of this world! On February 6, 1971, Apollo 14 Commander Alan Shepard, with a sly grin and a modified 6-iron, became the first (and only!) person to play golf on the Moon. Smuggling the clubhead and two balls in his spacesuit pocket, he attached the head to a geological sampling tool and took a few swings. While he admittedly 'shanked' the first one, the second ball 'went for miles and miles and miles,' thanks to the Moon's low gravity. It was a moment of pure human whimsy amidst serious scientific work, reminding everyone back home that even hundreds of thousands of miles away, a little fun is necessary. Talk about an exclusive golf course!
