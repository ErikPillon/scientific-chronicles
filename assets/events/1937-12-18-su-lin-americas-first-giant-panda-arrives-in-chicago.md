Get ready for some serious cute! On December 18, 1937, America's heart collectively melted when Su-Lin, a fluffy, adorable giant panda cub, arrived at the Brookfield Zoo in Chicago. This wasn't just any zoo exhibit; Su-Lin was the first giant panda ever brought to the Western Hemisphere, thanks to the intrepid explorer Ruth Harkness, who quite literally brought the cub out of the Chinese mountains in a wicker basket. Her arrival ignited "pandamania" across the nation, catapulting the mysterious, elusive panda into instant celebrity status. Su-Lin's short but impactful life helped introduce the world to these magnificent creatures and kickstart global fascination, and eventually, conservation efforts, for a species that was, until then, largely mythical to the Western world.
