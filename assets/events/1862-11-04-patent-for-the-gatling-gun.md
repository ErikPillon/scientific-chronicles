On November 4, 1862, during the tumultuous American Civil War, physician Richard Jordan Gatling secured a patent for an invention that would forever change warfare: the Gatling gun. Forget single-shot rifles; Gatling's creation was one of the earliest successful rapid-fire weapons, using multiple rotating barrels cranked by hand to unleash a devastating hail of bullets. Ironically, Gatling himself claimed he invented it to reduce the number of soldiers needed, hoping to make war less likely by making it more efficient and brutal. While its immediate impact on the Civil War was limited, the Gatling gun paved the way for modern machine guns, fundamentally altering military tactics and ushering in an era of unprecedented firepower. A truly revolutionary, if terrifying, piece of engineering.
