Ever needed to stash your luggage for a few hours without lugging it around? You can thank the ingenuity behind the coin-operated locker! While exact origin dates are fuzzy, these convenient self-service storage units really took off in the early 20th century. By 1909, they were being introduced in Japan by a company called Kōkaidō, revolutionizing how people managed their belongings while on the go. These clever contraptions provided a simple, secure solution for temporary storage at train stations, bus terminals, and public venues. Just pop in a coin, lock your stuff, and enjoy your hands-free adventure. A small invention, but a big convenience!
