Before 1964, if you wanted to call someone in Hawaii from the mainland, you were relying on temperamental radio waves – which could be a bit like shouting into the wind and hoping for the best. Then came the COMPAC cable! This massive undersea project, completed in '64, finally gave Hawaii a direct, reliable, and crystal-clear phone link to the continental US. It was a huge step for telecommunications, bringing the islands closer to the rest of the world and making those long-distance calls much more dependable. No more "Can you hear me now?" issues with Uncle Kalani – just reliable chatter across the Pacific!
