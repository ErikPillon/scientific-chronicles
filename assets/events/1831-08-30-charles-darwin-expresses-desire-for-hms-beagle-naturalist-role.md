Before the grand adventure, there was the 'should I or shouldn't I?' moment for young Charles Darwin. When his mentor, Professor Henslow, first suggested the unpaid, potentially gruelling role of naturalist on the HMS Beagle, Darwin was a bit on the fence. His family certainly wasn't thrilled, especially his dad who thought it was a waste of time. But Darwin's burning passion for natural history and collecting trumped the practicalities. He quickly decided he absolutely *had* to go, fighting for the position and ultimately convincing his father to let him pursue this wild, wonderful opportunity. Good thing he did, because that decision changed science forever! Imagine if he'd just stayed home...
