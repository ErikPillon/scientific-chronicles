For decades, motorcycles symbolized freedom, rebellion, and a distinct American spirit. But it wasn't until August 13, 1993, that these two-wheeled titans finally got their philatelic due! The U.S. Postal Service issued a stamp commemorating the 90th anniversary of Harley-Davidson, featuring a stylized image of a classic 'hog.' This wasn't just a pretty picture; it was a nod to an enduring cultural icon and a testament to American manufacturing. Collectors and enthusiasts alike rejoiced, finally able to lick and stick a piece of two-wheeled history onto their envelopes. Who knew postage could be so cool?
