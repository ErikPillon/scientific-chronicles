In 1742, mathematician Christian Goldbach casually dropped a bombshell in a letter to his pal Leonhard Euler: 'Every even integer greater than 2 is the sum of two prime numbers.' Simple, right? Wrong! This seemingly innocent statement, now known as Goldbach's Conjecture, has been an absolute nightmare for mathematicians ever since. Despite millions (and billions!) of numbers being checked, no one has ever found a counterexample, but no one has definitively proven it either. It's one of those tantalizing mysteries that sits at the heart of number theory, mocking us with its elegance and defying our best efforts. Talk about a tough nut to crack!
