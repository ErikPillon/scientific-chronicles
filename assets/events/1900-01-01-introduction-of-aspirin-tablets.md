You've got your revolutionary drug, Aspirin, but how do you get it into people's hands (and mouths) easily? That's where the Aspirin tablet comes in! While Felix Hoffmann synthesized acetylsalicylic acid in 1897, Bayer initially sold it as a powder. But it was the ingenious idea to press it into a stable, easy-to-dose tablet form around 1900 that truly democratized pain relief. This simple innovation transformed Aspirin from a lab curiosity into a global household staple, making it convenient to pop a pill for headaches, fevers, and aches, and setting a precedent for how most medications would be delivered for the next century.
