In a world before email, texts, and social media, how did you send a quick, special message? Enter the Greetings Telegram! Introduced by services like the UK's General Post Office in 1935, this wasn't just any old urgent message; it was a telegram specifically designed for celebrations, condolences, and well wishes. Often delivered in colorful, decorative envelopes, these short, punchy messages added a touch of formality and excitement to personal communication. They allowed people to instantly share joy or sorrow across distances, proving that even in the age of telegraphs, there was a demand for quick, heartfelt communication. It was the original "thinking of you" message, delivered with a satisfying *ping* (or rather, a knock on the door).
