It's 1903, and the newly minted Ford Motor Company needs to prove its worth. Enter Dr. Ernst Pfenning, a Chicago dentist with a keen eye for new tech. He became the proud owner of the very first Model A Ford sold to the public. This two-seater runabout, costing $850 (around $28,000 today!), wasn't just a fancy new toy; it was a validation of Henry Ford's burgeoning enterprise. This wasn't the Quadricycle, nor the Model T, but the solid first step in Ford's journey to mass-produce cars for the common person, marking the moment his automotive dream officially became a commercial reality.
