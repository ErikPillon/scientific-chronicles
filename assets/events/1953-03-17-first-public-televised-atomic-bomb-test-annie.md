You know how reality TV exploded? Well, in 1953, the ultimate reality show premiered: a live atomic bomb detonation. On March 17, during the 'Annie' test as part of Operation Upshot-Knothole at the Nevada Test Site, cameras rolled, and for the first time, the terrifying spectacle of a nuclear blast was broadcast live on television (though often delayed for analysis). People across America gathered around their black-and-white sets to watch a mushroom cloud bloom. It was a chilling, yet mesmerizing, public relations exercise, designed to show the public the might of American nuclear power and, perhaps, desensitize them to the horror. Suddenly, the abstract concept of the bomb became a terrifying visual in every home, marking a new era of public engagement (and anxiety) with the atomic age.
