Before elevators and modern safety regulations, escaping a burning building was often a leap of faith (literally!). While rudimentary ropes and ladders existed for ages, the official 'fire escape ladder' as a patented invention really kicked things up a notch. Abraham W. Van Der Burgh secured a US patent for his design in 1840, making the idea of a standardized, deployable ladder for escaping infernos a more structured reality. It was a crucial step in urban safety, giving folks a fighting chance to get out when things went fiery, eventually leading to the more robust, fixed fire escapes we see today. A real lifesaver, and a true step forward in fire safety tech!
