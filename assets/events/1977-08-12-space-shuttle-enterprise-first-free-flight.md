Imagine dropping a really, really expensive glider from the back of an airplane, and that glider is the future of space travel! That's essentially what happened on August 12, 1977, when the Space Shuttle Enterprise, with astronauts Fred Haise and Gordon Fullerton at the controls, performed its first-ever free flight. Released from its Boeing 747 carrier, the Enterprise glided unpowered through the California sky to a perfect landing at Edwards Air Force Base. This wasn't just a pretty stunt; it was a make-or-break moment proving the shuttle's aerodynamic design and flight control systems worked. The success of this and subsequent glide tests paved the way for the full Space Shuttle program, showing that humans could indeed fly this incredible machine like an airplane, albeit one that fell from orbit!
