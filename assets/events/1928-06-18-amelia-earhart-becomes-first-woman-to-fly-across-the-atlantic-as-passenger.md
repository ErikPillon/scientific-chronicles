On June 18, 1928, the Fokker F.VIIb/3m named "Friendship," piloted by Wilmer Stultz with Louis Gordon as co-pilot and Amelia Earhart as a rather famous passenger, successfully landed in Burry Port, Wales. After a harrowing 20-hour, 40-minute flight from Newfoundland through dense fog and storms, Earhart stepped out, making her the first woman to cross the Atlantic by air. While she wasn't at the controls, this flight catapulted her into international fame, igniting her career as an aviation icon and inspiring countless women. It was a bumpy ride, but it cemented her place in the history books and set the stage for even bigger solo adventures!
