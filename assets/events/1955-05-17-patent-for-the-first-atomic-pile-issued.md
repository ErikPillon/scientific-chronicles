After the top-secret work of the Manhattan Project, the patent for the neutronic reactor – the very device that achieved the world's first sustained nuclear chain reaction – was filed by brilliant minds Enrico Fermi and Leo Szilard on December 19, 1944. It then took over a decade for Uncle Sam to officially grant US Patent 2,708,656 on May 17, 1955, finally recognizing the genius behind harnessing the atom. This wasn't just a piece of paper; it was the legal foundation for everything from nuclear power to atomic weapons, truly a game-changer that reshaped the 20th century and demonstrated humanity's immense power.
