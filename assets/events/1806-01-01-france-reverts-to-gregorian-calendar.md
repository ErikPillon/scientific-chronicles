You know how sometimes you try a new app, only to realize the old one was better? France did that, but with calendars, and for a whopping 12 years! During the French Revolution, in a bid to completely break with old traditions (and the Church), they introduced the French Republican Calendar in 1793. This wild new system had 10-day weeks, 30-day months, and started from the revolution's first day. It was logical, decimal, and utterly confusing for everyone trying to coordinate with the rest of the world. By 1805, even Napoleon Bonaparte, who loved a good revolution, conceded it was impractical. So, with a stroke of genius (or common sense), he decreed that as of January 1, 1806, France would ditch the revolutionary dates and return to the good old Gregorian calendar. Peace and chronological order were finally restored, making international diplomacy (and birthday parties) much simpler again.
