What happens when two brilliant minds, fresh from pioneering the integrated circuit at Fairchild Semiconductor, decide they can do it better? They start Intel! In 1968, Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore (yes, *that* Moore of Moore's Law fame) teamed up with Andrew Grove to create what would become a behemoth in the semiconductor industry. Their initial vision wasn't just about making memory chips; it was about pushing the boundaries of what silicon could do. They were betting big on the idea that circuits could get exponentially smaller and more powerful, revolutionizing computing forever. It wasn't just another startup; it was the foundation of the 'Intel Inside' era, powering the personal computer revolution and laying the groundwork for the digital world we live in. Talk about a power move!
