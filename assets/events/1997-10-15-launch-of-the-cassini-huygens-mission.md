On October 15, 1997, a colossal Titan IVB/Centaur rocket thundered skyward from Cape Canaveral, carrying one of humanity's most ambitious voyages: the Cassini-Huygens mission. This wasn't just another space probe; it was a collaborative masterpiece by NASA, ESA, and ASI, designed to unlock the secrets of Saturn and its intriguing moons. The launch marked the beginning of a nearly seven-year journey across the solar system, a painstaking ballet of engineering and celestial mechanics. It promised a prolonged, in-depth study of a gas giant and its rings, setting the stage for stunning discoveries and breathtaking images that would redefine our understanding of the outer solar system and capture imaginations across the globe. What a send-off!
