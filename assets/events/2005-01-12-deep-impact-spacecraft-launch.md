On January 12, 2005, NASA decided to play a cosmic game of billiards, launching its Deep Impact spacecraft with a rather audacious goal: to intentionally crash into a comet! No, this wasn't an act of celestial road rage; it was a brilliantly calculated mission to peer inside a comet named Tempel 1. The main spacecraft deployed an impactor that sacrificed itself in a spectacular collision, kicking up a plume of primordial dust and ice. Scientists on Earth and the mothership then eagerly studied the debris, hoping to unlock secrets about the early solar system. Talk about a dramatic way to get a sample!
