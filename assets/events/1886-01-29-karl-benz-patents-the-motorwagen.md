Before Teslas and traffic jams, there was Karl Benz's Motorwagen, a three-wheeled contraption that looked more like a motorized tricycle than a sleek sedan. But don't let its humble appearance fool you! On January 29, 1886, Benz received German Patent DRP 37435 for his 'vehicle powered by a gas engine.' This wasn't just an invention; it was *the* invention that officially birthed the modern automobile. Imagine the audacity: building a machine that moved under its own power, without horses! While it took a bit of convincing (thanks, Bertha Benz, for that legendary road trip!), this patent laid the legal and technological foundation for an industry that would reshape cities, economies, and our very concept of freedom. The open road truly began here.
