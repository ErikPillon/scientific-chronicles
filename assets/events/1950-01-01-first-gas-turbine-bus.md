Imagine a bus that sounds like a jet plane! That's exactly what happened in 1950 when the British Rover Company unleashed the world's first gas turbine-powered bus. While gas turbines were already rocketing planes through the sky, putting one into a road vehicle was a bold, noisy, and somewhat impractical move. The idea was to harness the compact power of a jet engine for public transport, promising a smooth, vibration-free ride. However, its thirst for fuel and complex engineering meant it never quite caught on for mass transit, remaining a fascinating, roaring footnote in the history of transportation innovation. It was a 'too much, too soon' moment, but undeniably cool.
