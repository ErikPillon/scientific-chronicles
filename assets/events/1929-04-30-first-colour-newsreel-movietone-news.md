In an era where news was still largely black and white, Fox Movietone News made a splash on April 30, 1929, with the release of the first commercially exhibited colour newsreel. Using the then-new Technicolor process, the reel featured a ceremonial parade of warships, showcasing the vibrant hues of naval flags and uniforms. While early colour processes were a bit clunky and expensive, this innovation marked a significant step towards the vivid, immersive news experiences we take for granted today. It promised a future where historical moments weren't just recorded, but truly felt, thanks to the magic of colour.
