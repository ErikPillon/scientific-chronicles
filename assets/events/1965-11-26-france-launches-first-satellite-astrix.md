While the US and Soviet Union were duking it out for space supremacy, France decided it wanted a piece of the cosmic pie too! On November 26, 1965, from their Hammaguir launch site in Algeria, France successfully launched its first satellite, Astérix (officially named A-1). Named after the famous Gaulish warrior, this satellite made France the third country, after the USSR and USA, to launch its own satellite using its own rocket (a Diamant A). It wasn't about spying or grand scientific breakthroughs; it was primarily a technological demonstration and a huge statement of national capability and independence. Astérix might have only transmitted for a couple of days, but it cemented France's place in the exclusive club of space-faring nations, proving they had the brains and rockets to reach for the stars.
