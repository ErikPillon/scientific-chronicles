Imagine sitting by your radio, ears pressed close, as the crack of the bat and the roar of the crowd come alive through the airwaves! On August 5, 1921, Pittsburgh's KDKA radio made history by broadcasting a live baseball game between the Pittsburgh Pirates and the Philadelphia Phillies. Announcer Harold W. Arlin, using a telephone line to describe the action from the stadium, brought the excitement of the ballpark into homes across the region. This wasn't just a game; it was a groundbreaking moment for media, proving radio's power to connect people with live events. It launched a new era of sports fandom, making radio an indispensable companion for millions and paving the way for every sportscast we enjoy today!
