Imagine a scientific reality TV show, but with more sheep and less drama (initially). In May 1881, Louis Pasteur, already a science superstar, put his anthrax vaccine to the ultimate public test at Pouilly-le-Fort, France. Skeptics challenged him to immunize half a flock of sheep and then infect both vaccinated and unvaccinated groups with a deadly anthrax culture. The result? A perfectly executed scientific mic drop. The unvaccinated sheep dropped like flies, while the vaccinated ones frolicked, proving Pasteur's revolutionary vaccine was a game-changer. This wasn't just a win for the sheep; it solidified the germ theory of disease and launched the era of modern preventative medicine. Take that, doubters!
