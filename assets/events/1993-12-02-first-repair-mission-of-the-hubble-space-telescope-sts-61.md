When the Hubble Space Telescope launched in 1990, scientists were stoked! But then, a heartbreaking flaw was discovered: a tiny defect in its primary mirror meant images were blurry. It was like buying a Ferrari and realizing it needed glasses. Enter the STS-61 Space Shuttle mission in December 1993, a daring and complex repair job performed by a team of courageous astronauts. They embarked on multiple spacewalks to install corrective optics (COSTAR) and other instruments, essentially giving Hubble its much-needed cosmic eyeglasses. This incredible feat of in-orbit engineering transformed Hubble from a scientific disappointment into the universe's most iconic and productive observatory, delivering the stunning images and data we all know and love. It was a space-age success story!
