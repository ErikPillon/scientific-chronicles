In 1969, just as everyone was getting used to their sugar-free sodas, the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) dropped a bombshell: cyclamates, a popular artificial sweetener found in everything from Tab to diet foods, were banned! This sudden move, announced by Secretary Robert H. Finch, came after studies (particularly one involving rats) suggested a link between high doses of cyclamates and bladder cancer. The ban created immediate chaos for the food industry and left consumers wondering if their diet drinks were actually a health hazard. It kicked off a massive public debate about food additives and government regulation, making us all a little more suspicious about what we were putting in our mouths.
