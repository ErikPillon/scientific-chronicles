Forget birds, the real game-changers were bicycle mechanics from Ohio! On December 17, 1903, on a windswept beach near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, Orville and Wilbur Wright pulled off one of history's most epic mic drops: the first sustained, powered, and controlled flight of a heavier-than-air machine. Orville took the controls for a whopping 12 seconds, covering 120 feet – a distance shorter than the wingspan of a modern jumbo jet, but a giant leap for mankind. This wasn't just a joyride; it was the culmination of years of meticulous design, wind tunnel tests, and sheer grit. Their humble Flyer didn't just fly; it launched the aviation industry, shrinking our world and opening up the skies for exploration, travel, and even package delivery. Take that, gravity!
