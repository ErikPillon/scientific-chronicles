Not content with conquering the Atlantic (or at least being a passenger on its first female crossing), Amelia Earhart added another feather to her cap on April 8, 1931, by setting a new altitude record in a Pitcairn PCA-2 autogyro. The autogyro, a fascinating precursor to the helicopter, was a tricky beast to fly, but Earhart, ever the pioneer, mastered it, reaching 18,415 feet. She not only became the first woman to fly this peculiar aircraft but also used it for a cross-country tour, popularizing aviation to the masses. Her embrace of new flying machines showed her relentless spirit and her crucial role in promoting aviation's future. What an aerial trailblazer!
