Before Disney, Pixar, and even Felix the Cat, there was "Fantasmagorie." Created by French artist Émile Cohl and first screened on August 17, 1908, this short, whimsical film is widely considered the world's first animated cartoon. Cohl painstakingly drew over 700 frames on blackboards and then filmed the inversions, creating a fluid, almost dreamlike sequence of transforming objects and characters. It was a revolutionary moment, demonstrating the power of sequential images to bring drawings to life. This quirky, imaginative flick laid the foundation for an entire industry, proving that with enough patience and creativity, you could make pictures move!
