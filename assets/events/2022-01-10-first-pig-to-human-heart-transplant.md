In a moment that sounded like science fiction, the world watched in early 2022 as doctors at the University of Maryland performed the first successful pig-to-human heart transplant. David Bennett Sr., suffering from terminal heart disease, received a genetically modified pig heart, offering a glimmer of hope for patients awaiting organ donations. While the outcome was bittersweet—Mr. Bennett passed away two months later—this audacious surgery marked an extraordinary leap in xenotransplantation. It showed that animal organs could function in a human body, paving the way for future advancements and sparking intense ethical and scientific discussions. This wasn't just a transplant; it was a defiant challenge to the limits of what medicine can achieve.
