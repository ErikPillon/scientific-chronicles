Remember those giant 'brick' phones? Well, they had to start somewhere! On April 3, 1973, Motorola engineer Martin Cooper was strolling down a street in New York City, feeling pretty smug. He pulled out a bulky, handheld device – his prototype cellular phone – and made a call to his rival at Bell Labs, Joel Engel. 'Joel, I'm calling you from a 'personal, handheld, portable cell phone',' Cooper reportedly quipped. This wasn't just a phone call; it was the dawn of an era, liberating communication from landlines and paving the way for the smartphones that now run our lives. The future, quite literally, was in Cooper's hand!
