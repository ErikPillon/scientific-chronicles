Imagine needing surgery before you're even born! That's precisely what happened in 1981 when a pioneering team at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), led by Dr. Michael Harrison, performed the world's first successful open fetal surgery. The patient was a fetus diagnosed with a life-threatening urinary tract blockage. In a procedure that sounds like science fiction, surgeons carefully operated on the baby while it was still inside the mother's womb, correcting the defect. This groundbreaking achievement opened up a whole new field of medicine, offering hope to families facing severe congenital anomalies and proving that sometimes, the best patient care starts incredibly early – before the grand entrance into the world!
