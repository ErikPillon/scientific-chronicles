The Amazon. Just the name conjures images of impenetrable jungle, vast rivers, and untold mysteries. While indigenous people had lived along and explored its waters for millennia, the first documented European descent of the entire Amazon River was by Francisco de Orellana, starting in late 1541 and finishing in 1542. Driven by the lure of gold and the mythical El Dorado, his expedition endured incredible hardship, but charted much of the river's course. Later centuries saw scientific expeditions, like those of Humboldt, Wallace, and Bates, who cataloged its astounding biodiversity. From the early quest for riches to the later scientific endeavors, Amazon exploration has continuously revealed a region of unparalleled natural and cultural complexity, a true scientific frontier even today, always yielding new surprises.
