Forget cowboys and six-shooters for a moment! Before he became famous for his revolvers, Samuel Colt was busy inventing maritime mayhem. In 1842, he publicly demonstrated electrically detonated naval mines (then often called "torpedoes") in New York Harbor. These weren't your average explosive barrels; Colt could detonate them remotely using electric wires, sinking old ships with dramatic flair. His pioneering work in electrical ordnance was a truly electrifying development, showcasing the potential of electricity for military applications and changing naval warfare forever—though thankfully, they weren't widely adopted until later conflicts.
