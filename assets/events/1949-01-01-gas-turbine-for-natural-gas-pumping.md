Who knew the same technology that propels jet aircraft could also keep your home warm? In the late 1940s, industrial engineers realized the immense potential of gas turbines for stationary power applications. One of their most impactful innovations was deploying these powerful, efficient engines to drive compressors for pumping natural gas through vast pipeline networks. Before this, less efficient and more maintenance-intensive reciprocating engines dominated. Gas turbines offered a compact, high-power-density solution, drastically improving the speed and cost-effectiveness of moving natural gas across continents. It was a quieter, behind-the-scenes revolution, but one that directly fueled the post-war energy boom and ensured reliable delivery to millions.
