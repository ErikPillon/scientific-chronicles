Before Netflix and multiplexes, going to the movies was a novelty! While early film screenings popped up everywhere, the establishment of the first permanent, dedicated cinema in London around 1900 (like the Royal Bioscope, which started as the Biograph Concert Hall) marked a major cultural shift. No longer just a fairground curiosity, cinema became its own destination, drawing crowds eager to witness moving pictures in a purpose-built venue. This wasn't just about watching a film; it was about experiencing a new social ritual, paving the way for the grand picture palaces and the cinematic empires that would captivate generations.
