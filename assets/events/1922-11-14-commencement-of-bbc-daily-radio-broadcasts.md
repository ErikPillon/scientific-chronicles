While the BBC company was formed in October 1922, the real magic started crackling through the airwaves on November 14th of that year. That's when the British Broadcasting Company officially launched its daily radio broadcasts from its London station, 2LO. Imagine the scene: before this, news and entertainment were largely confined to newspapers and live performances. Suddenly, voices and music were filling living rooms, bringing the world directly to people's ears! The very first program included a news bulletin, weather forecast, and a performance by operatic tenor Peter Dawson. Arthur Burrows, the BBC's first Director of Programmes, was the voice for much of the early news. This wasn't just turning on a microphone; it was a societal shift, creating a shared national experience and paving the way for broadcasting to become the powerful medium we know today. It was the moment the UK truly tuned in.
