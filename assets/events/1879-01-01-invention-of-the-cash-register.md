Back in the late 19th century, saloon owners like James Ritty faced a sticky problem: employees skimming from the till. Ritty, had enough! Inspired by a ship's propeller counter, he and his brother John patented the first practical mechanical cash register in 1879. This wasn't just a glorified adding machine; it printed a receipt and visibly displayed the amount, making it much harder for employees to pocket cash unnoticed. When John H. Patterson later bought the patent and founded NCR (National Cash Register Company), he transformed it into an indispensable tool for business, revolutionizing retail by promoting honesty, tracking sales, and laying the groundwork for every modern point-of-sale system we use today. Cheers to that!
