On June 17, 1928, from Trepassey Harbor, Newfoundland, Amelia Earhart embarked on a journey that would change her life and aviation history forever. As a passenger aboard the Fokker F.VIIb/3m "Friendship," alongside pilot Wilmer Stultz and mechanic Louis Gordon, she set off into the vast, unpredictable expanse of the Atlantic Ocean. The goal? To be the first woman to fly across it. Despite Earhart not piloting the plane, the sheer courage and ambition of the undertaking captivated the world, turning her into an instant celebrity. This departure wasn't just a takeoff; it was the launch of a legend, a prelude to a daring life in the skies.
