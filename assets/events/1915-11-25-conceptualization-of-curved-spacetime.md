Forget everything you thought you knew about gravity being a pull; Albert Einstein, in his 1915 General Theory of Relativity, dropped a bombshell: gravity isn't a force, it's the effect of mass and energy warping the very fabric of spacetime! Imagine a bowling ball on a stretched rubber sheet; smaller marbles rolling nearby will curve towards it not because the bowling ball is 'pulling' them, but because the sheet itself is distorted. This mind-bending concept of 'curved space' (or more precisely, curved spacetime) revolutionized our understanding of the universe, from black holes to the Big Bang, proving that reality is far stranger and more beautiful than we could have imagined.
