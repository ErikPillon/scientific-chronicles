Before airmail was common, the mere idea of an airplane on a stamp was a novelty! While there were earlier stamps depicting dirigibles, the first known stamp to feature an actual airplane was issued by France in 1909. It commemorated Louis Blériot's record-setting flight across the English Channel. This wasn't strictly an *airmail* stamp, but rather a special issue celebrating an aerial feat. Nevertheless, seeing a flying machine gracing a postage stamp was a clear sign that aviation, once a futuristic dream, was becoming a concrete reality, ready to deliver more than just letters.
