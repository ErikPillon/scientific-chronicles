Before 1911, starting a car was a perilous chore. Drivers had to manually crank a lever, a task that was not only arduous but incredibly dangerous – a 'kickback' could easily break an arm. Enter Charles Kettering, a brilliant inventor who, at the request of Cadillac's Henry Leland (whose friend had tragically died from a crank accident), developed the first practical electric self-starter. First appearing on the 1912 Cadillac, this ingenious device meant drivers could simply press a button and *vroom*! It didn't just save arms; it revolutionized automotive accessibility, making driving easier and safer for everyone, especially women, who were less inclined to grapple with a crank. Talk about a literally shocking innovation that steered automotive history in a whole new direction!
