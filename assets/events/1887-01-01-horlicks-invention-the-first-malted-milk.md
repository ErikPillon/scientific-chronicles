Back in 1887, Wisconsin was already known for its dairy, but William Horlick was about to make it famous for something even more delicious: malted milk! This British-born pharmacist, who had already found success with his 'diastoid' infant food, set out to create an easily digestible and nutritious dried food product for infants and invalids. Combining dried milk, malted barley, and wheat flour, he patented his new creation. It wasn't just healthy; it was tasty! Soon, it became a staple in drugstores, soda fountains, and even arctic expeditions. From a simple health supplement to a beloved milkshake ingredient, malted milk’s origin story is a testament to Horlick's innovative spirit – and our collective love for creamy, malty goodness!
