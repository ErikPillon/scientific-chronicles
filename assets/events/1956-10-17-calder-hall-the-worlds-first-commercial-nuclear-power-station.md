On October 17, 1956, Queen Elizabeth II flipped a switch at Calder Hall in Cumbria, changing the world forever. This wasn't just Britain's first nuclear power station; it was the *world's* first to generate electricity on a commercial scale! Born from post-war energy needs and atomic research, Calder Hall symbolized a brave new era of seemingly limitless clean energy. While originally dual-purpose (producing plutonium for weapons and electricity), its inauguration marked a monumental shift in energy policy and engineering, promising to light up homes and factories without burning a single lump of coal. Talk about a glow-up for electricity generation!
