Once HIV was identified, the next critical step was understanding *how* it wreaked such havoc. Early research quickly revealed that the virus wasn't just floating around; it had a particular fondness for cells within the lymphatic system, especially CD4+ T-cells, the key orchestrators of the immune response. Scientists discovered that HIV directly infected and destroyed these crucial cells, leading to the profound immunodeficiency characteristic of AIDS. This insight was massive! It explained why AIDS patients became vulnerable to opportunistic infections and cancers, providing a clear pathway for researchers to develop strategies for treatment and prevention. Knowing where the enemy hides is half the battle, after all, and this discovery was a crucial step in the fight against AIDS.
