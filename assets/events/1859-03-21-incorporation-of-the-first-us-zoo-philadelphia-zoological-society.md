Before Netflix documentaries, how did Americans get their dose of exotic animals? Zoos, of course! And it all began with the incorporation of the Philadelphia Zoological Society on March 21, 1859. While the actual Philadelphia Zoo didn't open its gates until 1874 (the Civil War caused a slight delay, you know), this formal establishment was a monumental step. Spearheaded by figures like Dr. William Camac, the society aimed to create a place for scientific study, education, and public recreation, bringing the wonders of the animal kingdom closer to urban dwellers. This pioneering move set a precedent for public zoos across the country, kickstarting a national interest in zoology and conservation that continues to this day.
