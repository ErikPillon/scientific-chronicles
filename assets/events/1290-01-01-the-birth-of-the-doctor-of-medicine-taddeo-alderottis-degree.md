While healing has been around forever, formalized medical education, culminating in a 'Doctor of Medicine' degree, is a relatively newer concept. The University of Bologna in Italy, a hub of medieval learning, is often credited with truly formalizing this path. Around 1290, the renowned physician and scholar Taddeo Alderotti (who even appears in Dante's *Inferno*!) is widely considered one of the very first to receive a doctorate in medicine there. Before this, medical training was often an apprenticeship. The formal degree legitimized medicine as an academic discipline, standardizing knowledge and training, and elevating practitioners to a new professional status. So, next time you visit a doctor, remember that someone centuries ago laid the groundwork for their esteemed title!
