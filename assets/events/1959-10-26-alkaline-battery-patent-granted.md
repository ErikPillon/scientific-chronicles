Remember those old carbon-zinc batteries that seemed to die just when you needed them most? Well, in 1959, Lewis Urry, a chemical engineer working for Eveready Battery Company (now Energizer), literally supercharged the battery world. He developed and patented the alkaline dry-cell battery on October 26, 1959. By swapping acidic electrolytes for alkaline ones and tweaking the design, Urry created a battery that lasted significantly longer and delivered more power than its predecessors. This wasn't just a minor improvement; it was a game-changer for portable devices, from flashlights to transistor radios. His invention made our gadgets run longer and made 'low battery' alerts a little less frequent for generations. Thanks, Lewis, for giving us that extra juice!
