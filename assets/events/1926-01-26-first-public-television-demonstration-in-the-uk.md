Imagine a world without screens, then boom! On January 26, 1926, Scottish engineer John Logie Baird pulled off a stunt straight out of science fiction. In a Soho laboratory, he demonstrated the world's first true television system, transmitting moving images with tone graduations to members of the Royal Institution. It wasn't exactly HD, more like a flickering orange silhouette of his business partner's face, but it was revolutionary! This pivotal moment kicked off the television era, transforming how we consume news, entertainment, and questionable reality shows. From that tiny, grainy picture grew the global phenomenon that still dominates our living rooms today. Not bad for a bloke tinkering with old hatboxes and bicycle lamps!
