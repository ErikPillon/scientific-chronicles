Before the automobile, getting around was a slow, horse-powered affair. The invention of the practical, internal combustion engine-powered automobile, famously pioneered by Karl Benz in 1886 with his three-wheeled Benz Patent-Motorwagen, unleashed a revolution. While others experimented, Benz's design, along with his wife Bertha Benz's daring long-distance test drive, proved its viability. It wasn't just a new machine; it sparked entirely new industries, reshaped cities, and gave individuals unprecedented freedom of movement. From a curious novelty to a global necessity, the automobile's journey is one of engineering marvel and societal transformation, making the world feel a whole lot smaller, and a lot noisier, too!
