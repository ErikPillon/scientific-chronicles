Bridges are feats of engineering and beauty, so it was only a matter of time before one graced a U.S. stamp. That honor went to the magnificent Brooklyn Bridge, which appeared on a 5-cent stamp as part of the Pan-American Exposition Issue on May 1, 1901. This iconic suspension bridge, a symbol of American ingenuity and progress since its completion in 1883, was a perfect choice. Its appearance on the stamp didn't just celebrate its structural grandeur; it highlighted America's industrial might and its ability to conquer geographical challenges, making it a cultural icon in miniature. Even without crossing it, you could still appreciate its monumental significance!
