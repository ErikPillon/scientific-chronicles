For years, the 'millipede' was a bit of a misnomer; no known species actually boasted a thousand legs, much to the chagrin of pedantic entomologists. That all changed on December 16, 2021, when a paper published in *Scientific Reports* announced the discovery of *Eumillipes persephone* in Western Australia. This leggy marvel truly lives up to its name, sporting a mind-boggling 1,306 legs! Discovered deep underground, this pallid, eyeless creature smashed all previous records, finally giving the 'millipede' its rightful claim to fame. Lead author Paul E. Marek and his team revealed a new level of biodiversity, proving that even in seemingly well-explored groups, the natural world still holds astonishing surprises. Suddenly, 'centipede' just sounds a bit... underachieving.
