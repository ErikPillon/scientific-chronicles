The Hubble Space Telescope, launched in 1990, was supposed to revolutionize astronomy, but a tiny mirror flaw made its early images disappointingly blurry. Enter the crew of STS-61 on the Space Shuttle Endeavour in December 1993, who basically performed eye surgery in orbit! In a series of daring spacewalks, they installed corrective optics (COSTAR) and replaced other instruments. It was a nail-biting, triumphant mission that transformed Hubble from a cosmic flop into the universe's most iconic peeping tom, giving us those stunning, crystal-clear views we all know and love.
