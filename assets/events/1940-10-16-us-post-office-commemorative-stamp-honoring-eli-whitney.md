On October 16, 1940, the U.S. Post Office issued a 3-cent stamp depicting Eli Whitney, the famed inventor of the cotton gin and pioneer of interchangeable parts. This stamp was part of a series dedicated to celebrating significant American inventors and their contributions to the nation's progress. It served as a philatelic nod to Whitney's enduring legacy, highlighting how his mechanical genius shaped industrial development and agriculture. So, if you were mailing a letter in 1940, you might have sent a tribute to the man who truly changed the fabric of American life – quite literally, in the case of cotton!
