For centuries, watches relied on springs and gears. Then, in the ultimate symbol of mid-century modern innovation, the Hamilton Watch Company dropped the Hamilton Electric 500 on January 3, 1957. This wasn't just any watch; it was the world's first battery-powered electric watch, boasting a tiny, buzzing motor instead of a mainspring. Its futuristic, asymmetrical designs (thanks, Richard H. Arbib!) made it an instant style icon, especially popular with trendsetters like Elvis Presley. It didn't keep *perfect* time by today's quartz standards, but it was a revolutionary leap, marking the beginning of the end for purely mechanical wristwatches and setting the stage for the quartz revolution to come. It was literally electric cool.
