In 2004, English police forces began widely adopting the Taser stun gun, a device that delivers an incapacitating electrical shock. Initially rolled out to specialist firearms officers, its use quickly became a hot topic, sparking debates about officer safety versus civil liberties. While proponents championed it as a less-lethal alternative to firearms, critics raised concerns about potential misuse and potential harm, particularly concerning vulnerable individuals. This technology's integration ensured Tasers remained at the heart of public and policy discussions for years to come, profoundly impacting police tactics and public interaction.
