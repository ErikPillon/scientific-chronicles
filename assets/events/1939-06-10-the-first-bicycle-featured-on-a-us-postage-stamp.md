Who knew a bike could be such a big deal? On June 10, 1939, the United States Post Office Department released a stamp commemorating the 100th anniversary of baseball. But wait, where's the bike? The stamp, part of the 'Centennial of Baseball' series, featured a boy delivering a newspaper while riding a bicycle. It's a small detail, but this marked the very first time a bicycle graced an official U.S. postage stamp, a quiet nod to its growing role in American life, from delivering papers to leisurely rides. This understated philatelic moment quietly celebrated the two-wheeled contraption's integration into the fabric of daily American life.
