Imagine a world where your electric bill was just a guess! Elihu Thomson, a British-born American engineer, swooped in with his 1890 patent (US430889A) for an integrating wattmeter. This wasn't just another gadget; it was a game-changer. His device accurately measured electrical energy consumption over time, making fair billing possible and paving the way for the widespread adoption of electricity. Suddenly, utilities could measure usage precisely, and consumers could finally trust their electric bills (mostly!). It was a crucial step in turning electricity from a novelty into a dependable, everyday utility.
