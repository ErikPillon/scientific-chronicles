In 1877, just a year after Alexander Graham Bell patented his revolutionary device, President Rutherford B. Hayes had the very first telephone installed in the White House. Can you imagine the buzz? No more waiting for telegrams or sending messengers; instant communication was suddenly at the President's fingertips. While it didn't immediately replace all other forms of communication (they were still figuring out how to use it!), it was a clear signal that the world was shrinking, and even the highest office in the land was embracing this new, exciting, and slightly peculiar technology. It probably made ordering pizza a lot easier, eventually.
