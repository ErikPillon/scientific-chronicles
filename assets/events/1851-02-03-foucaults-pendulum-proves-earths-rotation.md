The 'result' of Foucault's pendulum wasn't just a scientific paper; it was a jaw-dropping physical demonstration that finally put an end to centuries of debate: the Earth really does rotate! Unveiled publicly in Paris in 1851 by physicist Léon Foucault, his massive pendulum – a heavy bob on a long wire – slowly but surely changed its plane of swing over hours. This wasn't magic, it was the ground beneath it (our Earth!) doing the turning. It was a beautiful, incontrovertible piece of evidence for geocentric skeptics, showing physics in action and making the invisible motion of our planet spectacularly visible. Talk about a mic drop moment for astrophysics!
