On October 20, 1962, Canada plugged into the atomic age with the start-up of the Nuclear Power Demonstration (NPD-2) reactor in Rolphton, Ontario. This wasn't just any old power plant; it was Canada's experimental foray into generating electricity from nuclear fission, using its unique CANDU reactor design. While it started small, only producing 20 MW, NPD-2 paved the way for Canada's impressive nuclear fleet. It was a big deal, proving the viability of heavy water reactors and showing the world Canada was serious about atomic energy. Talk about a bright idea!
