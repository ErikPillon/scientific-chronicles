For centuries, getting your teeth worked on meant lying back in a regular chair, or worse, just sitting wherever the "dentist" could reach. Not exactly conducive to precision work or patient comfort! But in 1832, British dentist James Snell, clearly a man who cared about both his patients and his posture, received a patent for what's often considered the first purpose-built dental chair. This wasn't just any old seat; it featured adjustable headrests, footrests, and often reclinable backs, finally giving dentists a proper working angle and patients a much less agonizing experience. It was a simple yet profound innovation that literally put dentistry on a more professional and comfortable footing, proving that sometimes, the best inventions just make life a little less of a pain in the neck (or mouth!).
