Before Wi-Fi and fiber optics, there was the coaxial cable, a true workhorse of communication, and we can thank Oliver Heaviside for its brilliant conception. Patented in 1880, Heaviside's design, with its central conductor surrounded by an insulating layer and a grounded shield, was a stroke of genius. It drastically reduced signal loss and interference, making long-distance, high-frequency signal transmission practical for the first time. Though it took a while for commercial adoption, his invention paved the way for everything from multi-channel telephone lines and television broadcasts to early computer networks. It's the unsung hero that helped wire the world!
