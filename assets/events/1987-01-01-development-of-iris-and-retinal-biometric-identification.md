Long before unlocking your phone with your face became commonplace, the concept of using your eyes for identification was taking shape. While the term 'eye print' can broadly refer to various unique eye features, the most significant breakthrough came with the development of iris recognition technology. In 1987, ophthalmologists Leonard Flom and Aran Safir received a patent for an iris recognition algorithm, later refined and popularized by John Daugman. The idea is brilliant: the intricate, chaotic patterns of your iris – the colored part of your eye – are incredibly unique and stable throughout life, even more so than fingerprints. This technology quickly moved from science fiction thrillers to high-security facilities, airports, and even everyday devices, offering a robust and contactless method of identity verification. It's a testament to how complex biological features can be harnessed for advanced technological applications, proving your eyes truly are unique windows to your identity.
