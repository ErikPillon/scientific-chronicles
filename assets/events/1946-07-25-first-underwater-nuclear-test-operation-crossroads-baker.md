Just a year after the world witnessed the destructive power of the atom bomb, the U.S. decided to see what would happen if they set one off underwater. On July 25, 1946, as part of "Operation Crossroads," the "Baker" test detonated a 21-kiloton device beneath the waves of Bikini Atoll. The result? A monstrous column of water, a radioactive tsunami, and a fleet of decommissioned target ships either obliterated or doused in deadly fallout. It was a stark, eerie demonstration of nuclear power's indiscriminate reach, forever changing naval strategy and prompting serious concerns about environmental contamination. Talk about a really bad day at the beach!
