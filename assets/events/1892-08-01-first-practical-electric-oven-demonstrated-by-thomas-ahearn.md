Imagine cooking without fire! In 1892, Canadian inventor Thomas Ahearn made this a reality with his groundbreaking electric oven. While earlier experiments and patents existed, Ahearn's was one of the first truly practical and publicly demonstrated electric stoves, notably used to cook a complete meal at the Windsor Hotel in Ottawa. This flameless wonder was a marvel of modern convenience, promising cleaner kitchens and more precise temperature control. Although early electric appliances were expensive and required significant power, Ahearn's invention was a crucial step towards the electric kitchens we know today, proving that electricity could do more than just light our homes—it could cook our dinner!
