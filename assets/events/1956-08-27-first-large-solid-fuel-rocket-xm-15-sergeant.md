Before we had those massive liquid-fueled giants, solid-fuel rockets were the unsung heroes. The development of the XM-15 Sergeant in the mid-1950s marked a huge leap in getting large, reliable solid rockets off the ground. No more fiddling with tricky propellants on the launchpad; these beauties came pre-packed and ready to go, making them game-changers for military applications and setting the stage for bigger, more stable space propulsion systems. It was a solid step forward, literally!
