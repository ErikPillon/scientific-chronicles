Before the automatic street light, someone literally had to go around and turn streetlights on and off. How quaint! The development of automatic controls, particularly using photoelectric cells that could detect changes in ambient light, was a game-changer in the early 20th century. While electric street lights themselves emerged earlier, truly automatic control gained traction in the 1920s and 1930s. This innovation made urban illumination far more efficient, ensuring cities were lit precisely when they needed to be, saving labor, and bringing a touch of automated magic to the urban landscape. It's one of those silent heroes of modern convenience we barely notice, quietly illuminating our nights.
