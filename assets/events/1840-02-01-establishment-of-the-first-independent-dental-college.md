Before 1840, if you wanted to become a dentist, you mostly learned on the job, like an apprentice barber-surgeon – yikes! But then, a couple of visionary American dentists, Horace Hayden and Chapin A. Harris, decided that dental health deserved serious academic rigor. In 1840, they opened the Baltimore College of Dental Surgery, making it the world's very first independent dental college. This wasn't just a clinic; it was a full-fledged institution dedicated to teaching the science and art of dentistry, complete with lectures, dissections, and practical training. It marked a huge leap for dentistry, transforming it from a trade into a respected medical profession and giving us much better reasons to smile.
