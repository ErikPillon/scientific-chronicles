Long before Times Square became a retina-searing beacon, Broadway earned its iconic nickname, the 'Great White Way,' thanks to the dazzling glow of electric lights. While its full, glorious illumination took time, the installation of early electric arc lights and incandescent bulbs in the late 1800s transformed the district. In 1880, Broadway saw some of the earliest electric streetlights, and by the 1890s, theaters and businesses were decked out in thousands of bulbs, creating a truly spectacular, almost magical, urban landscape. It was a testament to the power of electricity and a magnet for entertainment, signaling New York's arrival as a glittering global metropolis. Talk about a glow-up!
