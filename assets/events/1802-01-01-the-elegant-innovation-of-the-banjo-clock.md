Forget your grandfather clock; the Banjo clock was the stylish icon of early 19th-century American timekeeping. Patented by the ingenious Simon Willard in 1802, this isn't just a clock; it's a piece of art! Its distinctive banjo-like shape (hence the name, duh!) with a round dial, a narrow waist, and a decorative lower box made it a truly elegant addition to any discerning household. More than just aesthetics, Willard's design was a masterclass in precision and efficiency, often featuring an eight-day movement. It was a commercial hit, proving that even everyday objects could be elevated to works of sophisticated craftsmanship. Keeping time never looked so good!
