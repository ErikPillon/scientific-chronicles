Long before Avatar made us all don silly glasses, filmmakers were already tinkering with 3D! The dream of bringing audiences *into* the screen, rather than just showing them a flat image, dates back to the very dawn of cinema. Early experiments by people like Louis Ducos du Hauron in the late 19th century showed the potential, but it was really in the early 20th century that 3D movies started making a splash in public. The first commercial screenings, like "Jim the Penman" in 1915 using a dual-projector anaglyph system, had audiences gasping as things seemingly flew off the screen. While the technology (and public enthusiasm) waxed and waned over the decades, these early efforts laid the groundwork for every cinematic rollercoaster ride that followed, proving that sometimes, two dimensions just aren't enough to capture the imagination.
