Long before health insurance or emergency rooms, the very first hospital in America opened its doors in bustling colonial Philadelphia. Founded on May 11, 1751, by the visionary polymath Benjamin Franklin and pioneering physician Dr. Thomas Bond, Pennsylvania Hospital was established 'for the reception and cure of the sick poor.' It wasn't just a place for healing; it quickly became a crucial hub for medical education and research in the burgeoning colonies, offering structured care that was a vast improvement over home remedies and quack doctors. This institution laid the groundwork for modern healthcare in the United States, establishing a legacy of compassionate care, professional training, and medical innovation that continues to thrive today.
