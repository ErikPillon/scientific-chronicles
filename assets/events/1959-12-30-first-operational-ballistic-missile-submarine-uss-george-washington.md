During the Cold War, the race for strategic advantage was fierce. Enter the ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), a game-changing weapon system. The USS George Washington, launched in 1959, wasn't just a submarine; it was the first operational vessel capable of launching nuclear ballistic missiles from beneath the waves. This beast of a machine carried 16 Polaris missiles, each packing a nuclear punch, turning the ocean depths into a hidden launchpad. Its stealth and ability to operate undetected for long periods provided an unparalleled second-strike capability, profoundly altering the dynamics of nuclear deterrence. It basically told the world: 'Even if you hit us first, we'll still get you.' This underwater behemoth cemented the concept of Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD) and forever changed global military strategy.
