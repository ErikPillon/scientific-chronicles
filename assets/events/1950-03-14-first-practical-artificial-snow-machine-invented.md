Ever wondered how ski resorts manage to maintain their pristine slopes even when Mother Nature isn't feeling particularly snowy? You can thank the ingenuity of Wayne Pierce, Art Hunt, and Howie Scheafer. In 1950, while working on a cooling system for crops, they stumbled upon the perfect recipe for making snow. They realized that by atomizing water and blowing it through freezing air with a compressor, they could create artificial snowflakes. Their ingenious 'Method for Making Snow' received a U.S. patent on March 14, 1950. This invention was an absolute game-changer, liberating the ski industry from the whims of natural snowfall and allowing winter sports enthusiasts to hit the slopes more reliably than ever before.
