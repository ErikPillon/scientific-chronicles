On April 12, 1961, the world held its breath as Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin uttered "Poyekhali!" (Let's go!) and blasted off aboard Vostok 1. Just 108 minutes later, he returned, forever etched in history as the first human to journey into outer space and orbit our planet. His single orbit was a colossal leap for mankind, showcasing the incredible engineering of Sergei Korolev's team and catapulting the USSR ahead in the space race. It wasn't just a flight; it was a testament to human courage and ingenuity, proving that our species truly belonged among the stars.
