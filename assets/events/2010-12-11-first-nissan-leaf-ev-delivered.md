Remember when electric cars felt like science fiction? Well, on December 11, 2010, the future arrived! Nissan delivered the first mass-produced, all-electric Leaf to a very eager customer in Japan, with US deliveries quickly following. This wasn't just another car; it was a pivotal moment for sustainable transportation, proving that an affordable, practical EV could actually hit the road. It might not have had the supercar looks of later EVs, but the Leaf quietly paved the way for the electric vehicle boom we see today, making 'range anxiety' a household term and sparking a global shift toward greener wheels.
