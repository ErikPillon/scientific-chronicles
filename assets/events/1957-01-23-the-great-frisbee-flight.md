Before smartphones and social media, there was the ultimate low-tech thrill: tossing a plastic disc. The Frisbee, as we know it, really took off in the late 1950s thanks to Walter Frederick Morrison's 'Pluto Platter' (patented 1957) and the marketing genius of Wham-O. But its roots were even humbler: Yale students throwing around pie tins from the Frisbie Pie Company. Imagine that! From baked goods to a global phenomenon, the Frisbee proved that sometimes the simplest inventions create the most fun. It’s still defying gravity and bringing people together, one epic throw at a time.
