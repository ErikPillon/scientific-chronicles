On May 26, 1908, after years of painstaking and often frustrating drilling, prospectors for William Knox D'Arcy's syndicate struck oil in Masjid-i-Sulaiman, Persia (modern-day Iran). This wasn't just a gusher; it was the spark that ignited the Middle East's oil boom, transforming global geopolitics, economics, and energy consumption. Before this, the region was largely overlooked; afterward, it became the world's energy heartland. The discovery led to the formation of the Anglo-Persian Oil Company (later BP) and set in motion a century of resource competition, technological innovation, and profound social change, proving that sometimes, what's hidden deep underground can shift the entire world above it.
