The Altair 8800 wasn't just a computer; it was the spark that ignited the personal computer revolution! Released in 1975 by MITS, this kit-built microcomputer, based on Intel's 8080 microprocessor, was affordable enough for hobbyists and tinkers to buy. It didn't come with a keyboard or monitor—just a bunch of switches and blinking lights—but it captivated imaginations. Famously, Bill Gates and Paul Allen developed BASIC for the Altair, marking the birth of Microsoft. The Altair showed the world that computers weren't just for massive corporations or governments; they could be personal, accessible, and revolutionary. It truly put computing power into the hands of the people and changed everything.
