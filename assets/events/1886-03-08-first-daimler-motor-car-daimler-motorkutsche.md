When most people thought 'horse,' Gottlieb Daimler thought 'horsepower.' In 1886, with his brilliant engineer Wilhelm Maybach, he adapted their lightweight, high-speed internal combustion engine to a four-wheeled vehicle, creating what's often considered the world's first true automobile, the Daimler-Motorkutsche. While Karl Benz also gets credit for his 'Motorwagen' around the same time, Daimler's focus on powerful, versatile engines was a game-changer. This wasn't just a fancy buggy; it was the roaring ancestor of every car on the road today, kickstarting the automotive age and changing how we travel forever.
