Picture this: June 23, 1988. Washington D.C. is sweltering under a heatwave. And inside a Congressional hearing room, NASA climate scientist James Hansen dropped a bombshell. With the air conditioning famously malfunctioning (oh, the irony!), Hansen declared, with 99% certainty, that human-caused global warming wasn't just a theory for the future – it was *already here*. His blunt, direct testimony, backed by clear scientific data, was a watershed moment. It didn't just grab headlines; it catapulted climate change from academic discussions into the public and political spotlight, sparking decades of debate and research. Hansen literally put climate change on the map for the masses, making it impossible to ignore.
