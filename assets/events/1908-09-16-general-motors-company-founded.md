On September 16, 1908, William C. Durant founded General Motors, a move that would consolidate a burgeoning automotive industry and put America on wheels. Starting with Buick and quickly acquiring Oldsmobile, Cadillac, and Chevrolet, GM didn't just build cars; it built an empire that defined mass production and consumer culture for decades. It was the moment the dream of personal mobility went into high gear, transforming landscapes and lifestyles across the globe, and cementing the automobile's place in daily life.
