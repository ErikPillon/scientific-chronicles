Before calculators, before computers, there were mathematical tables – vast lists of numbers that made complex calculations possible for astronomers, navigators, and engineers. And the printing press was their best friend! Johannes Müller von Königsberg, better known as Regiomontanus, was a superstar German mathematician and astronomer. Around 1472, he published his *Ephemerides* and various trigonometric tables, which were among the earliest and most influential printed mathematical tables. These weren't just fancy books; they were indispensable tools that dramatically improved accuracy and spread scientific knowledge, making everything from predicting eclipses to sailing across oceans a whole lot easier. Talk about a glow-up for numbers!
