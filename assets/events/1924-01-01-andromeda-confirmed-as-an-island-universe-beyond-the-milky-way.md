For ages, astronomers thought our Milky Way was pretty much the whole universe. Then came Edwin Hubble, ready to shatter everyone's cosmic comfort zone. In 1924, this American astronomical legend, using the mighty Hooker Telescope, spotted Cepheid variable stars in the 'Andromeda Nebula.' These stellar beacons allowed him to accurately measure its distance. The jaw-dropping revelation? Andromeda wasn't a swirling gas cloud *within* our galaxy; it was a colossal 'island universe' hundreds of thousands of light-years away! This wasn't just a discovery; it was a universe-expanding revelation, proving our galaxy was just one of many. Hubble didn't just measure distances; he gave us infinity!
