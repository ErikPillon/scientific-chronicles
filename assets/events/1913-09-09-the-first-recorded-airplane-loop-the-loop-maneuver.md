In the early 1910s, flying was still a relatively new and terrifying endeavor for most. Then, two pilots, independently and almost simultaneously, decided to turn the world on its head – literally. Russian pilot Pyotr Nesterov is credited with performing the first *complete* loop on August 31, 1913, in Kiev. However, French aviator Adolphe Pégoud, on September 9, 1913, in Buc, France, performed a loop, and then several subsequent inverted flights and "tail-slides," to widespread public fanfare, becoming famous for the "loop-the-loop" and demonstrating the structural integrity of aircraft and the capabilities of skilled pilots. These breathtaking stunts ushered in the age of aerobatics, thrilling crowds and pushing the boundaries of flight.
