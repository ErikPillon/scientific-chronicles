In a move that made everyone wonder if they'd left their space helmets on too tight, American hero John Glenn—the first American to orbit Earth back in '62—returned to space in 1998 aboard the Space Shuttle Discovery, mission STS-95. At 77 years young, he became the oldest person to fly in space, shattering age barriers and inspiring millions. While his primary role was to serve as a human guinea pig for studies on aging in microgravity, let's be real, he also reminded us that legends never truly retire; they just find new orbits. It was a spectacular bookend to an already stellar career.
