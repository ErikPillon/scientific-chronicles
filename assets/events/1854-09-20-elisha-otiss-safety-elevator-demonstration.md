Before Elisha Otis came along, 'going up' meant a whole lot of stairs, and elevators were risky business. But at the 1854 New York Crystal Palace Exhibition, Otis famously changed everything! Perched on his newfangled elevator platform, he had an assistant cut the hoist rope. Instead of a spectacular (and deadly) crash, Otis stayed put, dramatically declaring, 'All safe, gentlemen!' His ingenious safety brake, which engaged instantly if the ropes failed, removed the biggest hurdle to public acceptance. Suddenly, skyscrapers weren't just dreams; they were practical possibilities. Thanks to Otis, we can all enjoy the view from the top without breaking a sweat (or a neck).
