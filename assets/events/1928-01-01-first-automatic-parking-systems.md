Parking a car in cramped city spaces has always been a headache. Enter the automatic garage, a surprisingly early innovation that aimed to solve this urban dilemma in the 1920s. These weren't your sleek, modern robotic systems, but rather elaborate mechanical contraptions that would lift, slide, and stack cars, minimizing human effort and maximizing space. The first fully automated garage system in the U.S. reportedly opened in Portland, Oregon, in 1928. Imagine pulling up, leaving your car, and watching a machine whisk it away – it was futuristic and mind-bogglingly clever for its time, a testament to early automotive-age ingenuity and a precursor to today's smart parking solutions.
