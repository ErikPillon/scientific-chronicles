Albert Einstein, the intellectual rockstar of his era, graced countless stages, captivating audiences with his mind-bending theories. While he delivered many lectures, a notable one was his Nobel Lecture on December 11, 1922, delivered in Sweden after receiving his Nobel Prize (which, ironically, was for the photoelectric effect, not relativity). Whether he was demystifying spacetime or simply charming a crowd with his dishevelled wit, an 'Einstein lecture' was always a major intellectual event. He had a unique ability to make the complex sound... well, still complex, but incredibly cool and worth listening to, even if you only understood half of it.
