Before Netflix and TikTok, there was radio, and CBS was a titan. The network's origins trace back to September 18, 1927, when talent agent Arthur Judson, frustrated with RCA's dominance, launched United Independent Broadcasters. It quickly ran into financial trouble and was renamed Columbia Phonograph Broadcasting System after a deal with the Columbia Phonograph Company. The pivotal moment came in 1928 when William S. Paley, a young advertising executive whose family owned a cigar company, bought the struggling network. Paley's vision and business acumen transformed it into the Columbia Broadcasting System, a powerhouse that would define American radio and later television for decades, bringing news, drama, and comedy right into people's living rooms. A true media empire in the making!
