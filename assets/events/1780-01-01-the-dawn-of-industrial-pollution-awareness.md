So, humanity started getting really clever with machines during the Industrial Revolution, creating amazing new products and power. But, oops! All that progress came with a hefty, grimy price: industrial pollution. From the smoke-choked skies of 18th-century London to rivers turned rainbow colors by factory discharge, the air got thick and the water got murky. Initially, it was just the smell of progress, but soon, people realized this wasn't just unsightly; it was seriously messing with public health and the environment. While it’s not a single 'event,' the growing recognition and eventual policy responses to industrial pollution mark a monumental shift in how we view the relationship between industry and our planet. It’s the origin story of environmentalism, really.
