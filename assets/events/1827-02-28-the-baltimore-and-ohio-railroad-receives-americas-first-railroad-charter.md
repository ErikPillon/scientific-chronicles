Before airplanes and cars, getting around was a slow, bumpy affair. The solution? Railroads! On February 28, 1827, the Baltimore and Ohio (B&O) Railroad received the very first corporate charter for a common carrier railroad in the United States. This wasn't just a piece of paper; it was the green light for an industrial revolution. Visionaries in Baltimore wanted a faster, cheaper way to compete with New York's Erie Canal for western trade. This charter allowed them to raise capital and build tracks, effectively kicking off America's love affair with the locomotive and transforming the nation's economy and geography. All aboard for economic progress!
