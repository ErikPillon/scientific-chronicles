Before "Big Oil" was even a twinkle in anyone's eye, a group of ambitious New Englanders formed the Pennsylvania Rock Oil Company on December 30, 1854. While they later renamed it Seneca Oil Company and hired Edwin Drake to drill, this incorporation marks the official beginning of the U.S. petroleum industry. Initially, they eyed rock oil for lighting and lubrication, not realizing they were about to tap into a resource that would fundamentally transform global economies, transportation, and geopolitics. It wasn't just about striking oil; it was about striking the foundation for a whole new industrial era, making modern life, with all its cars and plastics, possible.
