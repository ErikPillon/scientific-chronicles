Right after World War II, with the advent of more practical helicopters, folks dreamt of a future where you'd hail a 'helicopter bus' instead of a taxi. Major airlines and entrepreneurs, like those behind Los Angeles Airways and Sabena, actually launched scheduled passenger services in the late 1940s and 50s. Imagine flying over traffic from the airport to downtown! While it sounded incredibly futuristic, high operational costs, noise complaints, and safety concerns meant these aerial commutes never quite took off on a large scale. Still, for a brief, glorious period, the skies were almost bustling with rotor-wing public transport, proving that the dream of flying cars (or buses!) has been around for ages.
