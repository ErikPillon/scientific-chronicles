May 1943 saw one of WWII's most audacious missions: Operation Chastise. Under the leadership of Wing Commander Guy Gibson, the RAF's 617 Squadron (forever known as the Dambusters) took to the skies, armed with Barnes Wallis's revolutionary bouncing bombs. Their target? The industrial heartland of Germany, specifically its massive dams. The raid, flown at dangerously low altitudes and requiring incredible skill, created spectacular breaches and huge floods, shaking morale and proving that with enough ingenuity (and a bit of crazy), anything was possible. It was a movie-worthy mission before movies were even made about it!
