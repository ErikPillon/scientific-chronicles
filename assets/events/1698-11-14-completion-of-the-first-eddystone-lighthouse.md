Building a lighthouse on Eddystone Rocks, a treacherous reef off the coast of Cornwall, was considered madness. But in the late 17th century, a determined (some might say eccentric) engineer named Henry Winstanley took on the challenge. After years of battling storms, logistical nightmares, and a touch of piracy, his unique, ornate lighthouse was completed in 1698. While his original structure tragically fell victim to a massive storm, taking Winstanley with it, his audacity paved the way for future, sturdier designs. It was a monumental first step in conquering the high seas with a beacon of safety.
