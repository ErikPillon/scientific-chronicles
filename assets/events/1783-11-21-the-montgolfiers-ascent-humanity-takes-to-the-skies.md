Before airplanes, rockets, or even gliders, there were balloons! On November 21, 1783, a crowd gathered in Paris to witness a marvel: the first manned flight. Pilâtre de Rozier and Marquis d'Arlandes climbed into the basket of a beautiful hot-air balloon, designed by the brilliant Montgolfier brothers, Joseph-Michel and Jacques-Étienne. As the flame heated the air, the massive fabric envelope swelled, and gently, majestically, they lifted off the ground, ascending over 500 feet and floating for about 25 minutes. This wasn't just a flight; it was a revolution! It proved that humans could conquer the sky, sparking a worldwide obsession with aviation and opening up a whole new dimension of exploration. Talk about a breathtaking commute!
