Hold onto your preconceptions, because in 1924, a small skull discovered in a South African quarry turned the world of human evolution upside down! Dubbed the "Taung Child" by Raymond Dart, this fossil belonged to a previously unknown hominin, *Australopithecus africanus*. Before this, the scientific consensus was that human origins lay in Asia, with large brains evolving *before* upright walking. But the Taung Child's skull, with its small braincase yet clear indicators of bipedalism, screamed "Africa!" and suggested that walking came first. Dart faced significant resistance, but his persistence and meticulous analysis eventually convinced the scientific community. This discovery was a monumental shift, firmly placing Africa as the cradle of humanity and kickstarting decades of intensive paleoanthropological research on the continent. It really was a tiny skull with a massive impact!
