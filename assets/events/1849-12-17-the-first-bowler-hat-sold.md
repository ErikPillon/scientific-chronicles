Before the bowler, gentlemen's hats were mostly top hats, easily knocked off by low-hanging branches while riding. So, in 1849, Edward Coke commissioned London hatters James Lock & Co. to create a durable, close-fitting hat for his gamekeepers. Lock's gave the job to hatmakers Thomas and William Bowler, who crafted a stiff, round, low-crowned felt hat. On December 17, 1849, Coke reportedly stomped on the first prototype to test its strength before purchasing it. An instant hit, the 'Bowler' (named after its maker) became an iconic symbol of the working and middle classes, eventually becoming a global fashion staple. Talk about a hat with history!
