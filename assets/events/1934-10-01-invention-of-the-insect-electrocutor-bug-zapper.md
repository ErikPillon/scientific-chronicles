Ever been enjoying a nice evening outdoors only to be swarmed by uninvited, biting guests? Enter the insect electrocutor, your trusty bug zapper! While various methods to dispatch insects existed, the modern electric bug zapper, with its iconic high-voltage grid, saw significant development and patenting in the early 20th century. William M. Frost, for example, patented an "Insect Electrocutor" in 1934. The device lures insects with a light and then, *zap*, they meet their electrically charged demise. It's a simple, yet effective (and satisfyingly loud for some) invention that became a staple for outdoor pest control, proving that sometimes, a little electric shock is all it takes to keep the peace.
