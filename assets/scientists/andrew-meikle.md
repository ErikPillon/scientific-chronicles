Before Andrew Meikle, separating grain from chaff was back-breaking work, practically a biblical curse. But this Scottish mechanical marvel had enough of that! Born in 1719, Meikle brought the future to the farm with his invention of the threshing machine in the late 18th century. Suddenly, what took dozens of laborers days of endless flailing could be done in a fraction of the time, making agriculture far more efficient and feeding a growing population with less sweat. He didn't just invent a machine; he sparked an agricultural revolution, freeing countless hands for other endeavors. Move over, horse and cart; Meikle's machine was the original farm automation!
