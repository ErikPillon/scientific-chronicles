Herbert A. Hauptman was a mathematical wizard who peered into the atomic arrangements of molecules! This American mathematician and crystallographer, alongside Jerome Karle, revolutionized X-ray crystallography. Before their work, figuring out a molecule's 3D structure from X-ray data was like trying to solve a puzzle without seeing the picture. Hauptman developed 'direct methods' – mathematical formulas that, incredibly, allowed scientists to deduce the positions of atoms. This breakthrough, which earned him a share of the 1985 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, sped up the discovery of countless new drugs and materials. He made the invisible visible, with math!
