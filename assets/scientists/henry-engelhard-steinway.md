Henry Engelhard Steinway (born Heinrich Engelhard Steinweg in 1797) wasn't just making pianos; he was making *music history*. A master cabinetmaker with an ear for perfection, he left Germany for America in 1850 and, with his sons, founded Steinway & Sons. He wasn't just assembling instruments; he was inventing and refining, incorporating features like the overstrung scale and the duplex scale to create a richer, more powerful sound. His relentless pursuit of acoustic perfection and manufacturing innovation didn't just build a world-famous brand; it fundamentally reshaped how pianos were made and what they could achieve. Talk about hitting all the right notes!
