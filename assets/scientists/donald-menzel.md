Donald Menzel built his career on solar and stellar spectroscopy, helping work out the physical conditions and chemical composition inside stars and directing the Harvard College Observatory for over a decade. He trained a generation of American astronomers and pushed hard for the field to modernize with better instruments and international collaboration. But Menzel had a curious side hustle: he was one of the most prominent public debunkers of UFO sightings, writing multiple books arguing that supposed flying saucers were almost always misidentified natural phenomena, weather balloons, or optical effects. It's a strange footnote for a serious astrophysicist, but it made him one of the most recognizable scientific voices on the subject for decades. 🌞🛸
