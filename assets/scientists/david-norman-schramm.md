David N. Schramm was an American astrophysicist with a mind as vast as the universe he studied. He was a rockstar in cosmology, particularly renowned for his work on Big Bang nucleosynthesis – the process that cooked up the light elements in the early universe, just moments after its birth. Schramm was a wizard at connecting the incredibly small (nuclear physics) with the incredibly large (the cosmos), helping us understand how everything from protons and neutrons to galaxies and superclusters came to be. He was also a passionate advocate for science education and a major voice in the quest for dark matter. Sadly, we lost this brilliant thinker too soon in a plane crash, but his cosmic legacy continues to inspire stargazers and particle hunters alike.
