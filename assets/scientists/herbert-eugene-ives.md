Herbert E. Ives was the kind of scientist who made pictures move before you could even stream them. This American physicist and son of an inventor (Frederic E. Ives, another visual pioneer) was key in developing early forms of television and phototelegraphy. But his most famous gig? In 1938, he and G.R. Stilwell performed an experiment that directly confirmed Einstein's theory of time dilation, showing clocks actually run slower when moving. Talk about a mind-bender! He brought images to life and proved relativity on the side. What a show-off!
