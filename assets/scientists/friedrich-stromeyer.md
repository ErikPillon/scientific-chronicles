Friedrich Stromeyer (often misspelled 'Strohmeyer'!) was a German chemist who literally struck gold – or rather, cadmium! In 1817, while investigating zinc carbonate for pharmaceutical purity, he noticed a mysterious yellow impurity that turned brown when heated. Lo and behold, after some clever chemical detective work, he isolated a brand new metallic element: cadmium. This shiny, soft metal, initially found as an impurity, eventually found its way into pigments, batteries, and even nuclear reactors. Stromeyer's sharp eye and meticulous experimentation added a crucial piece to our understanding of the elements. Who knew impurities could be so exciting?
