Frank Borman wasn't just an astronaut; he was one of the brave pioneers who literally gave humanity a new perspective on itself. As commander of Apollo 8, he led the first crew to orbit the Moon, capturing that iconic 'Earthrise' photo that forever changed how we see our pale blue dot. Before that, he was part of the Gemini 7 mission, setting an endurance record. Borman was a serious, no-nonsense leader who inspired confidence during one of history's most audacious journeys. His steady hand guided humanity's gaze toward another world, reminding us all how small, yet interconnected, we truly are. Talk about a cosmic commute!
