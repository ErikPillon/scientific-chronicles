Herbert Thacker Herr was an American mechanical engineer who loved making big machines move better. He spent a significant part of his career at Westinghouse, where he was a key figure in developing and improving steam turbines and locomotives. Think about the power plants and massive trains of the early 20th century – Herr was one of the brilliant minds making them more efficient and powerful. He held numerous patents and pushed the boundaries of mechanical design, ensuring that industry and transportation kept chugging along at full steam! He was a true powerhouse behind the machines of his era.
