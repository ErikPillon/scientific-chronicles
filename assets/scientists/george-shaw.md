Meet George Shaw, a British naturalist who basically had the coolest job in the late 18th and early 19th centuries: describing all the weird and wonderful creatures arriving in Britain from newly explored lands! As a keeper at the British Museum, he got first dibs on examining exotic specimens. His most famous 'aha!' moment? Being one of the first Europeans to describe the platypus, a creature so bizarre he initially thought it might be a hoax stitched together from different animals. He also churned out several multi-volume works on zoology, meticulously documenting species and their characteristics, providing invaluable contributions to natural history. He brought the wild world to bewildered Europeans!
