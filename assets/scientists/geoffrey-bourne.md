Geoffrey Bourne, a British-American biologist with a penchant for primates, wasn't just monkeying around. He dove deep into histochemistry – that's the chemistry of tissues for us non-scientists – before swinging into primatology. Bourne spent a good chunk of his career studying monkeys and apes, trying to figure out what makes us tick, or rather, what makes us *like* them. He even founded the Yerkes Regional Primate Research Center at Emory University. So, next time you see a chimp, remember Bourne, who probably knew more about its cells than it did. He basically dedicated his life to asking, 'What's the difference between us and them?' and then trying to chemically dissect the answer.
