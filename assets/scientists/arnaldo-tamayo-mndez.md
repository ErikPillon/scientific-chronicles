Before space tourism was even a twinkle in Musk's eye, Arnaldo Tamayo Méndez was making history! In 1980, this Cuban military pilot became the first person of African descent and the first Latin American to orbit Earth, as part of the Soviet Interkosmos program. Spending eight days aboard the Salyut 6 space station, Méndez wasn't just enjoying the view; he was conducting scientific experiments. His journey wasn't just a personal achievement; it was a huge symbolic leap, showing the world that the cosmos was open to everyone, regardless of background or nationality. A true trailblazer who proved that the sky wasn't the limit!
