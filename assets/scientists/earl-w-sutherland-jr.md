Meet Earl W. Sutherland Jr., the medical maestro who figured out how hormones, those bossy little chemical messengers, actually get their commands inside a cell. He cracked the code, identifying cyclic AMP (cAMP) as the 'second messenger' – essentially, the diligent middleman that relays signals from hormones (the 'first messengers') from the cell surface to its interior. His groundbreaking work, which earned him a Nobel Prize, reshaped our understanding of cell communication and disease. So, next time your body is doing anything, remember Earl's discovery is probably orchestrating the cellular chat behind the scenes!
