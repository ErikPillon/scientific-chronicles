Frank Perret was the original daredevil of geology, famous for getting up close and personal with some of the world's most fiery mountains. Forget remote sensing; Perret preferred to be right there on the rim, often setting up observatories directly on active volcanoes like Mount Pelée and Vesuvius. He practically invented modern volcanology through his meticulous, hands-on observation and instrumental studies. Before him, studying volcanoes was more 'run away!' than 'measure this!' Perret's courage and dedication in understanding these magnificent, destructive forces were legendary. He didn't just watch eruptions; he lived alongside them, offering insights that were literally groundbreaking.
