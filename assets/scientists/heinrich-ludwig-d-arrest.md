Heinrich Ludwig d'Arrest, born in 1822, was a German astronomer who certainly knew how to spot a celestial body! While still a student in Berlin, he played a crucial role in the discovery of Neptune, meticulously checking star charts to confirm Urbain Le Verrier's theoretical prediction. But he wasn't just a sidekick; d'Arrest went on to discover his own comet (6P/d'Arrest, still visible today!) and several nebulae, proving he had an eagle eye for anything twinkling in the night sky. He was truly one of those stargazers who helped us map out our cosmic neighborhood, showing that sometimes, the best discoveries are hiding just out of sight.
