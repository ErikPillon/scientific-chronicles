Hugh Robert Mill was a Scottish polymath of the seas and skies, a true pioneer in British oceanography and meteorology. He started his career in meteorology, but his passion for the ocean led him to become a leading figure in early 20th-century marine science. He served as the librarian for the Royal Geographical Society and later as the director of the British Rainfall Organization, meticulously collecting and analyzing climatic data. Mill was instrumental in promoting oceanographic research and education in Britain, helping to shape the study of the world's oceans into a distinct scientific discipline. He was essentially the guy who made sure Britain knew its weather and its waters, inside and out.
