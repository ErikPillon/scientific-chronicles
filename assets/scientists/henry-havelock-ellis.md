Havelock Ellis was the kind of person who dared to talk about *that* topic when no one else would. This British physician decided that human sexuality wasn't just for hushed whispers; it was a legitimate field for scientific study. His multi-volume 'Studies in the Psychology of Sex' rocked the boat in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, challenging Victorian prudishness and paving the way for more open and understanding conversations about sex. He even coined terms like 'autoeroticism' and explored everything from homosexuality to fetishism with a surprisingly scientific, non-judgmental eye. Talk about breaking taboos and opening minds!
