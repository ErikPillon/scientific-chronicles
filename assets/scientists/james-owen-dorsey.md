Forget Indiana Jones, meet James Owen Dorsey – a real-life linguistic adventurer! This American ethnologist dedicated his life to something far more valuable than gold: the vanishing words and stories of Native American tribes, especially the Siouan and Caddoan peoples. He wasn't just observing; he was *listening*, spending years patiently documenting complex languages and cultural practices that were rapidly being eroded. His work became a foundational pillar for future anthropologists, ensuring that the voices of these communities wouldn't be lost to history. Talk about a legacy that echoes through time!
