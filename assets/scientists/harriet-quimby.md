Before she could even vote, Harriet Quimby was making headlines. Born in 1875, this multi-talented American journalist, screenwriter, and pilot was already a trailblazer as the first licensed female pilot in the U.S. But her biggest splash came on April 16, 1912, when she became the first woman to fly across the English Channel. Despite battling fog and chilly winds, and dressed head-to-toe in her signature purple flying suit, she navigated her monoplane from Dover, England, to a beach in France. Tragically, her life was cut short just months later in a flying accident. However, her daring achievement, overshadowed by the Titanic disaster that same week, remains a powerful symbol of early aviation courage and women's indomitable spirit. She literally soared over expectations!
