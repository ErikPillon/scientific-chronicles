Herbert Henry Dow was an American chemist with a knack for making things, especially industrial chemicals. Frustrated with inefficient methods of extracting bromine from brine, he invented the 'Dow process,' which revolutionized its production. This wasn't just a cool lab trick; it led him to found The Dow Chemical Company in 1897. His innovations didn't stop there, expanding into chlorine and other chemicals, all while focusing on efficiency and cost reduction. Basically, Dow was the guy who figured out how to get useful stuff out of salty water and built a chemical empire doing it. Talk about making a splash!
