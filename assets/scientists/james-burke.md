If you've ever wondered how a Roman chariot wheel is connected to a microwave oven, you've probably encountered the mind of James Burke. This legendary British science historian and broadcaster practically invented the 'everything is connected' genre with his groundbreaking TV series, 'Connections,' first airing in 1978. Burke doesn't just list facts; he weaves sprawling narratives, showing how seemingly disparate inventions and discoveries ripple through history, leading to unexpected outcomes. He's the ultimate intellectual detective, uncovering the glorious, messy, and often hilarious pathways of human ingenuity. His work isn't just history; it's a masterclass in seeing the big picture and understanding the unpredictable forces that shape our world.
