Forget Freudian couches and behaviorist carrots and sticks – Carl R. Rogers ushered in a revolution in psychology by putting the 'human' back in human beings. This American psychologist was a leading figure in the humanistic movement, believing that everyone has an innate drive toward self-actualization. His most famous contribution? Client-Centered Therapy (later Person-Centered Therapy), where the therapist isn't a sage diagnosing problems but a facilitator, offering unconditional positive regard, empathy, and genuineness to help clients find their own solutions. Rogers taught us that creating a supportive, non-judgmental environment is key to personal growth. His profoundly optimistic view of human potential transformed not just therapy but also education, leadership, and conflict resolution, making him one of the most cited psychologists of the 20th century. He truly championed the individual, helping people bloom from within.
