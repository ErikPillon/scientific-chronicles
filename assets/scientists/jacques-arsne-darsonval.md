Jacques-Arsène d'Arsonval was a French physician and biophysicist who loved all things electrical, especially when applied to the human body. He was a protégé of Claude Bernard and went on to invent the d'Arsonval galvanometer, a super-sensitive device for measuring current, which was crucial for early electrical measurements. But he didn't stop there! He delved into the effects of high-frequency alternating currents on biological systems, pioneering what became known as 'd'Arsonvalization' or electrotherapy. He even envisioned tapping into thermal energy differences in the ocean, foreseeing ocean thermal energy conversion (OTEC). This guy was electrifyingly ahead of his time in both medical tech and energy ideas!
