George A. Dorsey was basically Indiana Jones with a notepad and a passion for culture! This American anthropologist plunged into the heart of Native American communities, especially in the Plains region, documenting their traditions, languages, and stories before they faded away. As a curator at the Field Museum in Chicago, he brought these cultures to life for the public. Dorsey wasn't just observing; he was preserving, making him a crucial figure in early American anthropology. His adventures ensured that rich cultural histories wouldn't be forgotten.
