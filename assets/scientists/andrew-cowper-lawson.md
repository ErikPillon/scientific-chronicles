If you've ever felt the ground shake in California, you've got Andrew Cowper Lawson to thank for some of the best early explanations. This Scottish-American geologist was the rock star of Western U.S. geology, mapping vast swaths of California and unraveling the mysteries of its complex landforms. His biggest claim to fame? He was the head of the State Earthquake Investigation Commission after the devastating 1906 San Francisco earthquake. Lawson not only meticulously documented the disaster but also precisely identified the San Andreas Fault as the source, leading to crucial insights into earthquake mechanics and plate tectonics (before the full theory was even developed!). He literally put the 'fault' in 'earthquake fault,' shaping how we understand geological hazards today. Talk about making an impact, both literally and scientifically!
