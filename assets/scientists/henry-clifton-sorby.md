Meet Henry Clifton Sorby (1826-1908), the OG micro-explorer! Before Sorby, scientists mostly just stared at rocks and metals, scratching their heads. He had the bright idea of slicing them super thin, polishing them, and peeking through a microscope. Voilà! Suddenly, the hidden structures of rocks (petrography) and metals (metallography) were revealed. This wasn't just a cool party trick; it completely transformed geology and materials science, opening up a whole new universe of understanding about how materials form and behave. Basically, he taught us to look closer, and the scientific world has been thanking him ever since.
