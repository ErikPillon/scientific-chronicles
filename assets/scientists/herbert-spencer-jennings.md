Not to be confused with *the* Herbert Spencer (though he shared a middle name with the philosopher!), Herbert Spencer Jennings was an American zoologist who made big waves in the microscopic world. He was a pioneer in studying the genetics and behavior of protozoa, those fascinating single-celled creatures. Imagine trying to figure out heredity using tiny organisms that reproduce by simply dividing! Jennings did just that, shedding light on fundamental principles of genetics, variation, and evolution long before DNA was cool. He proved that even the smallest creatures have grand stories to tell about life itself.
