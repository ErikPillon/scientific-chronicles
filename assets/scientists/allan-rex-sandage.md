Allan Sandage, the American astronomer, was a true titan of the cosmos. A student of Edwin Hubble himself, Sandage dedicated his life to pinning down the universe's expansion rate (the Hubble Constant) and, by extension, its age. For decades, he was *the* guy measuring cosmic distances, often challenging emerging theories with his meticulous observations. He famously quipped that cosmology was a search for two numbers, and he spent his career trying to nail them. His work helped shape our understanding of the universe's past, present, and future, making him a legend in the field, even if sometimes a controversial one. He wasn't afraid to go against the grain, always letting the data lead the way, like a true scientific detective.
