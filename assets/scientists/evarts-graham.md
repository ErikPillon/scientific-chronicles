Ever heard of someone having a lung removed? Thank Evarts Graham for making that a possibility! This American surgeon was an absolute trailblazer in thoracic surgery. Back in 1933, he performed the first successful pneumonectomy (removing an entire lung) on a patient suffering from lung cancer. It was a monumental feat, not just because the surgery itself was incredibly risky, but because it opened up a whole new realm of possibilities for treating lung diseases. He basically proved that the human body, with the right surgical finesse, could survive such a drastic procedure, giving hope to countless patients.
