Georg Joachim Rheticus was basically the hype-man for one of the biggest scientific revolutions ever. Born in 1514 in Feldkirch (then part of Austria), this mathematician and astronomer sought out Nicolaus Copernicus and became his only student. While Copernicus was famously hesitant to publish his heliocentric theory (you know, sun-at-the-center, not Earth), Rheticus was like, 'Dude, this is gold!' He convinced Copernicus to let him publish a summary, the *Narratio Prima*, in 1540, which introduced the world to the radical idea that Earth wasn't the center of everything. He also made significant contributions to trigonometry. Rheticus wasn't just a student; he was the vital link that pushed a reluctant genius's world-changing ideas into the public sphere, helping to kickstart modern astronomy.
