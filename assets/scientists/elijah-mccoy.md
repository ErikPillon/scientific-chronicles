Ever heard the phrase 'the real McCoy'? You can thank Elijah McCoy! Born to formerly enslaved parents who escaped to Canada, McCoy became a brilliant mechanical engineer and inventor. Despite facing racial discrimination, he patented over 50 inventions, most famously his automatic lubricators for steam engines. Before McCoy, trains and factory machinery had to be stopped frequently for manual oiling. His devices allowed continuous operation, vastly improving efficiency. His inventions were so superior that others often tried to sell inferior copies, leading buyers to ask if they were getting 'the real McCoy' – a testament to his unmatched ingenuity and quality.
