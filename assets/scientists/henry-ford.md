Henry Ford (1863-1947) wasn't just selling cars; he was selling a revolution. He dreamed of making automobiles affordable for the average person, and he achieved it with the Model T. His real genius lay in applying scientific management and engineering principles to create the moving assembly line, dramatically slashing production time and costs. This wasn't just about making cars faster; it transformed manufacturing worldwide, ushering in an era of mass production and consumerism. While some of his social views were, let's say, *less* progressive than his engineering, his impact on industrial science and the modern world is undeniable. He put the world on wheels, one efficient black car at a time.
