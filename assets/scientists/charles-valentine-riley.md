Charles Valentine Riley was the British-American entomologist who declared war on crop-destroying bugs and often won! After moving to the U.S. as a teen, he became a trailblazer in agricultural entomology, eventually serving as the first U.S. State Entomologist. His most famous victory? Saving the French wine industry. When the tiny phylloxera aphid started decimating European grapevines, Riley discovered that American grape rootstock was resistant. His genius solution of grafting European vines onto American roots rescued France's vineyards from ruin. He also pioneered biological control methods, using natural predators to manage pests, a concept way ahead of its time. Basically, he was the grape's best friend and the bug's worst nightmare, ensuring our tables remained well-stocked with wine and food.
