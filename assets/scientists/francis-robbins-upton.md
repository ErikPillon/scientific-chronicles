You know Edison, but do you know Francis Robbins Upton? Born in 1852, this American mathematician and physicist was the unsung hero behind some of Edison's most famous inventions, especially the practical incandescent light bulb. While Edison was the visionary, Upton was the brilliant mind doing the complex calculations, the rigorous testing, and the scientific heavy lifting needed to turn an idea into a working product. He brought systematic scientific methodology to Edison's Menlo Park lab, helping make light bulbs last longer and be more efficient. He was the brains that made the bulb not just glow, but *work* for everyone. Every time you flip a light switch, give a quiet nod to Upton for his indispensable role in bringing electric light to the masses!
