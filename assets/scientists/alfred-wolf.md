Ever wonder how we find all that oil and gas buried deep beneath our feet? A big shout-out goes to Alfred Wolf, an Austrian-American physicist and geophysicist. Born in 1893, Wolf dedicated his career to figuring out how to 'listen' to the Earth. He was a crucial figure in the early days of seismic exploration, developing sophisticated instruments and methods that used sound waves to map subsurface geological structures. Think of him as an expert in Earth's ultrasound. His innovations at Geophysical Research Corporation and later at Shell Oil were instrumental in making petroleum exploration more efficient and less of a shot in the dark. Without his clever ideas, our modern world might look a whole lot different, and probably a lot less fueled!
