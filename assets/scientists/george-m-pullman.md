George M. Pullman wasn't just selling train tickets; he was selling luxury on wheels! This American engineer and industrialist saw a problem (uncomfortable overnight train travel) and delivered a plush, comfy solution: the Pullman sleeping car. Imagine traveling across continents in style, complete with fancy beds, attentive service, and gourmet dining – he made it happen. His innovations transformed long-distance rail travel from a bumpy chore into an elegant experience, setting new standards for comfort and service. Pullman showed that engineering brilliance wasn't just about making things work, but about making them wonderfully enjoyable. He basically turned train journeys into mobile hotels, making him a true pioneer of travel comfort.
