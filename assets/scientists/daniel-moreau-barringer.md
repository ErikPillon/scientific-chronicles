Daniel Barringer was a man with a singular obsession: a giant hole in the Arizona desert. While everyone else thought 'volcano,' this astute mining engineer and businessman looked at what is now known as Meteor Crater and declared, 'That's an impact from space!' He wasn't just right; he spent years and a small fortune trying to mine the crater for the supposed millions of tons of iron-nickel meteorite he believed lay beneath. Though he never found the bounty he sought, his pioneering work proved the crater's meteoric origin, turning a geological curiosity into a cosmic landmark. Talk about thinking outside the box... or rather, inside a very big one!
