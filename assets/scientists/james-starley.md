Before the Tour de France and daily commutes, there was James Starley, the English inventor who practically put the 'cycle' in 'bicycle.' Nicknamed the 'Father of the Bicycle Industry,' Starley was a genius of mechanical innovation. He didn't just make bikes; he revolutionized them, introducing critical features like the differential gear and the 'Rover Safety Bicycle' – the precursor to modern bikes with two equally sized wheels and a chain drive. His inventions transformed cycling from a quirky hobby into a practical mode of transport, making two-wheeled freedom accessible to the masses. He truly got the world moving on two wheels!
