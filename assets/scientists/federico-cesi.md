Meet Federico Cesi, a Roman noble with a penchant for pondering the natural world. Instead of just enjoying his aristocratic life, Cesi decided in 1603, at the tender age of 18, to found the Accademia dei Lincei – the first scientific academy on Earth! Imagine, a teen creating a hub for intellectual rockstars like Galileo. Cesi brought together brilliant minds to observe, experiment, and share knowledge, pushing science forward in an age of dogma. He literally invented the concept of a 'scientist' as a collaborative, curious mind. Talk about being ahead of your time, and doing it all before your twenties were even in full swing!
