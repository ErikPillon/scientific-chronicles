Before James Hargreaves, spinning yarn was a slow, laborious, single-thread affair, making textile production a bottleneck. Then, this British weaver, with a stroke of genius (or perhaps just watching a fallen spinning wheel), invented the Spinning Jenny in the 1760s. Suddenly, one person could spin multiple threads at once, dramatically increasing productivity and kicking off a textile revolution! While some traditional weavers weren't thrilled about the competition and even destroyed his machines, Hargreaves' invention helped lay the groundwork for the factory system and mass production. He might not have worn a lab coat, but he engineered a massive leap forward in industrial production, transforming the world one thread at a time. A true game-changer in the history of making stuff!
