D.D. Palmer, originally a grocer and magnetic healer from rural Iowa, stumbled upon what he believed was the root cause of many ailments: misaligned spines. In 1895, he performed the first chiropractic adjustment on a deaf janitor, Harvey Lillard, claiming to restore his hearing. Whether Lillard's hearing truly returned is debated, but Palmer's idea ignited a medical revolution, much to the chagrin of established doctors. He founded the Palmer School of Chiropractic, kickstarting a movement that's still adjusting spines (and opinions) today.
