Before craft breweries and artisanal sodas were a thing, there was Charles E. Hires. A Philadelphia pharmacist with a knack for making things bubbly, Hires introduced root beer to the masses. Legend has it he discovered a delicious herbal tea while on his honeymoon and decided to turn it into a commercial success. He marketed it as 'Hires Root Beer' in 1876, first as a dry concentrate for mixing at home, then later as a bottled beverage. This guy basically invented the concept of chilling out with a frosty, non-alcoholic brew long before anyone knew what 'chilling' meant. Thanks to him, countless childhoods had a sweet, foamy soundtrack.
