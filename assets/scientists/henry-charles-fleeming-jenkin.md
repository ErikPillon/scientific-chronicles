Henry Charles Fleeming Jenkin was one of those brilliant Victorian minds who laid the literal groundwork for our connected world. A true polymath, he wasn't content just being an electrical engineer; he dabbled in civil engineering, naval architecture, and even sanitary engineering! Jenkin's most impactful work, however, was on submarine telegraph cables. He was absolutely crucial in designing, testing, and laying the transatlantic cables that finally linked continents, making global communication a reality. He also helped define the fundamental electrical units of ohm, volt, and ampere for the British Association, standardizing the language of electricity. Jenkin's clever solutions to signal loss and cable insulation were pure genius, making sure that messages sent across the deep blue sea actually arrived intelligibly. He made sure the world could finally have a decent chat!
