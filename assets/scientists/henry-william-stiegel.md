Before 'Made in America' was a slogan, there was Henry William Stiegel! This German-born industrialist was a powerhouse in colonial Pennsylvania, bringing cutting-edge European metallurgy and glassmaking techniques to the New World. He established furnaces that produced high-quality cast iron goods and, more famously, founded the Stiegel Glassworks, creating exquisitely designed and colored glass that rivaled European imports. He was an innovator who pushed the boundaries of manufacturing in nascent America, laying groundwork for future industrialization, even if his grand ambitions eventually led to personal bankruptcy. A visionary, nonetheless!
