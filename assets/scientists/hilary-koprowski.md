If you're a fan of not getting polio, you owe a big thanks to Hilary Koprowski! Born in Warsaw in 1916, this brilliant virologist wasn't content just studying viruses; he wanted to beat them. While many know of Jonas Salk's injected polio vaccine, it was Koprowski who, in the 1950s, developed the first oral live-attenuated vaccine. Yep, that's right, a few drops on a sugar cube, and kids were protected from the dreaded disease. His work, often overshadowed but no less crucial, was instrumental in mass vaccination campaigns, especially in the developing world, making polio eradication a real possibility. He spent his career battling infectious diseases, from rabies to Ebola, proving that sometimes, the biggest breakthroughs come in tiny, ingestible doses. He definitely left the world a healthier place!
