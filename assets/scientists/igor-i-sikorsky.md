Igor I. Sikorsky, born in Kyiv in 1889, was a true titan of the skies. Before he even hit 25, he was already building multi-engine airplanes in Russia, including the world's first four-engine aircraft, the 'Russky Vityaz,' and the incredible 'Ilya Muromets' bombers that saw action in WWI. After the Russian Revolution, he immigrated to the U.S. and founded the Sikorsky Aircraft Corporation, which would eventually become synonymous with helicopters. His VS-300, first flown in 1939, and the subsequent R-4, were the practical prototypes that launched the helicopter industry. Sikorsky didn't just dream of flying; he built the machines that made the impossible happen, changing warfare, transportation, and rescue forever.
