You know that unit of power called the 'watt'? Well, it's named after *this* guy! James Watt, a brilliant Scottish inventor, basically supercharged the Industrial Revolution. While he didn't invent the steam engine, his clever design improvements – like the separate condenser – dramatically increased its efficiency and power. Suddenly, factories could run anywhere, not just next to rivers, and industries like textiles and mining exploded. He was the engineering genius who made engines practical, affordable, and powerful enough to change the world forever. So, next time your hairdryer is humming, remember the master who gave us the power to power it!
