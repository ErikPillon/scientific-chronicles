Meet Christiaan Barnard, the South African surgeon who had the nerve (and the skill!) to give someone a brand new ticker. On December 3, 1967, he made history by performing the world's first successful human-to-human heart transplant on Louis Washkansky. Washkansky lived for 18 days, but it was a monumental leap forward, proving that such an audacious feat was possible. Barnard was a rockstar doctor, known for his flamboyant personality almost as much as his surgical prowess. He opened up a whole new frontier in medicine, even if the early years were a roller coaster of triumph and tragedy. He truly had a heart for pushing boundaries, literally.
