Before batteries were cool, there was Georg von Kleist. This German cleric and natural philosopher, working independently of Pieter van Musschenbroek, accidentally (or brilliantly, depending on who you ask!) invented the Leyden jar in 1745. Imagine holding a bottle that could store enough zap to give you a serious jolt – that was Kleist's creation, the first true capacitor! His discovery was a shocker (pun intended!) that kickstarted serious research into electricity, making him an unsung hero of early electrical science.
