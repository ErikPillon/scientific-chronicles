Edred John Henry Corner, affectionately known as "E.J.H.," was a legendary British botanist who loved nothing more than the steamy, mysterious jungles of the tropics. His work on tropical plants, particularly fungi and palms, was monumental. He was famous for his "Durian Theory" of tree form, proposing that the shape of trees evolved from a primitive durian-like ancestor, which, let's be honest, sounds way cooler than your average botanical theory. From the Malaysian jungles to the Cambridge professorship, Corner was a dedicated explorer of biodiversity, leaving behind a colossal legacy of plant knowledge. He truly understood how to branch out in science!
