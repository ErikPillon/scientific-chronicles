Harold Masursky was basically the Moon's real estate agent, and then Mars's, and then Venus's! This American geologist at the U.S. Geological Survey was the go-to guy for choosing landing sites for NASA's most ambitious missions. He was crucial in picking where the Apollo astronauts would take their first steps and later helped guide probes to Mars (Viking) and Venus (Magellan). His deep understanding of planetary surfaces made him indispensable, ensuring that humanity's robotic and human explorers landed somewhere interesting and, crucially, safe. Masursky wasn't just mapping rocks; he was mapping humanity's future in space.
