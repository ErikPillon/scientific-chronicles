What's hiding beneath our feet, deep inside the Earth? Andrija Mohorovicic, the sharp Croatian seismologist and meteorologist, found a crucial part of the answer! Born in 1857, 'Mohorovičić' might be a mouthful, but his discovery is fundamental to geology. After observing a 1909 earthquake, he noticed that seismic waves sped up at a certain depth. His brilliant conclusion? There's a distinct boundary, now famously called the 'Moho,' where the Earth's crust transitions into the denser mantle. Before him, we largely guessed. After him, we had a map of Earth's internal layers. Mohorovičić literally gave us an X-ray vision of our planet's hidden structure, making him the ultimate ground-breaker!
