Anna Botsford Comstock was like the coolest nature teacher you never had, but totally wished you did! This American entomologist, artist, and educator revolutionized how we learn about the natural world. Instead of just dissecting bugs in a lab, Anna championed 'nature study,' urging kids (and adults!) to step outside and observe the wonders around them. She was a prolific illustrator, sketching her insect observations with incredible detail, and a tireless advocate for bringing science to the masses, especially through rural schools. A true trailblazer, Anna Comstock made sure that the buzzing, creeping, and growing things of the world got their moment in the educational spotlight.
