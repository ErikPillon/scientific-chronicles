Henry Darcy was a French engineer who probably didn't realize he'd become the patron saint of coffee filtration and oil extraction, but here we are! Born in Dijon, Darcy was a civil engineering superstar who focused on water supply. His most famous contribution, Darcy's Law, describes the flow of fluids through porous materials, like water through sand or oil through rock. He literally gave us the mathematical basis for understanding how groundwater moves and how to design municipal water systems. His experiments in Dijon's public fountains might seem quaint today, but they laid the bedrock for entire fields like hydrogeology and petroleum engineering. Next time you're enjoying a well-filtered coffee or pumping gas, give a silent nod to Monsieur Darcy!
