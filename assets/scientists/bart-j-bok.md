Bart J. Bok, a lively Dutch-American astronomer, had a knack for finding the cosmic nurseries where stars are born. He's best known for identifying "Bok globules," small, dark, dense clouds of interstellar gas and dust that are gravitationally collapsing to form new stars and planetary systems. It was a bit controversial at first, but his observations and theories proved correct, shining a light (or rather, a darkness) on a fundamental process in the universe. Bok was also a passionate popularizer of astronomy, making the wonders of the cosmos accessible to everyone, not just fellow scientists. He helped us understand where we all came from, star-stuff and all.
