Meet George Peacock, a 19th-century brainiac who wasn't content with the old-school way of doing math. This Cambridge don basically spearheaded a revolution in how mathematics was taught and understood in Britain. He co-founded the Analytical Society, pushing for a more continental, analytical approach to calculus, shaking off the Newtonian shackles. He also championed symbolic algebra, where symbols could represent anything, not just numbers. Think of him as the guy who made math a bit more abstract, a bit more flexible, and a whole lot more modern. Not just a pretty mind, he was also Dean of Ely Cathedral – talk about a man of diverse interests!
