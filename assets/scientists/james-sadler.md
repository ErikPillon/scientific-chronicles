Before there were astronauts, there were aeronauts, and James Sadler was one of the bravest. This English pastry chef wasn't content with keeping his feet on the ground; he wanted to soar! In 1784, he became the first Englishman to pilot a hot-air balloon, igniting a national obsession with flight. He undertook numerous perilous voyages, pushing the boundaries of early aviation and even making the first crossing of the Bristol Channel by air. Sadler's airborne adventures paved the way for future aerial pioneers, proving that with enough hot air (and a bit of courage), anything is possible!
