Gordon Allport was the kind of psychologist who actually cared about individuals, not just statistical averages. A true pioneer in American psychology, he famously said, "If you want to know what a person is like, why not ask him?" which sounds simple but was revolutionary at the time! He championed the study of personality traits and was instrumental in developing humanistic psychology, moving away from purely behaviorist or psychoanalytic views. Allport wasn't just interested in what made us tick, but what made each of us uniquely us. His work on prejudice and rumor also remains shockingly relevant today. A real heavyweight in understanding the human mind.
