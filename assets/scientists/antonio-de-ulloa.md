Antonio de Ulloa was no ordinary Spanish naval officer; he was a full-blown polymath, ready to tackle anything from cartography to colonial governance. Best known for his epic journey to Peru in the mid-18th century as part of a French geodesic mission, he helped measure a degree of longitude at the equator – a crucial step in proving Earth's oblate spheroid shape. But wait, there's more! He also chronicled the natural history and ethnography of South America, published influential scientific works, and even served as the first Spanish governor of Louisiana. Ulloa literally sailed the world, measured the Earth, and governed a colony. Talk about a resume!
