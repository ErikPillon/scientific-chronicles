Ernst von Bergmann was the German surgeon who said 'enough with the germs!' and brought antiseptic practices into the operating room. Long before him, surgery was a high-risk gamble, largely due to rampant infections. Building on Lister's work, von Bergmann wasn't just an early adopter; he was a fierce advocate and innovator, implementing strict aseptic techniques, including the sterilization of instruments, surgical gowns, and bandages. This dramatically reduced post-operative infections and saved countless lives. He was also a master surgeon in his own right, making groundbreaking advances in brain and abdominal surgery. Essentially, he helped transform surgery from a dangerous last resort into a much safer, more precise medical art. So, if you've ever had a successful surgery, you owe a debt to Bergmann for making operating rooms a much cleaner, more life-saving place.
