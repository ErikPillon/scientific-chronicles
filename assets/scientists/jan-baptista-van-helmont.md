Jan Baptista van Helmont was a 17th-century polymath who basically invented the idea of 'gas' (yes, *that* gas!). This Flemish physician and chemist was far ahead of his time, challenging ancient Greek ideas with experiments. His famous willow tree experiment proved that plants don't just eat soil, a radical idea back then. He also distinguished different types of 'air,' coining the term 'gas' for substances like carbon dioxide. While he had some quirky ideas (like spontaneous generation of mice from dirty shirts), his rigorous experimental approach and insights into chemistry and physiology make him a true pioneer. He really put the 'air' into scientific inquiry!
