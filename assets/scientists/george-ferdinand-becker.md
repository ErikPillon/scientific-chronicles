Forget Indiana Jones, meet George Ferdinand Becker, the actual rockstar (pun intended!) of American geology. This brainy geologist didn't just collect rocks; he crunched numbers. He was one of the first to apply rigorous mathematical and physical principles to geological problems, from calculating the cooling of the Earth to understanding volcanic eruptions and the formation of ore deposits. He worked for the U.S. Geological Survey for decades, even dabbling in earthquake studies and mining geology. Becker was the kind of guy who saw the Earth not just as dirt and rocks, but as a giant, complex physics problem waiting to be solved. Smart, right?
