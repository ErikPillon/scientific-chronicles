Henri Poincaré was a French intellectual titan, a true 'last universalist' who tackled mathematics, physics, and even philosophy with unmatched brilliance. Before Einstein's special relativity, Poincaré was already thinking about the relativity of space and time, the constancy of the speed of light, and the Lorentz transformations. His work on the three-body problem laid the groundwork for chaos theory, proving that even seemingly simple systems could produce unpredictable behavior. He was a pioneer in algebraic topology, celestial mechanics, and electrodynamics, basically shaping the intellectual landscape of early 20th-century science. Poincaré was the kind of genius who made everyone else feel a bit slow, a true giant whose insights echo through science even today. He had a brain that was truly out of this world!
