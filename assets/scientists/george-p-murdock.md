George P. Murdock was the ultimate data detective of human culture! This American anthropologist made it his mission to systematically compare societies worldwide, creating massive databases like the Human Relations Area Files (HRAF). Think of it as an early, analog version of "big data" for cultures. By organizing ethnographic information from hundreds of societies, Murdock helped scholars identify universal patterns in human behavior, social structures, and cultural practices. He basically built the ultimate cheat sheet for understanding humanity's diverse ways, proving that even social sciences can be rigorously empirical and incredibly insightful. He made comparative anthropology truly scientific!
