Garrett Augustus Morgan was an ingenious African American inventor who made daily life safer for millions, even if you've never heard his name! Born in Kentucky, Morgan moved to Cleveland and became a successful businessman. But his real knack was for problem-solving that genuinely benefited the public. In 1914, he patented a "safety hood" (an early gas mask) after witnessing a tragic factory fire, famously using it himself to rescue workers from a tunnel collapse in 1916. Then, after seeing a terrible car crash, he designed and patented a three-position traffic signal in 1923 – the precursor to the modern traffic light, dramatically improving road safety. Morgan’s innovations were not only groundbreaking but life-saving, showcasing a brilliant mind dedicated to practical solutions for a rapidly industrializing world, all while overcoming significant societal obstacles.
