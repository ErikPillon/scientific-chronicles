Before COVID-19 made ventilators household names, there was Forrest M. Bird, the legendary aviator and biomedical engineer who essentially invented the modern respirator. After serving as a military pilot and understanding the challenges of breathing at high altitudes, Bird applied his engineering genius to create devices that could literally breathe for people. From the revolutionary Bird Mark 7 medical respirator to pediatric ventilators, his inventions dramatically improved patient care and saved countless lives, often using principles he learned from aircraft superchargers. Talk about a breath of fresh air for medical technology – his innovations were truly transformative!
