Meet Iosif Samuilovich Shklovskii, the Soviet astrophysicist who wasn't afraid to think big and bold. Born in 1916, Shklovskii was a theoretical powerhouse, making groundbreaking contributions to radio astronomy and high-energy astrophysics. He's famous for predicting the synchrotron radiation from the Crab Nebula, which was later confirmed, and for his early work on the nature of pulsars and X-ray stars. Shklovskii also proposed the idea of a 'cosmic background' radiation, laying some groundwork for later CMB discoveries. He was a brilliant mind, known for his unconventional thinking and strong opinions, which sometimes put him at odds with the establishment. His book 'Cosmic Radio Waves' became a classic. Essentially, if there was a mysterious glow or a strange signal from space, Shklovskii was probably among the first to figure out what was going on. He even dabbled in the search for extraterrestrial intelligence, because why not?
