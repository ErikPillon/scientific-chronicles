Long before 'eco-friendly' was a hashtag, there was George Perkins Marsh, a true OG environmentalist. This American diplomat, scholar, and polyglot dropped a bombshell in 1864 with his book 'Man and Nature; or, Physical Geography as Modified by Human Action.' Marsh didn't just observe; he meticulously documented how human activities—like deforestation and poor agricultural practices—were messing with the planet's delicate balance, impacting everything from soil to climate. He basically told everyone, 'Hey, we're not just visitors here; we're changing things, and not always for the better!' His work was a wake-up call, inspiring Teddy Roosevelt and setting the stage for modern conservation. What a visionary!
