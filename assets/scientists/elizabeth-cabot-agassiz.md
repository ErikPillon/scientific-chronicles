Elizabeth Cabot Agassiz was far more than just the wife of a famous scientist; she was a formidable mind in her own right. A born educator, she ran a school for girls that later evolved into the Harvard Annex, which we now know as Radcliffe College – and guess who became its first president? Yep, Elizabeth! She also wasn't afraid to get her hands dirty, accompanying her zoologist husband, Louis Agassiz, on epic scientific expeditions, chronicling their adventures and observations. From exploring the Amazon to studying marine life off the coast of Massachusetts, she proved that an inquiring mind and a sharp pen were just as important as a fancy scientific degree. She truly believed in accessible education for women, making sure they too could dive deep into the world of knowledge, literally and figuratively.
