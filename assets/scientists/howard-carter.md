Howard Carter wasn't your average guy with a shovel. This British archaeologist spent years digging through the scorching sands of Egypt, driven by an unshakeable belief that the tomb of the 'boy king' Tutankhamun lay hidden somewhere in the Valley of the Kings. And oh boy, was he right! In 1922, after a decade of searching and on the brink of despair, he found it – a virtually untouched pharaoh's tomb, overflowing with gold and treasures. His famous exchange with Lord Carnarvon when he first peered into the antechamber – 'Can you see anything?' 'Yes, wonderful things!' – launched a global sensation, single-handedly reviving the world's fascination with ancient Egypt and cementing his place in history as the ultimate treasure hunter.
