George Dobson was the atmospheric detective who basically put ozone on the scientific map. Before anyone worried about ozone holes, Dobson was meticulously measuring the amount of ozone in Earth's atmosphere using instruments he designed himself – the famous Dobson spectrophotometer. His long-term data series, starting in the 1920s, became the baseline for all future ozone studies. When scientists later discovered the ozone hole, it was Dobson's historical measurements that confirmed a dramatic depletion. He basically gave us the tool and the historical context to understand one of the biggest environmental challenges of our time.
