Hazel Bishop wasn't just about looking good; she was about making beauty *work*. This brilliant organic chemist, after years of working in dermatology labs, got fed up with lipstick that smeared and faded into oblivion. So, she did what any good scientist would: she invented a better one! In the late 1940s, Hazel created the first 'kiss-proof' and 'smear-proof' lipstick. Her company, Hazel Bishop Inc., took off like a rocket, proving that a smart woman with a chemistry degree could conquer the cosmetics world and help millions of women keep their pout perfect, no matter what! She truly empowered women with lasting color.
