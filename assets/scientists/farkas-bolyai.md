Farkas Bolyai, a Hungarian mathematical maestro, was more than just a brilliant mind; he was the proud papa of János Bolyai, one of the pioneers of non-Euclidean geometry. While he dabbled in parallel postulates himself, his greatest contribution might have been his relentless encouragement (and sometimes exasperation) with his son's revolutionary ideas. He spent years trying to prove Euclid's fifth postulate, only to eventually accept its unprovability and advise his son, "for God's sake, I beseech you, give it up!" — advice János, thankfully, ignored to the benefit of all mathematics.
