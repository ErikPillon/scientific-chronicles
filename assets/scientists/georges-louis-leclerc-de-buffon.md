Georges-Louis Leclerc, Comte de Buffon, was basically the Wikipedia of the 18th century, but way more elegant and bound in multiple volumes! This brilliant French naturalist and mathematician set out to compile *Histoire Naturelle, générale et particulière*, a sprawling 36-volume (eventually 44!) encyclopedia intended to encompass all known natural history. Talk about ambitious! He was a trailblazer in thinking about the Earth's age (way older than anyone thought), the variability of species, and even hinted at concepts resembling evolution long before Darwin. His work challenged prevailing dogmas and introduced ideas about environmental influence on life. Buffon wasn't just a scientist; he was also a masterful writer, famously quipping "Style is the man himself." His grand narrative approach to science made him incredibly popular, inspiring generations and proving that you could be both rigorous and utterly captivating.
