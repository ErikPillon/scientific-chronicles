If you've ever taken a psychological test, you probably owe a quiet nod to Anne Anastasi. This American psychologist, often called the 'high priestess of psychological testing,' was *the* expert on psychometrics – the science of measuring mental abilities and processes. Anastasi spent her career ensuring that psychological tests were not just interesting, but rigorously valid and reliable. She argued fiercely against the misuse of tests, emphasizing the importance of context and environment in interpreting scores. Her influential textbook, 'Psychological Testing,' became a standard for generations. Anne was a master at turning fuzzy psychological concepts into measurable data, making her a true champion of scientific rigor in the study of the mind.
