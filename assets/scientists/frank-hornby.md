Frank Hornby was the absolute king of making learning fun, long before STEM was a buzzword. This British inventor gifted the world with Meccano, a revolutionary construction toy that allowed kids (and adults!) to build intricate machines using reusable metal parts, nuts, and bolts. He didn't stop there; he also created Hornby Model Railways, sparking a lifelong passion for trains in countless enthusiasts. Hornby wasn't just selling toys; he was subtly teaching mechanical principles, problem-solving, and engineering skills, disguised as playtime. He believed in the power of hands-on creativity, and his timeless inventions continue to inspire budding engineers and dreamers to this day. Talk about a playful genius!
