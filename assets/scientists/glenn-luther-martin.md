Forget humble beginnings, Glenn L. Martin started his aviation empire in an abandoned church – talk about a divine calling to the skies! This American pioneer was flying his own designs by 1909 and went on to found one of the most significant aircraft manufacturing companies, which eventually morphed into Lockheed Martin. From early biplanes to groundbreaking bombers, Martin's vision for flight was immense, literally building the wings that would carry countless innovations and define an era of aerospace manufacturing. He showed that with enough ingenuity, you could build an aviation powerhouse anywhere.
