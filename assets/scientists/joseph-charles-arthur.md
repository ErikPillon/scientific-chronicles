J.C. Arthur, or Joseph Charles Arthur, was an American botanist and mycologist who practically wrote the book on rust fungi. No, seriously, he did! Rusts are pesky plant pathogens that cause significant agricultural damage, and Arthur spent decades meticulously studying, classifying, and understanding them. From his base at Purdue University, he became the world's foremost authority on Uredinales (the order of rust fungi), compiling monumental works that are still referenced today. Imagine dedicating your entire career to microscopic fungi that ruin crops – it takes immense dedication and an eagle eye! His work was crucial for plant pathology, helping farmers and scientists develop strategies to combat these destructive diseases. He might not have discovered a new element, but he saved a lot of wheat fields, which, let's be honest, is arguably just as important for humanity!
