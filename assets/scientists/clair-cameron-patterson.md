Clair Cameron Patterson was a geochemist with a serious mission: figure out how old Earth really is. In 1956, using meteorites (which preserve the solar system's original composition) and meticulous lead-uranium dating, he precisely calculated our planet's age to be 4.55 billion years. Boom! But Patterson wasn't content with just cosmic answers. He then turned his brilliant mind to a much more terrestrial problem: lead pollution. He discovered alarming levels of lead in the environment, largely due to leaded gasoline and industrial waste. For decades, he relentlessly battled corporate giants and government inertia, eventually playing a pivotal role in the global phase-out of leaded gasoline. A true scientific hero who used his intellect to benefit all of humanity, not just to solve abstract problems but to safeguard public health and the environment.
