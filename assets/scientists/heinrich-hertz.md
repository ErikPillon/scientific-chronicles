Remember when radios, TVs, and Wi-Fi were just wild ideas? Well, Heinrich Hertz basically made them possible! This brilliant German physicist, born in 1857, experimentally confirmed James Clerk Maxwell's theory of electromagnetic waves. Think about it: he proved that these invisible waves could travel through space, bounce off things, and generally do their thing, setting the stage for every wireless gadget you can't live without. Sadly, he left us too soon at 36, but his legacy? It's literally all around us, buzzing through the airwaves, making sure your cat videos load instantly. Not too shabby for a guy who just wanted to prove a theory!
