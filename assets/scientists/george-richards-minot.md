George Richards Minot was an American physician whose dietary discovery scored him a Nobel Prize and saved countless lives. Back in the day, pernicious anemia was a killer, untreatable and devastating. Minot, along with William P. Murphy and George H. Whipple, figured out that feeding patients raw liver (yes, you read that right!) could effectively reverse the condition. It turns out liver is packed with Vitamin B12, the missing ingredient. This wasn't just a quirky diet; it was a groundbreaking insight into nutrition and blood disorders, shifting medical understanding and turning a fatal diagnosis into a manageable one. Cheers to Minot for making liver less 'ick' and more 'life-saving'!
