Hermann Franz Moritz Kopp was the kind of chemist who wasn't just interested in what things *were*, but also *how* they behaved. This German polymath spent his career meticulously investigating the relationship between a substance's physical properties (like boiling point or density) and its chemical makeup, giving us 'Kopp's Law' concerning atomic heat capacity. But he wasn't just a lab coat wearer; Kopp was also a pioneering historian of chemistry, meticulously documenting the evolution of his field. He bridged the gap between empirical observation and historical understanding, proving that sometimes, to truly understand matter, you also need to understand its story.
