Friedrich Georg Wilhelm von Struve was basically the rockstar astronomer of his time, making a huge splash in both Germany and Russia. This celestial sleuth was obsessed with double stars, cataloging thousands of them with precision that would make any modern stargazer jealous. Not content with just observing, he also tackled the tricky business of stellar parallax, attempting to measure the distance to other stars. Oh, and he founded the magnificent Pulkovo Observatory near St. Petersburg, which quickly became a world-class center for astronomical research. His work laid foundational stones for astrophysics and geodesy, leaving an astronomical legacy that shines brightly.
