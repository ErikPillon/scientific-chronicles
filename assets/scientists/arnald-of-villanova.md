Arnald of Villanova was a true Renaissance man before the Renaissance even began! Hailing from the Crown of Aragon in the 13th and 14th centuries, he wasn't just a physician; he was also a prolific alchemist, astrologer, and theologian. He translated crucial Arabic medical texts into Latin, vastly enriching European medical knowledge, and was a pioneer in using distilled spirits for medicinal purposes (hello, early tinctures and elixirs!). His influence spread across Europe, treating popes and kings while advocating for experimental medicine. Basically, he was one of the big brains making medieval medicine a bit less medieval and a lot more scientific.
