Before smartphones and cloud contacts, there was the Rolodex, and Arnold Neustadter was its genius inventor. In 1956, this American entrepreneur brought his brilliant rotating card file to the world, making it ridiculously easy to find and organize business contacts. No more fumbling through stacks of business cards or trying to decipher scribbled notes! The Rolodex became a desk staple in offices everywhere, a symbol of mid-century efficiency, and an icon of pre-digital information management. So, while not a 'scientist' in the traditional sense, Neustadter's creation was a technological marvel of its time, solving a common problem with elegant simplicity. He made finding that important contact a literal flick of the wrist.
