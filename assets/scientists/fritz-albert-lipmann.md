If cellular energy was a bustling city, Fritz Albert Lipmann was the urban planner who mapped its power grid. This German-American biochemist, shared a Nobel Prize in 1953 for discovering Coenzyme A (CoA), the unsung hero that helps cells turn food into energy. He also clarified the central role of ATP (adenosine triphosphate) as the universal energy currency, proving that it wasn't just a random molecule but the absolute workhorse of all biological reactions. Basically, he figured out how your body fuels everything from thinking to wiggling your toes. Pretty fundamental stuff!
