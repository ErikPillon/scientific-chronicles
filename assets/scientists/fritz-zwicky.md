Fritz Zwicky was the Swiss astronomer who was so ahead of his time, the scientific community barely caught up decades later. In the 1930s, this eccentric genius at Caltech not only coined the term 'dark matter' to explain the missing mass in galaxy clusters but also predicted the existence of neutron stars and that supernovae could produce cosmic rays. He was a force of nature, known for his unconventional thinking and sometimes abrasive personality, but his bold predictions were eventually proven right. He saw the universe's weirdest secrets long before anyone else dared to look. What a maverick!
