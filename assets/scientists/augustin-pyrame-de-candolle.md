Meet Augustin Pyrame de Candolle, the Swiss botanical titan who basically organized the entire plant world! Born in Geneva, he took on the monumental task of classifying plants when botany was still a bit of a wild garden. He developed one of the first natural systems of plant classification, moving beyond simply how plants *looked* to focus on their true botanical relationships. His multi-volume work, "Prodromus Systematis Naturalis Regni Vegetabilis," was like the Google Maps of the plant kingdom, helping future botanists navigate its complexities. He was also an early proponent of biogeography, realizing that where plants grew told a story. So, next time you see a beautifully organized botanical garden, tip your hat to de Candolle – he made sure every plant had its proper place!
