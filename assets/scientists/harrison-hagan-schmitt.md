Meet Harrison "Jack" Schmitt, the ultimate field geologist who took his hammer to the Moon! As a trained geologist, Schmitt wasn't your typical test pilot astronaut; he was chosen for Apollo 17 specifically for his scientific expertise. In December 1972, he became the only scientist to ever walk on the lunar surface, directly applying his geological skills to collect samples and make observations. Imagine being the first (and so far, only!) geologist to actually touch and study another celestial body in person. He basically took Earth science and launched it into space, proving that a good rock hammer and keen eye are useful anywhere, even 380,000 kilometers away!
