Charles Atwood Kofoid was the kind of scientist who loved the tiny, unseen world. This American zoologist spent his career peering into microscopes, becoming a leading authority on protozoa – those fascinating single-celled organisms that play huge roles in ecosystems. He was a giant in marine biology, especially for his extensive research on plankton, the microscopic drifters of the oceans, contributing significantly to our understanding of ocean food webs. Kofoid also delved into parasitic protozoa, shedding light on their life cycles and impact on human health, proving that sometimes, the smallest creatures hold the biggest scientific stories.
