Before David Wesson, cottonseed oil was pretty much a waste product. But this American chemist, with a knack for turning trash into treasure, developed a process to refine it into an edible, shelf-stable cooking oil. Yep, we're talking about the Wesson Oil you still see in stores! He worked for the Southern Cotton Oil Company and perfected the hydrogenation process, making fats more versatile and less prone to spoilage. This wasn't just about a cooking ingredient; it was a major step in food science, extending food lifespans and making affordable cooking fats accessible to everyone. He made sure our fries were crispier and our baking more reliable. A true unsung hero of the American kitchen!
