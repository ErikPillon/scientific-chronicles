Christoph Clavius was the unsung hero who fixed our calendar! This German Jesuit astronomer and mathematician was the mastermind behind the Gregorian calendar reform of 1582. Before him, the Julian calendar had drifted significantly, causing headaches for everyone trying to track religious holidays (and, well, just the date!). Clavius, with his brilliant calculations, helped Pope Gregory XIII implement the necessary adjustments, aligning the calendar with the solar year and giving us the system we still use today. He was also an influential educator, writing widely used mathematics textbooks and corresponding with Galileo. So, next time your phone correctly shows the date, you can thank Clavius for making sure we're all on the same page – literally!
