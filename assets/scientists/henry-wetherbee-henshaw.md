Henry Wetherbee Henshaw was a true renaissance man of American science, with a knack for observing both feathers and fascinating cultures! This intrepid ornithologist started his career mapping and studying birds across the American West, often in challenging conditions. His meticulous documentation of avian life made him a leading figure in the burgeoning field of ornithology in the late 19th century. But his curiosity didn't stop at birds; he also delved into ethnology, recording the languages and traditions of Native American tribes. From the skies to ancient customs, Henshaw was always broadening our understanding of the natural and human world.
