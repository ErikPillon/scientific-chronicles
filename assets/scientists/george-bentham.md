George Bentham was a botanical powerhouse who basically wrote the book on how to organize plants in the 19th century. Along with his partner-in-plants, Joseph Dalton Hooker, he produced the monumental 'Genera Plantarum,' classifying thousands of plant species based on their natural relationships – a massive upgrade from earlier, more artificial systems. He was a meticulous observer and illustrator, describing countless species, particularly from Australia. Without his diligent work, our understanding of plant biodiversity and evolution would be far messier. He truly brought order to the botanical kingdom.
