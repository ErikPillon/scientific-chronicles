Meet Alcuin, the OG intellectual heavyweight who essentially ran Charlemagne's brain trust in the 8th century. Hailing from York, this scholar-monk crossed the Channel and became the chief architect of the Carolingian Renaissance. He wasn't discovering new planets, but he *was* busy revitalizing education, standardizing handwriting (hello, Carolingian minuscule!), and basically saving classical knowledge from fading into oblivion. He whipped up new curricula, founded schools, and made sure folks remembered how to read, write, and think about arithmetic, geometry, music, and astronomy – the foundational 'sciences' of his day. Without Alcuin, medieval Europe might have been a lot dimmer. He proved that sometimes, the biggest scientific impact comes from organizing knowledge, not just discovering it.
