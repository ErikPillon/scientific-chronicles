James Hall Jr. was to New York geology what a keen detective is to a cold case – he meticulously uncovered its deep, ancient secrets! For over 60 years, this American polymath served as the State Geologist of New York, compiling monumental works on its geology and paleontology. His detailed publications, especially his multi-volume 'Paleontology of New York,' became a bedrock for understanding North American geological history, providing a systematic way to map and interpret rock layers based on their fossil content. He might not have discovered dinosaurs in his backyard, but he certainly put New York's ancient past on the scientific map with astonishing detail and dedication. He was a true geological giant whose work resonates with earth scientists even today.
