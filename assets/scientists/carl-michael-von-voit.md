Carl von Voit was the OG nutritionist, a German physiologist who basically gave us the scientific lowdown on what happens when we eat. Forget fads and diets based on guesswork; Voit was all about hard data. He used calorimeters and detailed analyses of food intake and waste products to figure out how our bodies process proteins, fats, and carbohydrates. He studied the metabolism of humans and animals with meticulous precision, establishing fundamental principles of energy balance and nutrient requirements. His research in Munich attracted a legion of students and researchers, making his laboratory a global hub for nutritional science. Voit’s work wasn't just about telling you what to eat; it was about understanding the complex chemical factory inside us. He literally weighed, measured, and calculated his way to laying the foundation for modern dietary guidelines. He's why we talk about calories and macronutrients today!
