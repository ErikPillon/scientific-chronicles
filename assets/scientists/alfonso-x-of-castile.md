Alfonso X, often dubbed "the Wise," was more than just a king; he was a medieval intellectual powerhouse who turned his court into a scientific hub. Ruling Castile in the 13th century, he was obsessed with knowledge, especially astronomy. His biggest hit? The Alfonsine Tables, a comprehensive set of astronomical tables that became the gold standard for predicting planetary positions and eclipses for centuries. These weren't just some doodles; they were a massive collaborative effort involving Christian, Jewish, and Muslim scholars, showcasing his incredible open-mindedness for the era. He funded translations of Arabic and Hebrew scientific texts into Castilian, kickstarting the flow of ancient and Islamic wisdom into Europe. A king who preferred stars to skirmishes, Alfonso X proved that even monarchs could be total science nerds, leaving a legacy that outshone any crown.
