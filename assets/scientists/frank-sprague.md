Frank Sprague was the kind of electrical engineer who literally got the world moving. This American naval officer-turned-inventor was a visionary in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, earning him the title 'Father of Electric Traction.' He didn't just tinker with electricity; he fundamentally transformed urban transportation. Sprague developed the first successful electric streetcar system in Richmond, Virginia, proving electric power could be reliable and efficient for mass transit. But he didn't stop there! He also invented the multiple-unit control system for trains and electric elevators that could automatically stop at any floor. Essentially, if you've ever ridden a subway or a lift, you're experiencing Frank Sprague's genius. He made cities vertical and horizontal, electrifying daily life!
