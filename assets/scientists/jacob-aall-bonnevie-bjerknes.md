Jacob Aall Bonnevie Bjerknes, or 'J.B.' as he was known, was a meteorology rockstar who basically put the 'front' in weather fronts. He was a key player in the Bergen School of Meteorology, developing the groundbreaking polar front theory during WWI, which explained the dynamics of weather systems as interactions between warm and cold air masses. Later, in the U.S., he made huge strides in understanding large-scale atmospheric and oceanic phenomena, famously connecting the El Niño Southern Oscillation (ENSO) to global weather patterns. His work laid the foundation for modern weather forecasting and climate science. Talk about making a lasting impact on how we understand the sky!
