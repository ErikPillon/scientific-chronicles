Who says you can't be a doctor, an engineer, *and* an astronaut? Mae C. Jemison laughed in the face of such limits! Born in Alabama and raised in Chicago, this brilliant polymath not only earned degrees in chemical engineering and African American studies but also became a medical doctor. Then, in 1992, she blasted off aboard the Space Shuttle Endeavour, making history as the first African-American woman to orbit Earth. Talk about reaching for the stars! Jemison isn't just a pioneer in space; she's a fierce advocate for science literacy and diversity, proving that intelligence, determination, and a healthy dose of curiosity can take you absolutely anywhere. She's living proof that the sky isn't the limit!
