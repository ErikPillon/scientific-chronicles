Emmett J. Culligan was a man who couldn't stand hard water, and thank goodness for that! This ingenious American inventor and entrepreneur revolutionized home water treatment. After experimenting with various minerals, he developed an effective and affordable water softener for residential use in the 1920s. In 1936, he launched Culligan Zeolite Company (later just Culligan), with a brilliant business model: renting out water softeners and exchanging exhausted units for regenerated ones. Suddenly, soft water wasn't just for fancy hotels; it was available for every household, leading to cleaner dishes, softer laundry, and less scale in pipes. His catchphrase 'Hey, Culligan Man!' became iconic. Culligan didn't just invent a product; he created an entire service industry that became synonymous with better water.
