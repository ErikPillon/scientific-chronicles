Forget Indiana Jones; Carl Blegen was the real deal when it came to uncovering ancient secrets! Born in Minnesota in 1887, this American archaeologist became a titan in the world of classical archaeology. He's most famous for his meticulous excavations at Troy, where he meticulously confirmed the various layers of the city, supporting the historical reality behind Homer's epic tales. But his digging didn't stop there! Blegen also led the excavation of the Mycenaean palace at Pylos in Greece, where he unearthed thousands of clay tablets inscribed with Linear B script. This discovery was a massive breakthrough, confirming the Mycenaean civilization as the earliest Greek-speaking culture. Blegen wasn't just finding old pots; he was rewriting ancient history with his trowel and dedication, bringing the Bronze Age to vibrant life!
