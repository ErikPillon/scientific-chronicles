Hermann Günther Grassmann was the ultimate intellectual juggler, excelling in so many fields you'd think he had a whole team working under his name. This German polymath made revolutionary contributions to mathematics, laying the groundwork for what we now know as vector spaces, linear algebra, and exterior algebra – concepts that are absolutely fundamental in modern physics and computer graphics. But that's not all! He was also a respected physicist, linguist (developing new theories on phonetics and Sanskrit grammar), and even a philosopher. His work was so far ahead of its time that it wasn't fully appreciated until decades after his death. Grassmann proved that sometimes, being a genius means your peers might need a little time to catch up to your vision.
