Georg von Peurbach was basically the rockstar astronomer of the 15th century, shining a light on the heavens when Europe was still figuring things out. This Austrian genius taught mathematics and astronomy, meticulously observing eclipses and planetary positions. He wrote influential texts like the "Theoricae novae planetarum" which simplified Ptolemaic planetary theory. His precision and dedication laid crucial groundwork for Copernicus and Kepler, making him a vital bridge between ancient and modern astronomy. Talk about a star performer!
