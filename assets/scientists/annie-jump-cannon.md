Ever wondered how astronomers categorize stars? Thank Annie Jump Cannon! This American astronomer was a true star whisperer, spending decades at Harvard College Observatory where she classified an astonishing 350,000 stars by hand – yes, by hand! She was part of the famous 'Harvard Computers,' a group of women who processed astronomical data. Annie developed and refined the stellar classification system (O, B, A, F, G, K, M – remember that mnemonic?) that’s still the global standard. Her meticulous work helped astronomers understand stellar evolution and composition, proving that sometimes, the most groundbreaking discoveries come from simply looking, learning, and classifying, one star at a time.
