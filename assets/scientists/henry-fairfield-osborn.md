Henry Fairfield Osborn (1857-1935) was a big name in early 20th-century American science, running the American Museum of Natural History for a quarter-century and discovering/naming iconic dinosaurs like *Tyrannosaurus rex* and *Velociraptor*. He was a powerhouse in paleontology and evolutionary biology, shaping public understanding of prehistoric life. However, his story has a serious asterisk: he was also a vocal proponent of eugenics, a now discredited and harmful ideology. So, while his scientific contributions to paleontology were monumental, his social and political views serve as a stark reminder that scientific brilliance doesn't always equal moral rectitude. A complex figure, indeed.
