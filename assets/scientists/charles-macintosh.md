Meet Charles Macintosh, the Scottish chemist who, tired of getting soaked, decided to do something about it. Born in 1766, Charles fiddled with chemicals until he discovered a way to bond rubber with fabric, creating the world's first truly waterproof material in 1823. Presto! The 'mackintosh' raincoat was born, saving countless fashionistas and everyday folks from soggy despair. So next time you're dry during a downpour, tip your hat to Charles. He literally rubber-stamped his place in history, making rainy days a bit less miserable for everyone. What a guy!
