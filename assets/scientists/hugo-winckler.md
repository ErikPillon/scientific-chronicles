Hugo Winckler was the real-life Indiana Jones of ancient Anatolia! This German archaeologist and philologist struck gold (or rather, clay tablets) when he uncovered the capital city of the mighty Hittite Empire, Hattusa, in modern-day Turkey. Before Winckler, the Hittites were largely a biblical footnote, but his excavations from 1906 onward brought this powerful Bronze Age civilization vividly back to life. He discovered thousands of cuneiform tablets, including the famous treaty between the Hittites and the Egyptians – basically, ancient international law! It was a monumental discovery that rewrote the history books of the Near East. Thanks to Winckler, we now know that the Hittites were a major player, not just a passing mention. Talk about digging up history!
