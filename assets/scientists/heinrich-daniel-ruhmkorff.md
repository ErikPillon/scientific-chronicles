Heinrich Daniel Ruhmkorff was a German instrument maker whose name became synonymous with high-voltage electricity in the 19th century. He didn't invent the induction coil, but he refined it to such an impressive degree that his 'Ruhmkorff coil' became *the* go-to device for generating powerful sparks and high voltages. These coils were vital for early experiments in electricity, magnetism, vacuum tubes, and even X-rays, essentially providing the juice for countless scientific discoveries. His meticulous craftsmanship and improvements made his coils reliable and widely adopted, propelling scientific research forward and making him a key, if often unsung, hero in the early days of electrical science. He really got the sparks flying!
