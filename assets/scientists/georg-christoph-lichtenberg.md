Georg Christoph Lichtenberg was a German intellectual who was basically the Twitter before Twitter, but with way more substance and wit. Born in 1742, he was a physics professor at Göttingen, a sharp experimentalist who worked on electricity (Lichtenberg figures, anyone?). But he's perhaps best remembered for his 'Waste Books' – notebooks filled with brilliant aphorisms, satirical observations, and philosophical musings. Imagine a mind that could explain electrical phenomena one minute and then pen a profound, hilarious jab at human folly the next. He was skeptical, insightful, and always questioning, making him a true Enlightenment polymath. Lichtenberg proved that you could be a serious scientist and still have a riotously funny, philosophical take on the world.
