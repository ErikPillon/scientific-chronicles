Frank Conrad was the ultimate tinkerer whose backyard experiments accidentally kickstarted the age of broadcasting! This Westinghouse engineer, while fiddling with radio equipment in his garage, began playing music and chatting on the air, primarily to test his gadgets. People actually started listening, and *poof* — the world's first commercial radio station, KDKA, was born from his amateur hobby in 1920. Conrad didn't just invent components; he inadvertently created a whole new industry and a way for news, music, and stories to reach millions. He turned a passion project into a global phenomenon, proving that sometimes the biggest revolutions start with a simple, curious 'What if I try this?'
