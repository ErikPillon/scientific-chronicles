Friedrich Hund was one of those brilliant minds who helped shape quantum mechanics into the wild ride it is today. This German physicist is famous for 'Hund's rules,' which sound like something you'd find in a secret society handbook, but are actually fundamental principles that explain how electrons fill atomic orbitals. Basically, he figured out why atoms behave the way they do when forming bonds, a cornerstone of chemistry and materials science. He was also a pioneer in molecular orbital theory and worked alongside other physics giants. Living to a ripe old 101, he certainly had plenty of time to see his quantum contributions reverberate through science!
