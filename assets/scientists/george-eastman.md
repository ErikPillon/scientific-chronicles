Before selfies and Instagram filters, there was George Eastman, the American visionary who democratized photography. Tired of lugging around bulky cameras and processing chemicals, he invented roll film and the user-friendly Kodak camera, essentially turning photography from a complex science project into a casual hobby for the masses. His iconic slogan, "You push the button, we do the rest," perfectly captured the revolution he ignited. Eastman wasn't just an inventor; he was a marketing genius who practically invented the snapshot and gave us a brand name that just *sounded* like a camera click: "Kodak." Talk about a picture-perfect legacy!
