John Dee was one of Elizabethan England's most learned men, a serious mathematician who wrote an influential preface promoting Euclidean geometry, amassed one of the largest private libraries in England, and served as an advisor and personal astrologer to Queen Elizabeth I, reportedly even selecting the astrologically auspicious date for her coronation. He was also deeply involved in navigation science, training many of the era's explorers and helping develop techniques and instruments used in England's expanding maritime ventures. But Dee's reputation took a stranger turn later in life when he became consumed with attempts to communicate with angels through a medium and a supposed 'scrying' crystal, blending cutting-edge mathematics with what today reads as pure occult mysticism. A genuinely brilliant mind who apparently saw no contradiction between calculus and conjuring spirits. 🔮📐
