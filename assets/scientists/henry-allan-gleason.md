Henry A. Gleason was an American botanist and ecologist who wasn't afraid to challenge the status quo. Back when everyone thought plant communities were like super-organisms with predictable stages, Gleason was like, 'Nah, it's more complicated.' He championed the 'individualistic concept,' arguing that plant distribution was more about individual species responding to environmental factors, rather than neat, unified communities. His ideas were a bit ahead of their time, but later became foundational to modern ecology, proving that sometimes, the lone wolf has the clearest view of the forest.
