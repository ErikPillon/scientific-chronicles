Garnet Carter, an American entrepreneur from Lookout Mountain, Tennessee, wasn't a lab coat-wearing scientist, but he certainly engineered a worldwide phenomenon: miniature golf! In 1927, he was developing a resort and wanted a fun, accessible activity for his guests. Traditional golf courses were too big and costly, so he designed "Tom Thumb Golf," a scaled-down version with artificial greens and whimsical obstacles. It was an instant hit! His courses were so popular that he patented them and began franchising. Carter essentially democratized golf, making it available to everyone, everywhere, and creating a whole new form of entertainment that continues to amuse families (and sometimes frustrate competitive friends) to this day. A true visionary of fun and leisure engineering!
