Charles Gordon Curtis was an American engineer and inventor who wasn't just fiddling with gears; he was shaping the future of power. Born in San Francisco, Curtis made his mark with significant improvements to the steam turbine, especially his multi-stage impulse design. While Charles Parsons is often lauded for the initial steam turbine, Curtis's innovations were crucial for making these powerhouses more efficient and practical for large-scale electricity generation and marine propulsion. Imagine a world without powerful generators – that's the impact this guy had! He held numerous patents and his work was pivotal in the development of electric power systems. He truly was a driving force behind the electrification of the world, making sure the lights stayed on and ships kept moving.
