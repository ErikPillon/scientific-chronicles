Alphonse Bertillon was the OG forensic scientist, a French criminologist who practically invented the mugshot and the scientific identification of criminals. Frustrated by the inability to consistently identify repeat offenders, he developed "Bertillonage" in the 1880s – a system that meticulously recorded body measurements (like arm span, head length, and foot size), alongside photographs and physical descriptions. While fingerprinting eventually superseded it, Bertillon's system was revolutionary for its time, bringing scientific rigor to police work. He was the Sherlock Holmes of data points, transforming criminal investigation from guesswork into a systematic, measurable endeavor. He literally measured up criminals to keep society safe!
