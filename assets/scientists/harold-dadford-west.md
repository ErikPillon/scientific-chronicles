Meet Harold Dadford West, a groundbreaking biochemist who wasn't just breaking scientific barriers but racial ones too. In a time when opportunities for Black scientists were scarce, West became a giant in his field, eventually serving as president of Meharry Medical College. He's famously credited with discovering the therapeutic uses of the amino acid threonine. Think about it: a crucial building block of life, and West helped us understand how to use it for good! His work paved the way for better understanding of nutrition and metabolism, making him a true unsung hero of medical science.
