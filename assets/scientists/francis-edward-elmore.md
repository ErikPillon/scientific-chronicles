Before Francis Edward Elmore, getting valuable minerals out of crushed ore was a messy business. This British metallurgical wizard, born in 1866, basically taught us how to make minerals float their way to freedom using oil. His 'Elmore process' meant mining companies could extract more goodies with less waste, which was a huge deal for the industry. Imagine rocks and water, then add a bit of oil, shake, and *poof* – the good stuff comes to the top. Elmore's genius wasn't just about chemistry; it was about making money by being clever. He truly gave the mining world a lift – quite literally! His innovations significantly improved the efficiency and economic viability of extracting valuable metals from low-grade ores, a process still fundamental in modern mining.
