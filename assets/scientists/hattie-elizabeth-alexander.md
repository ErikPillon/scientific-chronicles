Hattie Alexander was a real medical superhero, especially for kids! Back when H. influenzae meningitis was a terrifying death sentence for most children, she rolled up her sleeves and found the cure. Seriously, she saved countless lives with her smarts and persistence, becoming a trailblazer in pediatrics and microbiology. Her groundbreaking work dramatically dropped mortality rates from nearly 100% to under 20% by introducing a specific antiserum and later antibiotics. Take that, deadly bacteria! Her legacy isn't just about discovery; it's about the very real difference she made for families facing the unimaginable.
