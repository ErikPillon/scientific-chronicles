Anton-Lazzaro Moro was an 18th-century Italian priest and natural philosopher with a serious fascination for rocks and the Earth's history. He was a prominent proponent of the Neptunian theory, which argued that all rocks, including volcanic ones, were formed by precipitation from a primordial ocean. While later proven incorrect by Hutton's Plutonist views, Moro's detailed observations of fossils and stratigraphy were valuable for the nascent field of geology. He might have been a bit wet in his geological theories (literally!), but his contributions helped lay the groundwork for understanding Earth's ancient past, proving that even a wrong theory can push science forward.
