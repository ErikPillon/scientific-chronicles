Curtis Marbut, born in 1863, might not be a household name, but if you've ever appreciated healthy soil (and who hasn't, really?), you owe him a nod! This American geologist and geographer basically invented modern soil science in the United States. As head of the U.S. Bureau of Soils, he tirelessly traveled the country, mapping and classifying soils, developing the first comprehensive system for understanding Earth's critical skin. He understood that soil wasn't just dirt; it was a complex living system, and his work was fundamental to agriculture and environmental understanding. He taught us how to truly 'dig' into the ground beneath our feet.
