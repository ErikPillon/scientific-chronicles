Charles A. Coffin didn't invent the lightbulb, but he certainly turned its glow into a global empire. As the first CEO of General Electric, he transformed a collection of Edison's companies into an industrial powerhouse. Coffin was the strategic mastermind behind GE's rise, creating a blueprint for modern corporations by integrating research, manufacturing, and global sales of electrical technology. He knew that innovation wasn't just about discovery, but about bringing those discoveries to the world at scale. He built a behemoth that lit up cities, powered factories, and truly electrified the 20th century.
