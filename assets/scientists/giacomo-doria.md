Giacomo Doria wasn't just interested in bugs; he was obsessed! This intrepid Italian nobleman and naturalist spent his life exploring the far reaches of the globe, from Persia to Borneo, collecting a staggering array of insects and other specimens. But Doria wasn't content just collecting; he was a meticulous researcher, describing countless new species and contributing massively to our understanding of biodiversity. His real legacy, however, might be the Civic Museum of Natural History in Genoa, which he not only founded but directed for over four decades. He transformed it into a world-class institution, a testament to his passion for the natural world and his dedication to making science accessible. A true Renaissance man of natural history, with a serious bug for adventure.
