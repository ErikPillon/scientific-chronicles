Before Earle Dickson, if you got a little cut, you had to perform an elaborate bandage ritual with gauze and tape. But Earle, a cotton buyer for Johnson & Johnson, had a perpetually accident-prone wife, Josephine. Tired of her minor kitchen mishaps requiring his help, he ingeniously pre-assembled small pieces of gauze onto adhesive tape, covering them with crinoline to keep them sterile. Voila! The Band-Aid was born in 1920, making self-care (and his wife's life) much simpler. Talk about a true domestic hero who accidentally revolutionized first aid!
