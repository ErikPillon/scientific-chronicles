Jack Cover, a physicist and inventor, literally zapped his way into history by inventing the Taser. Yes, *that* Taser, the less-lethal weapon that delivers an electric shock to incapacitate. A former NASA scientist who worked on the Apollo program, Cover was inspired by a comic book story, "Tom Swift and His Electric Rifle," and by the challenge of creating a safer way to apprehend criminals. He patented the "Thomas A. Swift's Electric Rifle" (TASER) in 1974. His invention stemmed from a desire to reduce gun violence and create an alternative. While controversial, the Taser remains a widely used tool for law enforcement globally, a testament to Cover's innovative spirit and his mission to use science for a safer, albeit electrically charged, world. From moon missions to incapacitating devices, Cover's career was certainly... shocking!
