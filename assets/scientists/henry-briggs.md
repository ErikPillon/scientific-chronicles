Henry Briggs was an English mathematician who, let's be honest, made life a whole lot easier for anyone doing complex calculations. He was tight with John Napier (the inventor of logarithms) and, after meeting him, decided to refine Napier's system. Briggs pushed for and developed *common logarithms* (base-10), making them incredibly practical for astronomers and navigators. His extensive tables of logarithms became indispensable tools, reducing grueling multiplication and division into simple addition and subtraction. He basically gave the world a calculator before calculators existed, proving that sometimes, making things simpler is the real genius.
