Eliza Maria Mosher was a woman of many firsts and fierce determination! She was among the earliest female physicians in the United States, breaking barriers in a male-dominated field. Not content with just practicing medicine, she became a champion of public health and hygiene, stressing the importance of proper posture, exercise, and sanitation. Mosher was also the very first Dean of Women at the University of Michigan, where she advocated for the well-being and academic success of female students. A true visionary, she dedicated her life to health, education, and empowering women in the medical world.
