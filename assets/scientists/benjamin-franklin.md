Benjamin Franklin wasn't just a Founding Father with a kite; he was a one-man scientific revolution who dabbled in everything from electricity to the very nature of human society. Born in Boston in 1706, this self-taught polymath became a global sensation for his daring experiments with electricity, proving lightning was indeed electrical and inventing the lightning rod, saving countless homes from fiery fates. But his genius didn't stop there: he gave us bifocals, the Franklin stove (a more efficient heater), and even mapped the Gulf Stream. He was a printer, inventor, diplomat, and philosopher all rolled into one, always curious, always experimenting. Franklin famously quipped, "By failing to prepare, you are preparing to fail," and he certainly prepared us for a future powered by scientific inquiry and practical innovation. He truly electrified the Enlightenment, both literally and figuratively!
