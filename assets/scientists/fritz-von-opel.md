Fritz von Opel, heir to the Opel automotive empire, wasn't content just selling cars. He was a true speed demon and a pioneer of rocketry, earning the nickname 'Rocket Fritz.' In 1928, he stunned the world by rocketing across a track in a car powered by solid-fuel rockets, reaching incredible speeds and demonstrating the immense potential of rocket propulsion. The next year, he took to the skies in a rocket-powered aircraft, proving that the sky wasn't even the limit for his ambitions. His high-octane stunts not only entertained but also pushed the early development of rocket technology, leaving a fiery legacy in both automotive and aerospace history. He was a thrill-seeker who truly embraced the future.
