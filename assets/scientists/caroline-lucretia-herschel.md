Caroline Herschel wasn't just tagging along with her famous astronomer brother William; she was a comet-spotting, star-charting rockstar in her own right! Born in Germany, she moved to England, initially helping William with his telescopic endeavors. But Caroline soon became a phenomenal astronomer in her own right, systematically searching the skies. She discovered eight comets, including the famous 35P/Herschel-Rigollet, and tirelessly revised star catalogs. She wasn't just the first woman to discover a comet; she was also the first woman in Britain to be paid for her scientific work, breaking major barriers in the late 18th and early 19th centuries. Talk about a trailblazer who truly reached for the stars!
