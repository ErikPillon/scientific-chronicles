Anthony Fokker was a Dutch aviation daredevil and genius who, quite literally, built an empire out of thin air! From an early age, he was obsessed with flight, designing his first aircraft, the 'Spin' (Spider), before he was even 20. During World War I, his Fokker company became infamous for producing formidable German fighter planes, including the iconic Fokker Dr.I triplane flown by the Red Baron. Post-war, he moved to the United States, continuing to innovate and design successful commercial aircraft. Fokker was more than an engineer; he was an entrepreneur who adapted to changing times, always pushing the boundaries of what airplanes could do. He showed the world that with enough vision, you could truly soar.
