If you wanted to peer into the cosmos in the 18th century, you went to James Short. This Scottish optical wizard didn't just make telescopes; he crafted instruments of unparalleled precision and clarity. He perfected the art of grinding and polishing metallic mirrors for reflecting telescopes, making his designs superior to many of his contemporaries. His telescopes were so sought after that even King George III bought one! Short's work helped astronomers make groundbreaking observations, literally bringing the stars closer for scientific inquiry. He proved that sometimes, the clearest vision comes from the best lenses (or in this case, mirrors!).
