Jacob Bernoulli was a heavyweight in the world of 17th-century mathematics, part of the legendary Bernoulli family who seemingly had science in their DNA. He was a pioneer in calculus, probability theory, and mechanics, and he gave us some foundational ideas like the Law of Large Numbers (which helps us understand how random events average out over time). He also explored the properties of the logarithmic spiral, even asking for it to be engraved on his tombstone with the inscription 'Eadem mutata resurgo' ('Though changed, I shall arise the same'). A brilliant mind who truly shaped the mathematical landscape of his era.
