Carl Friedrich von Weizsäcker was a Renaissance man of the 20th century, a theoretical physicist who helped develop theories on nuclear fusion (think how stars shine!) but was equally at home pondering the philosophical and ethical dimensions of science. He was involved in Germany's wartime nuclear program, a complex period he reflected on deeply for the rest of his long life. After the war, he became a prominent voice for peace and responsibility in the nuclear age, shifting his focus towards cosmology, quantum mechanics, and the bigger questions of humanity's place in the universe. He truly believed scientists had a moral obligation to consider the impact of their discoveries.
