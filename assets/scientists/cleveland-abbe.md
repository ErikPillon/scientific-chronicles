Ever check the weather app on your phone? You can thank Cleveland Abbe, at least a little bit! This American meteorologist was a true visionary who, in the late 19th century, basically invented modern weather forecasting in the United States. As a professor at the Cincinnati Observatory, he started issuing daily weather bulletins, which was a pretty radical idea at the time. His success led to his appointment as the chief meteorologist for the U.S. Army Signal Service, which eventually became the U.S. Weather Bureau (now the National Weather Service). Abbe standardized observations, promoted the use of telegraphs for data collection, and pushed for scientific methods to predict the whims of Mother Nature. He literally changed how we talk about tomorrow's weather!
