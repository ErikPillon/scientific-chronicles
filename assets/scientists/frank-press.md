Frank Press was a towering figure in geophysics, not just shaking up the scientific world but also the political one. This American seismologist was a pioneer in understanding the Earth's deep structure and the mechanics of earthquakes, contributing significantly to our grasp of plate tectonics. But he wasn't just confined to labs and lecture halls; he served as science advisor to President Jimmy Carter, chairing the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology. He literally helped shape national policy based on scientific understanding, bridging the gap between deep Earth mysteries and real-world decisions. Later, as President of the National Academy of Sciences, he continued to advocate for science's vital role in society. A truly influential mind who moved mountains both literally and metaphorically!
