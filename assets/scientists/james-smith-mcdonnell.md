If you've ever seen a fighter jet streak across the sky or a spacecraft blast into orbit, you've likely seen the legacy of James S. McDonnell. This American aviation titan founded McDonnell Aircraft Corporation, which became a giant in military aviation, producing legendary planes like the F-4 Phantom and playing a pivotal role in the Mercury and Gemini space programs. 'Mr. Mac' wasn't just a businessman; he was an engineer at heart, driven by a passion for flight and space exploration. His vision helped propel humanity into the jet age and beyond, proving that the sky (and space!) was never the limit.
