Bruno Rossi was the ultimate cosmic detective, chasing down mysterious particles from space! Born in Venice, this brilliant physicist started out making groundbreaking discoveries about cosmic rays in the 1930s, even proving they weren't just gamma rays but actual charged particles. When fascism reared its ugly head in Italy, he fled, eventually landing in the US, where he continued his cosmic quest, including significant contributions to the Manhattan Project. But his real stellar moment? He virtually invented X-ray astronomy in the 1960s, leading the team that first detected X-rays from outside our solar system – specifically, from a source now known as Scorpius X-1. His work quite literally opened a new window to the universe, letting us peek at phenomena previously invisible. Not bad for a guy who just wanted to know what was raining down from the heavens!
