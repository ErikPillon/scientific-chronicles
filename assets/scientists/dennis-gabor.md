Dennis Gabor was a true visionary who literally brought the third dimension to our vision. Born in Hungary and later becoming a British citizen, this electrical engineer and physicist conjured up the concept of holography in 1947 while trying to improve electron microscopes. He figured out how to record not just the intensity of light, but also its phase, allowing for the reconstruction of a full 3D image. While it took the invention of the laser years later for holography to really shine, Gabor's foundational work earned him the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1971. He effectively showed us how to capture reality in a whole new, dazzlingly immersive way.
