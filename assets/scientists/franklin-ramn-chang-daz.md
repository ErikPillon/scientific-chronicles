Born in Costa Rica, Franklin Chang Díaz dreamt of the stars from a young age. After moving to the U.S., he not only earned a Ph.D. in plasma physics but then joined NASA's astronaut corps in 1981. He made history in 1986 as the first Hispanic person to venture into space, kicking off an incredible career with a record-tying seven Space Shuttle missions! This dude didn't just fly; he worked on groundbreaking plasma propulsion engines, spent over 1,600 hours in space, and became a beacon for aspiring scientists and engineers across Latin America and beyond. From a kid in San José to a space pioneer, Chang Díaz truly showed that the sky's *not* the limit.
