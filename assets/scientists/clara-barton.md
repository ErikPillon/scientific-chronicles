Meet Clara Barton, the force of nature who basically invented disaster relief in the U.S. Born on Christmas Day, she spent her life giving back, from teaching at 15 to braving battlefields during the Civil War, earning the nickname 'Angel of the Battlefield.' But she wasn't content just patching up soldiers; after seeing the International Red Cross in action, she decided America needed its own version. So, in 1881, she founded the American Red Cross, turning her passion for helping into a national institution. She even convinced the government to include aid for natural disasters, not just war. Clara was all about action, compassion, and getting things done – a true pioneer who showed that one determined woman could change the world, one bandage and one act of kindness at a time. Forget the drama, Clara was the real deal when it came to hands-on aid.
