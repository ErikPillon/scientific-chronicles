Charles Edwin Bessey was the plant whisperer before 'plant whisperer' was a thing. This American botanist revolutionized how we think about flowering plants. Back in the day, classifying flora was a bit of a tangled mess, but Bessey brought order to the botanical chaos with his 'Bessey System' of classification. He moved away from purely artificial systems to ones based on evolutionary relationships, shaking up the botanical world and setting the stage for modern plant taxonomy. He also mentored a whole generation of botanists, proving that even in the world of roots and shoots, good teaching sprouts great minds. He certainly knew how to put plants in their place!
