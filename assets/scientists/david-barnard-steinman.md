David Barnard Steinman, born in 1886 in New York, wasn't just an engineer; he was an artist of steel and concrete, transforming functional structures into breathtaking landmarks. If a bridge needed to be built, especially one that looked impossible, Steinman was your guy. He designed over 400 bridges across five continents, including the iconic Mackinac Bridge in Michigan, a suspension bridge so long it was once the longest in the world. He was obsessed with safety and aesthetics, ensuring his creations were not only robust but also visually stunning. He literally wrote the book on bridge engineering! This guy made sure that when you crossed a river, you didn't just get to the other side; you experienced a masterpiece. A true architectural superhero with a slide rule for a cape.
