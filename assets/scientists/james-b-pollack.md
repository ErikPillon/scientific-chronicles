If planets could talk, they'd definitely thank James B. Pollack! This American astrophysicist was obsessed with what makes planetary atmospheres tick. He played a key role in understanding why Venus is such a fiery, runaway greenhouse oven and how Mars lost its water and became a dusty desert. His work was absolutely crucial for missions to these planets. Pollack also delved into the atmospheric effects of asteroid impacts, including the one that wiped out the dinosaurs. He literally gave us the ultimate weather report for the solar system's biggest players, contributing massively to our knowledge of climate change on other worlds (and ours!). His work was truly out of this world!
