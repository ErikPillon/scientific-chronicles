If you've ever wondered about ocean currents and the deep blue's mysteries, you might want to thank Columbus O'Donnell Iselin! This American oceanographer, born in 1904, was a titan in marine science during the mid-20th century. He spent most of his illustrious career at the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, eventually serving as its long-time director. Iselin was instrumental in understanding large-scale ocean circulation, the Gulf Stream, and the physical oceanography of the North Atlantic. His work wasn't just theoretical; he was a leader in developing oceanographic research vessels and techniques, essentially building the framework for modern ocean exploration. He helped turn oceanography into a rigorous scientific discipline, revealing the complex, hidden workings of our planet's vast waters.
