Ever tried to remember a phone number and wondered why your brain could only handle about seven digits? Thank George A. Miller, the American psychologist who mapped out the "magical number seven, plus or minus two" limit of our short-term memory! This titan of cognitive psychology helped kickstart the cognitive revolution, shifting psychology's focus from observable behavior to the inner workings of the mind. He explored language, memory, and how we process information, making our mental machinery a bit less mysterious. Truly a mind-reader of the academic world!
