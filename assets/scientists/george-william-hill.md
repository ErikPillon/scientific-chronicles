While many gaze at the stars with wonder, George William Hill looked at them with a calculator and a very big brain. This American mathematician and astronomer was a master of celestial mechanics, focusing his incredible intellect on the notoriously tricky 'three-body problem' – calculating the gravitational interactions of three celestial objects. His work, particularly on lunar theory, significantly advanced our ability to predict the movements of the Moon and planets with astonishing accuracy. Imagine trying to chart the wobbly dance of celestial bodies without modern computers! Hill's analytical methods were so profound that they're still fundamental in astrodynamics today. He truly brought order to the cosmic chaos, one equation at a time.
