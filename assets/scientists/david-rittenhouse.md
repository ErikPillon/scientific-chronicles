David Rittenhouse was basically America's Renaissance man before it was cool. This Pennsylvania-born polymath was an astronomer, mathematician, clockmaker, and ingenious instrument maker during the 18th century. He didn't just tell time; he built intricate orreries (mechanical models of the solar system) so precise they were practically scientific marvels, including what's considered the first planetarium in the United States. He observed and accurately calculated the transits of Venus and Mercury, was elected to the American Philosophical Society, and even became the first director of the U.S. Mint. Rittenhouse was a self-taught genius who mixed practical craftsmanship with profound scientific inquiry, proving that American ingenuity was alive and well even before the nation officially declared its independence.
