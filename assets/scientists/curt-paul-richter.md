Curt Paul Richter, born in 1894, was the kind of scientist who wanted to know what made us tick – quite literally! This American psychobiologist was a pioneer in understanding our internal 'biological clocks' long before it was a fashionable topic. He didn't just study rats; he helped reveal fundamental principles of self-regulatory behavior, stress, and how our bodies intrinsically try to maintain balance. His work laid crucial groundwork for chronobiology and behavioral neuroscience, showing that even the simplest organisms have sophisticated internal timing mechanisms. He was basically the original 'bio-hacker' before the term even existed!
