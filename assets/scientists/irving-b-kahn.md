Irving Kahn was the kind of guy who saw the future of television before most people even had a decent antenna. A true visionary in communications, he didn't just tinker; he built empires, from early cable TV systems (hello, subscriber fees!) to pioneering satellite communications. He basically laid the groundwork for how we binge-watch today, even if he couldn't have imagined Netflix. He once acquired a company to get a patent for a TV signal booster, showing he was always ahead of the curve. If you've ever enjoyed crystal-clear channels (or at least clearer than rabbit ears), you've got Kahn to thank. A fascinating blend of engineer and entrepreneur, he was never afraid to push the boundaries of what was possible in media.
