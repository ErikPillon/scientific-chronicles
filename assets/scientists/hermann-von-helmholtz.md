Hermann von Helmholtz was the kind of genius who made everyone else feel a bit lazy. This German polymath was a master of pretty much everything, dabbling (and excelling!) in physics, physiology, medicine, and mathematics. He's famous for formulating the law of conservation of energy (take that, perpetual motion machines!), inventing the ophthalmoscope (hello, eye doctors!), and even making groundbreaking discoveries about how we perceive sound and color. Basically, if it involved understanding the human body or the fundamental laws of the universe, Helmholtz probably had a brilliant idea about it. He was a human Wikipedia before Wikipedia existed, proving that some minds just don't like to stay in one lane.
