Elihu Thomson was a dynamo in the world of electricity! Born in Manchester, England, but making his name in America, he was a true polymath with over 700 patents to his name. From arc lighting and electric welding to advanced alternating current systems, Thomson's inventions illuminated cities and powered industries. He co-founded the Thomson-Houston Electric Company, which later merged with Edison General Electric Company to form the behemoth we know today as General Electric. A brilliant mind who truly helped electrify the modern world, he even turned down an offer from Thomas Edison to lead his laboratory – a bold move!
