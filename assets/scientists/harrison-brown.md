Harrison Brown was a scientific polymath who wasn't afraid to look at the big picture – from the age of the Earth to the future of humanity. This American geochemist and nuclear chemist made significant contributions to understanding the age of our planet by studying meteorites. He was also a prominent voice advocating for nuclear arms control and warned about the environmental impact of human activity long before it was fashionable. Brown wasn't just calculating; he was contemplating, using science not just to understand the universe but to guide our place within it. A true intellectual heavyweight with a conscience!
