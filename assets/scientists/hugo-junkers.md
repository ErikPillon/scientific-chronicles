Hugo Junkers was no junker when it came to innovation! This German engineering genius was way ahead of his time, especially in aviation. While everyone else was messing around with wood and fabric, Junkers was busy crafting sleek, all-metal aircraft, literally changing the face of flight. He designed the world's first all-metal passenger plane, the Junkers F.13, in 1919 – a game-changer that made air travel safer and more robust. But his brilliance wasn't limited to planes; he also invented gas boilers and calorimeters. He was a true polymath, a man who saw the future in strong, lightweight alloys and wasn't afraid to build it. Sadly, his independent spirit clashed with the rising Nazi regime, and his companies were eventually nationalized. A visionary who built the future of aviation, then lost control of it.
