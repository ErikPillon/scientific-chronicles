Meet G. H. Hardy, the British mathematical titan who made number theory sexy (or at least, incredibly rigorous). This isn't your average guy who just *likes* numbers; Hardy was obsessed with their purity and elegance, dedicating his life to mathematical analysis. But he's perhaps most famously known for something beyond his own brilliant proofs: his incredible mentorship of Srinivasa Ramanujan, a self-taught mathematical genius from India. Hardy saw the raw, untrained brilliance in Ramanujan's bizarre notebooks and, against all odds, brought him to Cambridge. This unlikely duo produced some of the most profound mathematical work of the 20th century. Hardy was an eccentric, a pacifist, and a cricket fan, but above all, a visionary who understood that true genius knows no boundaries.
