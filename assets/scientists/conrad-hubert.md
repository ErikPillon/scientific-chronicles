Before smartphone flashlights, there was Conrad Hubert! Born Akiba Horowitz in Russia in 1856, he emigrated to the U.S. and became a tinkerer extraordinaire. Legend has it he was playing with a primitive battery and bulb when he realized the potential for portable light. In 1899, he patented his 'electric hand torch' – a device we now know and love as the flashlight. His company, American Electrical Novelty and Manufacturing Company (later to become Eveready), made flashlights a household item, saving countless fumbles in the dark. So next time you flick on a light in a power outage, give a silent nod to Hubert, the guy who banished gloom!
