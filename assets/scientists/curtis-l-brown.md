If you ever wondered who gets to zoom into space and come back, meet Curt Brown! Born in 1956, this American astronaut and retired Air Force colonel has seen more of Earth from above than most of us will ever see from the ground. He's a veteran of six Space Shuttle missions, commanding several of them, and was at the helm during pivotal missions, including those involving the Hubble Space Telescope. He's one of those cool individuals who gets to say 'I flew the Space Shuttle' – not once, but six times! Definitely a guy who knows how to make a mission happen.
