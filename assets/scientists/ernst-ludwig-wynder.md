Imagine being the person who definitively told the world that smoking causes cancer – that was Ernst Ludwig Wynder! Born in Germany in 1922 and later becoming an American citizen, Wynder was a brilliant epidemiologist and public health hero. As a medical student in the late 1940s, he conducted one of the earliest and most influential studies providing irrefutable evidence of the link between cigarette smoking and lung cancer. His relentless research and advocacy played a critical role in changing public perception and sparking anti-smoking campaigns worldwide. He literally saved millions of lives by uncovering a deadly truth. Talk about making a monumental impact!
