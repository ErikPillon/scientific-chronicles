Ever noticed how adding salt to water changes its boiling point? Thank François-Marie Raoult for explaining why! This French chemist, active in the late 19th century, became a legend in physical chemistry for his groundbreaking work on solutions. He meticulously studied how solutes affect the physical properties of solvents, leading to his famous "Raoult's Law," which predicts the vapor pressure of an ideal solution. This seemingly simple discovery was a huge deal, providing fundamental insights into the nature of solutions and becoming a cornerstone of chemical thermodynamics. He basically gave us the rulebook for how stuff dissolves and boils, which is pretty foundational if you ask us.
