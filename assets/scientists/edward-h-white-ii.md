Meet Edward H. White II, the daredevil American astronaut who made history by stepping out of his Gemini 4 spacecraft in 1965, becoming the first American to 'stroll' in space. He wasn't just a space walker; he was a U.S. Air Force pilot and engineer, contributing immensely to the early days of the space race. Sadly, his illustrious career was cut short when he tragically died alongside Gus Grissom and Roger Chaffee in the Apollo 1 pre-launch fire in 1967. A true hero who pushed the boundaries of human exploration.
