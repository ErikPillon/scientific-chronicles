When it comes to space travel, physicians are often the unsung heroes keeping astronauts in tip-top shape. But Boris Borisovich Yegorov wasn't content just to stay on the ground. This Soviet medical doctor, born in 1937, literally became the first physician to ever fly into space! In 1964, he launched aboard the Voskhod 1 mission, a multi-person crew where he served as the mission doctor. Imagine being the medic, the patient, and the subject of your own research, all at once, while orbiting Earth! He paved the way for understanding how the human body adapts to microgravity, combining his medical expertise with the ultimate hands-on experiment. Talk about a house call!
