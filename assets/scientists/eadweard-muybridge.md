Eadweard Muybridge, an English photographer with an absolutely wild backstory (including a murder trial!), became a scientific legend by literally stopping time. In the late 1870s, he perfected chronophotography, using multiple cameras to capture sequential phases of motion. His most famous experiment, funded by former California governor Leland Stanford, proved once and for all that a galloping horse *does* lift all four hooves off the ground. These groundbreaking motion studies didn't just settle bar bets; they were a fundamental step towards understanding animal locomotion and, crucially, laid the very groundwork for the invention of motion pictures. So, next time you're binging a TV show, tip your hat to the man who made moving pictures possible!
