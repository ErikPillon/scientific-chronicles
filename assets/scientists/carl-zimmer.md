Carl Zimmer is the science writer you wish you had for every biology class – he makes even the trickiest topics like genetics, evolution, and viruses sound utterly fascinating. An American journalist and author, Zimmer has a knack for translating high-level scientific research into compelling narratives for the general public. He's a prolific writer for The New York Times, STAT, and many others, not to mention his numerous award-winning books. Whether he's diving into the mysterious world of parasites or explaining the latest CRISPR breakthroughs, Zimmer's writing is always clear, witty, and deeply researched. He connects complex scientific concepts to everyday life, helping us understand the incredible stories unfolding in labs around the world and within our own bodies. He's basically the ultimate tour guide to the wonders of life science, making sure no one gets lost in the jargon.
