Ferdinand Keller was the Swiss archaeologist who inadvertently stumbled upon a whole new world – or rather, a very old one! In the mid-19th century, low water levels in Lake Zurich revealed mysterious wooden piles. Keller, a keen observer and the president of the Antiquarian Society in Zurich, recognized these as remnants of prehistoric pile dwellings. His systematic excavation and study of these 'lake dwellings' launched a new era in archaeology, offering incredible insights into Stone and Bronze Age life in Europe. He didn't just find old stuff; he created a scientific framework for understanding entire prehistoric communities that once lived over water. Pretty cool for a guy just trying to keep track of local antiques!
