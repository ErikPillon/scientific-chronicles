Meet Charles Clermont-Ganneau, the French archaeologist who had a knack for ancient mysteries and a sharp eye for a good forgery! In the late 19th century, he became a legend by not only discovering priceless artifacts but also by exposing some of the most elaborate hoaxes of his time. His biggest coup? Playing a key role in deciphering the Mesha Stele (also known as the Moabite Stone) in 1868, a treasure trove of ancient Semitic writing that shed light on biblical history. He raced against time and Bedouin tribes to secure casts of the inscription, even as parts of it were being smashed. Clermont-Ganneau was a true academic adventurer, blending linguistic genius with real-world grit to unlock secrets buried for millennia.
