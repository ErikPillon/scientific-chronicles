Antoine-Thomson d’Abbadie was a 19th-century French adventurer with a serious scientific streak. Alongside his brother Arnaud, he spent a decade crisscrossing Ethiopia, meticulously mapping the region and cataloging its languages and cultures. But he wasn't just a fancy explorer; Antoine-Thomson was also a sharp astronomer and geophysicist. He even tried to determine the length of the metre using observations from the Earth's flattening – talk about high ambitions! He left a legacy not just of geographical knowledge but also of detailed scientific observation from a truly intrepid spirit. This guy literally put Ethiopia on the scientific map!
