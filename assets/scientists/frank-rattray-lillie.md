Frank Rattray Lillie was the kind of scientist who really got into the nitty-gritty of how life begins. This American embryologist and zoologist was a major player in the early 20th century, particularly famous for his work on cell lineage and the development of the chick embryo. He also made significant contributions to understanding sexual differentiation, especially with his studies on freemartins (a sterile female bovine born twin to a male), which provided crucial insights into hormonal influences on development. Beyond the lab, he was instrumental in shaping American biology through his leadership at the Marine Biological Laboratory at Woods Hole. Lillie wasn't just watching life unfold; he was figuring out its fundamental rules, helping to build the foundations of modern developmental biology.
