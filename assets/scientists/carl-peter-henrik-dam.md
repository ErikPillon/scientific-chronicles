Henrik Dam, a Danish biochemist with a knack for noticing the little things, stumbled upon something huge: Vitamin K! While researching cholesterol metabolism in chickens, he noticed a mysterious bleeding disorder when they were fed a specific diet. Turns out, it was a brand-new vitamin, essential for blood clotting. His discovery earned him a Nobel Prize and completely changed our understanding of nutrition and medicine. Thanks to Dam, doctors now know how to prevent serious bleeding issues, all because he paid attention to some unusually bruised chickens.
