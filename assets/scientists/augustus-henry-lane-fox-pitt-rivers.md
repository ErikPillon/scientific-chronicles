Forget Indiana Jones, meet Augustus Henry Lane-Fox Pitt-Rivers, the real OG of scientific archaeology! This British military man turned antiquarian wasn't content with treasure hunting. Oh no, he revolutionized archaeology by insisting on incredibly detailed excavation methods, documenting *everything* – not just the fancy finds. He believed that even common artifacts, when properly cataloged and contextualized, could tell a story about human evolution and culture. His estates became open-air labs for systematic digs, and he created a magnificent museum (still bearing his name at Oxford) to showcase his findings, emphasizing typology and evolution. Pitt-Rivers literally wrote the book on how to do archaeology properly, moving it from hobby to rigorous science. He really dug into the details, and the field is forever grateful!
