Denton A. Cooley was a cardiac surgeon who practically redefined what was possible inside the human chest. This Texan surgical titan was a trailblazer, performing the first successful human heart transplant in the U.S. in 1968 and, just a year later in 1969, making headlines again with the first implantation of a total artificial heart into a human. Cooley was a rapid-fire surgeon, known for his incredible speed and precision in the operating room, which often translated to better outcomes for his patients. He built an empire of heart health at the Texas Heart Institute, saving countless lives and pushing the boundaries of medical science with a blend of audacious skill and relentless dedication. He was, quite literally, a man with a big heart for fixing others'.
