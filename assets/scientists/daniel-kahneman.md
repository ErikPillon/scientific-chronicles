Ever wonder why you make those slightly bonkers decisions? Blame Daniel Kahneman! This Nobel Prize winner, alongside his brilliant collaborator Amos Tversky, cracked open the human mind's quirky shortcuts, introducing the world to cognitive biases and prospect theory. He showed us that we're not always the perfectly rational beings we think we are, often relying on 'System 1' (fast, intuitive) instead of 'System 2' (slow, logical). His insights fundamentally changed fields from economics to marketing, revealing the hilarious and often illogical ways our brains operate. Basically, he gave us the scientific excuse for why we sometimes choose the less-than-optimal option. Cheers to that!
