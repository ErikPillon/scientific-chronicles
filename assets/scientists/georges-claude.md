Georges Claude was the kind of inventor who literally brightened up the world – and cooled it down, too! This brilliant French engineer is best known as the 'father of neon lights.' He was the first to apply electric discharge to a sealed tube of neon gas to create that iconic, vibrant glow that still illuminates cityscapes and businesses worldwide. But Claude wasn't a one-trick pony; he also made significant advances in the liquefaction of air, developing industrial processes that made it cheaper and more efficient to produce liquid oxygen and nitrogen. So, from dazzling signs to essential industrial gases, Claude's innovations left an unmistakable mark on both technology and culture. A true glowing genius!
