Meet I. Bernard Cohen, the ultimate science history nerd (in the best way possible). This American scholar practically wrote the book on, well, writing about science history. His particular obsession? Isaac Newton. Cohen spent decades digging through Newton's archives, bringing the complex mind of the great physicist to life for generations of historians and enthusiasts. If you've ever wondered how Newton figured out gravity or got into alchemy, chances are Cohen laid the groundwork for your understanding. He didn't discover a new planet, but he certainly discovered new ways to appreciate the human minds behind scientific breakthroughs.
