Herman Frasch was a German-American chemist and engineer whose name is synonymous with making sulfur extraction *way* easier. Before his brilliant invention, getting sulfur out of the ground was a dangerous, expensive, and often failed endeavor. Frasch's ingenious process involved pumping superheated water and air into sulfur deposits, melting the sulfur and forcing it to the surface. This method transformed the sulfur industry, making it accessible and affordable, which was crucial for everything from fertilizers to explosives. He was a true chemical alchemist, turning underground deposits into industrial gold!
