Before we had fancy apps to identify bugs, there was Asa Fitch! This American pioneer was the very first state entomologist for New York, basically making him the original bug buster. He dedicated his life to studying insects, especially the ones messing with farmers' crops. His detailed reports and practical advice on pest control were groundbreaking for the time, helping agriculture thrive and proving that even tiny critters needed serious scientific attention. He made entomology a legit field, not just a hobby for curious naturalists.
