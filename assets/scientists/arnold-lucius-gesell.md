Ever wondered if your baby's development is 'normal'? Thank Arnold Gesell, an American pediatrician and psychologist who basically wrote the book on how kids grow. He spent ages meticulously observing and documenting the developmental stages of countless children, creating those famous Gesell developmental schedules. He argued that maturation, or biological unfolding, played a huge role, shaping how children hit milestones from crawling to talking. While his ideas sparked plenty of debate, his systematic approach laid crucial groundwork for understanding child development and is still referenced today. Basically, he helped parents everywhere freak out a little less (or more!) about their kids' progress.
