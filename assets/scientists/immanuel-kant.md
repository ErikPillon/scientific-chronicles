Immanuel Kant, born in Königsberg, Prussia (now Kaliningrad, Russia) in 1724, was a philosophical rockstar who barely left his hometown but profoundly reshaped how we think about...well, everything! While primarily known for his intricate philosophical systems like "Critique of Pure Reason," Kant wasn't just pondering ethics and epistemology; he was also a dabbler in science. He proposed the "nebular hypothesis" for the formation of the solar system way back in 1755, suggesting that planets formed from a rotating cloud of gas and dust—a theory surprisingly close to modern cosmology. He's the guy who tried to reconcile rationalism and empiricism, arguing that our minds actively structure our experience of reality. So, next time you're contemplating the universe or what's right and wrong, give a nod to Kant. He showed us that using our own understanding is truly courageous!
