If your phone calls today sound clear, you might want to thank Harold DeForest Arnold. This American physicist, working for Western Electric and Bell Labs, was instrumental in developing the high-vacuum tube amplifier. Before his genius, making a phone call across the country was like shouting into the wind and hoping someone heard you. Arnold's work on the audion amplifier was a game-changer, making long-distance telephony practical and ushering in an era of clearer communication. He essentially put the "long" in long-distance, making sure your voice could travel miles without getting lost in static.
