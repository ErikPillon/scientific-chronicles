Before refrigeration was a thing, Gail Borden Jr. tackled the challenge of keeping food from spoiling, especially for travelers and soldiers. His big hit? Condensed milk, which he patented in 1856. Imagine milk that wouldn't go bad for ages – it was a game-changer! From improving meat biscuits to making milk safe and portable, Borden was a true pioneer in food science, making sure no one had to drink sour milk again. A true hero for our cereals and coffee, his innovations literally nourished a growing nation and its military, proving that sometimes, the simplest ideas have the biggest impact. Who knew a cow's product could be so revolutionary?
