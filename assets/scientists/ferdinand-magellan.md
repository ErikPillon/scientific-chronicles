Talk about a bucket list achievement! Ferdinand Magellan, a Portuguese hotshot, was the brains (and the brawn) behind the first-ever global circumnavigation. Sure, he didn't quite make it back himself – thanks to a rather unfortunate skirmish in the Philippines – but his expedition proved once and for all that the Earth was, indeed, round and vast. His epic journey kicked off in 1519, proving that sometimes, you just gotta sail off into the unknown to make history. His voyage dramatically expanded Europe's geographic knowledge and opened up new trade routes, though it came at a significant human cost. A true pioneer, even if he met a somewhat sticky end.
