Conwy Lloyd Morgan was a true intellectual pioneer who helped shape how we think about animal minds. This British psychologist and ethologist is most famous for "Morgan's Canon," a principle that basically says, "Don't assume an animal is thinking like a human if a simpler explanation for its behavior will do." It was a crucial step in moving animal psychology away from pure anecdote towards rigorous scientific observation. He didn't just study animal behavior; he taught us *how* to study it properly, paving the way for modern ethology and comparative psychology with a healthy dose of scientific skepticism.
