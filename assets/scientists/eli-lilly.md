Meet Eli Lilly, a Civil War veteran who swapped his uniform for a lab coat (metaphorically speaking) to revolutionize medicine. After serving bravely, he opened his pharmaceutical company in 1876 in Indianapolis with a simple but radical idea: make high-quality, scientifically sound medicines, not just patent remedies. Lilly was obsessed with consistency and purity, setting standards long before regulations caught up. His company was among the first to mass-produce gelatin capsules and later, to produce insulin on a commercial scale. He wasn't a 'discoverer' of new compounds, but an inventor of modern pharmaceutical practices, making reliable drugs accessible and trustworthy. Talk about a prescription for success!
