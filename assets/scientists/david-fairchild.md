David Fairchild, born in Lansing, Michigan, in 1869, was America's original plant influencer, way before Instagram. As a botanist and agricultural explorer for the USDA, he was tasked with finding plants that could boost American agriculture and horticulture. And boy, did he deliver! He circled the globe multiple times, dodging disease and danger, bringing back thousands of species. Thanks to Fairchild, Americans enjoy navel oranges, avocados, mangoes, pistachios, bamboo, cherry blossoms, and countless other staples and ornamentals. He literally changed the flavor of a nation and painted its parks pink every spring. His home in Coconut Grove, Florida, "The Kampong," is now a botanical garden, a living testament to his insatiable curiosity and incredible finds. What a green thumb!
