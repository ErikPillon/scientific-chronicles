Imagine a world without skyscrapers. Before Elisha Graves Otis, they were pretty much impossible because, let's be honest, who wants to go up in an elevator that might just drop you? Otis, a brilliant mechanic, solved this problem in 1853 with his revolutionary safety device: a simple mechanism that would automatically engage if the hoisting ropes broke. He famously demonstrated it at the 1854 New York World's Fair, cutting the rope and proclaiming, 'All safe, gentlemen, all safe!' His invention not only allowed buildings to reach for the sky but also paved the way for modern urban living. Thanks, Elisha, for taking us to new heights, safely!
