Charles Wilson Markle was a quiet genius who helped lay the digital groundwork for the internet as we know it. A Canadian computer scientist, he was a key player in the team that developed the Interface Message Processor (IMP) – the "heart" of the early ARPANET. Essentially, he helped build the very first routers that made packet-switching possible, enabling computers to talk to each other across vast distances. If you're reading this online, you owe a little thanks to Markle for helping make those bytes flow, transforming how we connect and communicate globally.
