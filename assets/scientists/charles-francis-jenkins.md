Charles Francis Jenkins was a busy man trying to get us all to sit down and stare at screens. This American inventor was a true visionary in the early days of moving images. In the 1890s, he developed an early motion picture projector, helping to kickstart the age of cinema. But he didn't stop there! He then turned his inventive mind to television, creating early mechanical television systems. While he wasn't the sole inventor of either, his contributions were crucial, pushing the boundaries of what was possible in visual entertainment and communication. So, next time you're binge-watching, spare a thought for Jenkins, who was working out the kinks long before 'Netflix and chill' was even a glimmer in anyone's eye.
