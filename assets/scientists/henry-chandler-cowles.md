Henry Chandler Cowles, born 1869, was a plant guru who essentially invented modern ecology in North America. Forget just looking at plants; Cowles got down and dirty, hiking the Indiana Dunes to figure out how plant communities change over time (that's ecological succession, folks!). His work was so groundbreaking, it's like he handed the scientific world a blueprint for understanding how ecosystems evolve. He literally helped shape our understanding of how life on Earth adapts and transforms. Pretty big deal for a guy who just liked sandy beaches and plants!
