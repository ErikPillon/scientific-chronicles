Elisha Gray was a super smart electrical engineer and inventor, best known for his intense rivalry with Alexander Graham Bell over the invention of the telephone. On February 14, 1876, literally hours after Bell, Gray filed a caveat for a telephone design – a 'what if' patent. While Bell ultimately secured the primary telephone patent, Gray held numerous patents for other significant electrical devices, especially in telegraphy, like the telautograph. He was a foundational figure in electric communications, even if he missed out on the telephone jackpot. His contributions laid much of the groundwork for the wired world we know today.
