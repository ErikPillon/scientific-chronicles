Clyde W. Tombaugh was an American astronomer, famous for doing what few others had done before him: discovering a planet! In 1930, at the tender age of 24, while working at the Lowell Observatory, he painstakingly compared photographic plates and spotted a tiny, moving speck that turned out to be Pluto. Talk about a glow-up for a farm boy from Illinois who started out building his own telescopes! While Pluto's planetary status has since been reclassified (ouch!), Tombaugh's systematic and diligent search methods were a triumph of observational astronomy. He wasn't just looking for something; he found a whole new corner of our solar system, proving that sometimes, the universe just needs a good detective.
