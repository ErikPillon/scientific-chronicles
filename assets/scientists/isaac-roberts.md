Isaac Roberts was the guy who taught the universe how to pose for a picture. This self-taught Welsh astronomer and successful businessman (who made his fortune in construction, oddly enough!) turned his considerable talent and resources to capturing the cosmos. Using custom-built telescopes and photographic plates, he pioneered long-exposure astrophotography, revealing structures in nebulae and galaxies that were simply invisible to the human eye, even through the most powerful telescopes of his day. His stunning images of Andromeda, Orion, and the Pleiades were groundbreaking, proving that photography was an indispensable tool for astronomy. He showed us that galaxies had spiral arms and nebulae had intricate filaments, basically mapping out the true beauty of the universe long before Hubble came along. A true visionary who brought the distant cosmos a little closer to home.
