Meet Erik H. Erikson, the guy who gave your mid-life crisis a name (or at least, a stage). Born in Germany, he became an American psychoanalyst who wasn't content just to analyze childhood; he decided to map out *all* of life. He gifted us the concept of identity crisis and showed how our personalities keep evolving through eight psychosocial stages, from infancy to old age. Before Erikson, Freud got all the glory for childhood drama, but Erikson said, 'Hold my beer,' and explained that we're grappling with trust, autonomy, initiative, and more, well into our golden years. He even wrote a Pulitzer-winning biography of Gandhi. Basically, he made growing up (and growing old) a lot more interesting – and a lot less linear than some thought.
