Bernard Brunhes, a brilliant French geophysicist born in 1867, had a mind that dug deep – literally! While studying ancient lava flows in Auvergne, France, he stumbled upon something utterly mind-boggling: some rocks were magnetized in the complete opposite direction to Earth's current magnetic field. Hold up, what?! This wasn't some geological anomaly; it was the first compelling evidence that our planet's magnetic poles actually *flip* over geological timescales. This discovery, now famously known as the Brunhes-Matuyama reversal (after his work and later confirmation by Motonori Matuyama), completely revolutionized our understanding of Earth's dynamic core and the timeline of its magnetic field. Sadly, Brunhes passed away relatively young in 1910, just as his groundbreaking insights were starting to shake up the scientific world. But his legacy? It's as solid as the rocks he studied, proving that sometimes, the biggest truths are found by looking at things backward.
