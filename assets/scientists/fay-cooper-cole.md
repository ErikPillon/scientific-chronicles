Fay-Cooper Cole was an American anthropologist who dug deep, both literally and figuratively, into human history. After getting his start studying the Indigenous peoples of the Philippines, he championed a scientific approach to anthropology, emphasizing meticulous fieldwork and data collection. He later became a leading figure at the University of Chicago, helping to establish one of the most respected anthropology departments in the US. Cole wasn't just observing cultures; he was building the framework for how we understand them, leaving a lasting legacy in the academic world.
