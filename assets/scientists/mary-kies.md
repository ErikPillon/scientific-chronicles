In a world where women's contributions were often overlooked or attributed to their male relatives, Mary Kies broke through with an innovation all her own. Born in 1752, this resourceful Connecticut woman earned the distinction of being the first woman to be granted a U.S. patent *in her own name* on May 5, 1809. Her invention? A clever technique for weaving straw with silk or thread to create more durable and fashionable hats. First Lady Dolley Madison herself lauded Kies's invention, recognizing its economic potential for American women who were seeking new ways to support themselves. So next time you appreciate a good accessory, remember Mary Kies – the original patent-holding fashionista!
