Before Chapin Aaron Harris, dentistry was a bit like the Wild West – full of grit, not always glorious, and certainly lacking in formal training. But along with Horace Hayden, Harris changed the game. He wasn't content with dentistry being a trade; he wanted it to be a respected profession rooted in science. He co-founded the Baltimore College of Dental Surgery in 1840, making it the world's very first dental school. This groundbreaking institution, along with the first dental journal he also helped establish, transformed dentistry from a craft into a legitimate medical discipline, setting the standard for professional dental education globally. Talk about a smile makeover!
