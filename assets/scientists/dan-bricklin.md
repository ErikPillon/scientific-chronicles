Before Excel, before Google Sheets, there was VisiCalc, and Dan Bricklin was the genius who cooked it up. Imagine doing financial projections by hand – a nightmare, right? Bricklin, along with Bob Frankston, created the first electronic spreadsheet program, turning chunky personal computers into indispensable business tools overnight. VisiCalc wasn't just software; it was a revelation, making calculations dynamic and accessible. It’s hard to overstate its impact; it essentially kickstarted the PC revolution, making computers useful for the average businessperson and showing the world what software could truly achieve. Thanks, Dan, for saving us from endless ledger sheets!
