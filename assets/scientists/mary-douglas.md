Dame Mary Douglas dove deep into the messy, fascinating world of human culture, turning abstract concepts like 'purity' and 'danger' into analytical tools. This British social anthropologist challenged us to see how societies create order out of chaos, often through rituals and classifications that seem baffling from the outside. Her book, 'Purity and Danger,' is legendary, showing how everything from dietary laws to taboos makes perfect sense within a cultural framework. She wasn't just observing people; she was decoding the hidden logic of human behavior, making us all think twice about what we consider 'normal' and shedding light on universal human patterns.
