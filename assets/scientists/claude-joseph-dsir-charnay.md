Before Instagram, there was Claude-Joseph-Désiré Charnay, a French explorer who wasn't just looking at ancient ruins – he was *capturing* them! A true pioneer, Charnay journeyed through Mexico and Central America in the mid-19th century, lugging heavy photographic equipment to places like Palenque and Chichen Itza. His stunning albumen prints provided some of the earliest, most detailed visual records of these magnificent, overgrown cities. His work wasn't just pretty pictures; it fueled global fascination with pre-Columbian civilizations and helped establish archaeology as a serious field, proving that sometimes, a picture really is worth a thousand digs.
