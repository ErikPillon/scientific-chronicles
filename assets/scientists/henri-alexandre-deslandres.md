Henri-Alexandre Deslandres was a French astronomer who loved the sun so much, he practically invented a way to take its picture in detail, layer by layer. He was a trailblazer in solar physics, renowned for developing the spectroheliograph (independently invented by George Ellery Hale around the same time). This incredible instrument allowed astronomers to photograph the sun in specific wavelengths of light, revealing features like prominences, flares, and plages that were previously invisible. Deslandres's work revolutionized our understanding of the sun's atmosphere and its dynamic activity, moving solar observation beyond simple sunspot counting to a sophisticated analysis of its physical processes. He spent decades directing observatories and pushing the boundaries of astronomical instrumentation, truly illuminating our closest star. He was basically the original Instagram influencer for the sun!
