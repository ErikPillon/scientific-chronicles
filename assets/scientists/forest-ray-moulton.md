Meet Forest Ray Moulton, the American astronomer and mathematician who loved thinking big – as in, whole planetary systems! Back in the early 20th century, he teamed up with geologist Thomas Chamberlin to cook up the 'planetesimal hypothesis,' suggesting that planets formed from tiny bits of matter clumping together. It was a cosmic dust-up idea that challenged older theories and helped shape our understanding of how worlds are born. Beyond theoretical physics, he was a whiz at celestial mechanics, charting the dance of planets with impressive mathematical precision. Basically, he was figuring out the universe's choreography before it was cool.
