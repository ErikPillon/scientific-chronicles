In 1963, a former textile factory worker and amateur parachutist from the Soviet Union, Valentina Tereshkova, became the first woman to journey into space. Piloting Vostok 6, she orbited Earth 48 times over three days, proving that the final frontier wasn't just for men. Her mission wasn't just a propaganda coup for the Soviets; it was a powerful statement about human potential and gender equality, inspiring countless girls to look up at the stars and dream big. Still a national hero, Tereshkova remains a living legend, forever etched in the annals of space history.
