Frans Cornelis Donders wasn't just your average 19th-century physician; he was an absolute titan of vision. Imagine a world where eyeglasses were more of a guessing game than a precise prescription – Donders changed that! He meticulously studied the eye, unraveling mysteries like accommodation (how your eye focuses on things) and astigmatism, essentially writing the textbook on modern ophthalmology. But wait, there's more! This polymath wasn't content just fixing eyeballs. He also dipped his toes into the nascent field of experimental psychology, becoming a pioneer in measuring mental processes. He famously conducted experiments to quantify reaction times, showing that different types of mental tasks (like choosing between responses) took measurable, distinct amounts of time. So, next time you put on your perfectly calibrated glasses or process information in a blink, give a nod to Donders – the man who made seeing, and thinking, a science.
