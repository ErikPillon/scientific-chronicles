Before there were electron microscopes and CRISPR, there was Christian Gottfried Ehrenberg, a German natural historian with an insatiable curiosity for the tiny, invisible world. Armed with a microscope, he meticulously documented thousands of species of infusoria (microorganisms like ciliates and flagellates) and diatoms. He even figured out that many rocks, like chalk, were essentially made up of fossilized microscopic creatures – an absolute revelation for geology and paleontology! Ehrenberg basically put micropaleontology on the map, showing us that even the smallest life forms have played a colossal role in shaping Earth's history and geology. He was the original 'micro-influencer' of the scientific world!
