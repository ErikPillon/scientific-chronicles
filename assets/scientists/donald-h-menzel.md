Donald H. Menzel was an American astronomer who spent a good chunk of his career staring intently at the sun – not literally, mind you, but through telescopes and spectra. He made significant contributions to solar physics, figuring out the composition and behavior of our closest star. Beyond the sun, he delved deep into the physics of gaseous nebulae, those stunning cosmic clouds where stars are born. Menzel wasn't just about the science; he was also known for his skepticism regarding UFOs, even chairing a government project to investigate them, concluding they were mostly misidentifications. So, whether you were interested in stellar mysteries or earthly hoaxes, Menzel was your go-to guy, always ready to shed some light on the subject with a healthy dose of scientific rigor.
