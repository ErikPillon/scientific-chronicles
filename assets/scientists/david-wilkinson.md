When you talk about big bang proof, you've got to mention David Wilkinson! This American astrophysicist was a key player in the team that confirmed the cosmic microwave background (CMB) radiation – the faint afterglow of the Big Bang itself. Working alongside Robert Dicke and others at Princeton, he helped build the instruments that essentially gave us a baby picture of the universe. From ground-based detectors to the groundbreaking COBE and WMAP satellites, Wilkinson spent his career meticulously studying these ancient ripples, helping us understand the universe's origin and evolution. He didn't just study the stars; he helped us hear the universe's first whispers. Talk about a cosmic achievement!
