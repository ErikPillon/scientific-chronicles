Born in 1786, Giovanni Amici was the kind of brilliant tinkerer who made the invisible visible. This Italian optician, astronomer, and microscopist was a master craftsman, renowned for significantly improving the design of optical instruments. He developed the "Amici prism," which is still used today in various optical devices, and revolutionized the microscope with his innovative achromatic and immersion objectives. Thanks to Amici, scientists could finally peer into the microscopic world with unprecedented clarity, making discoveries that were previously impossible. Not content with just the tiny, he also built advanced telescopes and made significant astronomical observations, including detailed drawings of Jupiter's moon Ganymede. Basically, if it involved a lens and seeing things better, Amici was your guy!
