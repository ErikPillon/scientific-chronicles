Georg Cantor wasn't just good at math; he redefined what math *could be*. Born in Russia but primarily active in Germany, this brilliant mathematician tackled the biggest concept of all: infinity. Before Cantor, infinity was just... infinity. But he dared to ask, 'Are some infinities bigger than others?' And guess what? He proved they are! He founded set theory and introduced transfinite numbers, showing that the number of points on a line is 'more infinite' than the number of whole numbers. This blew a lot of minds (and ruffled a few feathers among his peers, who initially found his ideas heretical). Despite facing significant criticism and mental health struggles, Cantor's revolutionary work ultimately provided the foundation for much of modern mathematics. He basically expanded the universe of numbers.
