Charles Greeley Abbot was an American astrophysicist who was literally obsessed with the Sun. For over six decades, he meticulously measured solar radiation and its potential impact on weather and climate, serving a whopping 27 years as Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution. He wasn't just tracking sunspots; he was trying to crack the code of our star's influence on Earth. While some of his specific theories might have been debated, his dedication to long-term data collection laid crucial groundwork for future solar research. Talk about a long-term commitment to sunshine!
