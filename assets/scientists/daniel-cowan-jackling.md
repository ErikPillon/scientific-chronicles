Daniel Cowan Jackling wasn't just a mining engineer; he was an industrial alchemist who turned low-grade rock into high-grade profits. This American pioneer revolutionized the copper industry by figuring out how to economically extract copper from massive deposits of very low-grade ore. Everyone else said it couldn't be done, but Jackling built giant open-pit mines and processing plants, making previously worthless land incredibly valuable. He essentially scaled up mining to an unprecedented degree, reshaping landscapes and the global copper supply with his innovative methods and sheer determination. A true titan of industry who dug deep, both literally and figuratively!
