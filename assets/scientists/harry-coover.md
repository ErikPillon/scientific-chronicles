Ever had a 'sticky' situation? Thank Harry Coover! This brilliant American chemist, working for Eastman Kodak in 1942, was actually trying to create clear plastic gun sights during WWII. Instead, he ended up with a substance that was... well, *too* sticky. He shelved it, deeming it a failure. Fast forward to 1951, and while trying to find a heat-resistant jet canopy material, he and his team rediscovered the incredibly adhesive cyanoacrylate formula. This time, they realized its potential. Voila! Super Glue was born. From fixing broken pottery to emergency medical stitches in Vietnam, Harry's 'failed' experiment became a household staple and a medical lifesaver. He certainly stuck with it in the end!
