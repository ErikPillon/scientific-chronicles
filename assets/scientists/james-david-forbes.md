If 'polymath' was a competitive sport, James David Forbes would have a shelf full of gold medals. This Scottish natural philosopher was a force of nature, dabbling brilliantly in physics, meteorology, geology, and, most famously, glaciology. He trekked across Alpine glaciers, meticulously mapping their movements and challenging existing theories on ice flow, becoming a founding father of modern glaciology. But wait, there's more! He also conducted pioneering experiments on the conduction of heat in metals, invented an early form of a seismometer, and made significant contributions to meteorology. Forbes was the kind of scientist who saw connections everywhere, proving that the world's natural phenomena were just waiting for a brilliant mind to unravel their secrets.
