Arno Penzias, a German-born American physicist, teamed up with Robert Wilson at Bell Labs, and together they accidentally stumbled upon the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) in 1964. They were trying to get rid of annoying static in their new antenna, even cleaning out pigeon poop, but that persistent hum wouldn't quit! Turns out, that 'noise' was the faint echo of the Big Bang, revolutionizing cosmology and confirming the universe's fiery beginning. Their accidental discovery earned them a Nobel Prize in Physics in 1978. It just goes to show, sometimes the greatest discoveries are hiding in plain sight (or rather, in the background static).
