Love a good sweet treat? You might want to thank Andreas Marggraf, the German chemist who basically made sure we didn't run out of sugar when cane sugar was scarce. In 1747, he made the revolutionary discovery that sugar found in beetroot was identical to that extracted from sugarcane. While it took his student, Franz Carl Achard, to make industrial production a reality, Marggraf’s initial breakthrough was the sweet beginning! He wasn't just a sugar detective; Marggraf was a pioneer in analytical chemistry, famous for his meticulous experimental methods and for isolating zinc from calamine using charcoal. He was the kind of chemist who could tell you what was hiding in your vegetables and how to get it out, changing agriculture and industry forever, one beet at a time.
