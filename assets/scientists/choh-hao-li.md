Choh Hao Li, a dazzling biochemist who left China for the US, became a rockstar in hormone research. He was the first to successfully isolate and then synthesize human growth hormone (hGH) in the 1950s and 60s. Before him, hGH was like a mythical beast, but Li lassoed it, opening doors for treating growth deficiencies and understanding the body's complex chemistry. Imagine, kids growing taller thanks to his lab wizardry! He basically taught us how hormones work their magic, one peptide at a time. A true chemical maestro!
