Charles Glen King was the biochemist who practically handed us our daily dose of vitamin C. This American scientist, along with his team, was a pioneer in the early 1930s, successfully isolating and identifying vitamin C (ascorbic acid) from lemon juice. This was a monumental breakthrough because it definitively proved that scurvy was a vitamin deficiency disease, not just some ancient mariner's curse. While Albert Szent-Györgyi also did crucial work around the same time, King's independent isolation was a major achievement that paved the way for understanding its chemical structure and synthesizing it. Thanks to King, getting your essential nutrients became a whole lot clearer – and probably tastier! He certainly put the 'C' in clarity.
