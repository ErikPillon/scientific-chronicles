Hayward A. Harvey was the kind of inventor who made battleships tougher than ever before. This American genius wasn't just fiddling in a workshop; he was fundamentally changing how navies protected their ships. His 'Harveyized' steel, developed in the late 19th century, was a total game-changer. It involved hardening the surface of steel plates while keeping the core tough, making them incredibly resistant to projectiles. Suddenly, battleships could take a hit and keep on ticking, thanks to Harvey's metallurgical smarts. He essentially gave the world's navies a serious upgrade in defensive power, influencing warship design for decades!
