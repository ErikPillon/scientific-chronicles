Ever heard of Pratt & Whitney? Well, one half of that legendary duo was Francis Pratt! Born in 1827, this American mechanical engineer and inventor was obsessed with precision. He co-founded the company that became synonymous with high-quality machine tools, which were absolutely essential for the burgeoning industrial age. Think about it: to build anything complex – from sewing machines to engines – you need incredibly accurate parts, and Pratt's innovations made that possible. He was one of those behind-the-scenes masterminds who didn't just build machines; he built the *machines that built other machines*. His legacy? Making things perfectly accurate, one gear at a time, establishing a standard for precision manufacturing that endures to this day.
