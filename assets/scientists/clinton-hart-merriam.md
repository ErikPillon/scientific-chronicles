Clinton Hart Merriam was a scientific jack-of-all-trades, a naturalist who literally charted the ecological landscape of North America! Best known as the founder and long-time chief of the U.S. Biological Survey (now part of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service), Merriam was obsessed with classifying and understanding the distribution of plants and animals. He developed the concept of "life zones," arguing that distinct biological communities exist at specific elevations and latitudes. But he wasn't just about flora and fauna; he also spent significant time documenting Native American cultures, particularly their languages and customs. He literally helped define how we understand ecosystems and preserve biodiversity across a continent!
