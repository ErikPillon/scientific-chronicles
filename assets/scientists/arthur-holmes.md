Arthur Holmes was a British geologist who basically invented the geological clock. He pioneered the use of radiometric dating – using radioactive decay to figure out the age of rocks – allowing scientists to finally pin down the Earth's true age and develop a robust geological timescale. Before him, estimates were all over the place! Not content with just dating the Earth, Holmes also championed the idea of continental drift, suggesting that convection currents in the Earth's mantle were the driving force behind the movement of continents. While his mantle convection idea was initially met with skepticism, it turned out to be spot on, laying the groundwork for plate tectonics. Holmes was truly a visionary who reshaped our understanding of Earth's dynamic history.
