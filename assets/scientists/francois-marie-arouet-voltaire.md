Voltaire was the ultimate intellectual rockstar of 18th-century France. This quick-witted, sharp-tongued philosopher wasn't afraid to poke fun at the powerful or challenge the status quo, especially when it came to religious dogma and political tyranny. While he didn't discover a new planet or invent a gizmo, he was a massive advocate for scientific reason, free inquiry, and critical thinking, famously popularizing Isaac Newton's ideas across Europe. He basically helped lay the groundwork for a world where asking 'why?' was celebrated, not condemned. Think of him as the guy who made intellectual curiosity cool, even if it meant a few stints in the Bastille. He constantly championed empirical observation and rational thought, pushing society away from superstition and towards a more enlightened, scientific outlook.
