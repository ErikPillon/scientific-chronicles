If ever there was a true Renaissance man, it was Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz! Born in 1646, this German powerhouse was a mathematician, philosopher, logician, and diplomat all rolled into one. He's famously known for independently developing calculus (yes, alongside Newton, and it was a whole drama!), but that's just the tip of his intellectual iceberg. Leibniz also pioneered early mechanical calculators (the stepped reckoner!), envisioned binary code, and proposed concepts that predate modern computer science. His philosophical contributions, like the monads and the idea of 'the best of all possible worlds,' still get thinkers scratching their heads today. Leibniz was a tireless genius, shaping multiple fields until his death in 1716.
