Clemens von Pirquet, an Austrian pediatrician with a knack for naming things, is the brainy chap who gifted us the term "allergy." Yes, every time your nose runs because of pollen, you can thank him! He noticed that some patients reacted differently to substances after a second exposure and decided to call this altered reaction "allergie" (meaning "other reaction"). But he didn't stop there; he also developed the revolutionary tuberculin test, a simple skin prick that could detect tuberculosis, a major killer of his era. He basically gave doctors a superpower to spot a hidden disease before it fully ravaged a patient. Pirquet was a true medical innovator, fundamentally changing our understanding of immune responses and giving us essential tools for diagnosis. His insights into hypersensitivity laid the groundwork for modern allergology, making life slightly less sneezy for generations to come.
