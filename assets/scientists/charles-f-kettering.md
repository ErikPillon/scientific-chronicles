Charles F. Kettering was basically Tony Stark before Iron Man was even a twinkle in a comic book artist's eye. This American inventor and engineer was a powerhouse of innovation, making driving a car far less strenuous. Tired of hand-cranking engines? Kettering invented the electric self-starter, making cars accessible to many more people (and saving a lot of broken arms). He also developed leaded gasoline (which had its problems, yes, but was a major advance at the time for engine performance), Freon refrigerants, and even made improvements to diesel engines. As head of research at General Motors, he essentially turbo-charged the auto industry and left an indelible mark on how we live, travel, and even keep our food cold. What a dynamo!
