George Baxter was a printing wizard from the 19th century who basically invented color printing as we knew it before digital. Imagine a world of black-and-white etchings, then suddenly, BAM! Full-color prints, thanks to his ingenious layering process using oil inks. He wasn't just slapping colors on; he developed a complex method, often using up to twenty different blocks, to achieve remarkable detail and richness. He made art accessible and added a splash of color to Victorian homes, long before Instagram made everything pretty. Talk about a true original who brought the rainbow to paper!
