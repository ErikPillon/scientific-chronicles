Charlie Duke isn't just a cool name; he's a genuine American hero who's been to the Moon and back! As the Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 16 in 1972, Charlie became the tenth person to stroll on the lunar surface, exploring the rugged Descartes Highlands. Before that giant leap, he was the voice of CAPCOM during Apollo 11's historic landing, famously causing Neil Armstrong to lose his cool for a second. His enthusiastic 'Magnificent!' upon first seeing the Moon from the LM window became legendary. Post-NASA, this former Air Force Brigadier General and test pilot became a successful businessman and a passionate Christian speaker. Charlie Duke represents the daring, intelligent spirit of the Apollo generation, reminding us that even amongst the stars, a little human awe goes a long way. He's still around, sharing tales of lunar dust and cosmic wonder!
