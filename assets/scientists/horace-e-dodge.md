Horace E. Dodge, along with his brother John, wasn't just a gearhead; he was a titan of early American industry! These two mechanical whizzes started their journey making bicycle parts, then moved on to engine and chassis components for the nascent automotive world. They were initially key suppliers to Henry Ford, basically building much of the Ford Model T's guts. But Horace wasn't one to stay in the shadows. Soon, the Dodge brothers decided, 'Why build parts for others when we can build our own fantastic cars?' And so, in 1914, the Dodge Brothers Company was born, quickly becoming a respected automaker known for its robust and reliable vehicles. Horace's engineering prowess and commitment to quality made 'Dodge' a household name. His impact on mass production and automotive design was huge, even if his life was cut tragically short. He and John revolutionized how cars were made and sold, putting America on wheels with grit and smarts.
