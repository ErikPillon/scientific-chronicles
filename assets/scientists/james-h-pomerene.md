These early behemoths pushed the boundaries of speed and architecture, laying the groundwork for the powerful computers we use today. Pomerene's expertise was crucial in making sure these complex contraptions actually worked, transforming theoretical designs into functional machines. He was the guy making the wires hum and the vacuum tubes glow, ensuring that the future of computing had a solid, working foundation.
