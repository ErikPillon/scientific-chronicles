While Otto Hahn often gets the lion's share of credit for nuclear fission, Fritz Strassmann was the meticulous chemist who performed the crucial experiments. In 1938, working with Hahn and Lise Meitner (who provided the theoretical explanation), Strassmann's chemical analysis showed that bombarding uranium with neutrons actually produced barium – a much lighter element. This was utterly baffling, defying all known physics, but Strassmann's chemistry was impeccable. Their findings confirmed that the uranium nucleus had split, unleashing the atomic age. He was the unsung hero who chemically proved the impossible was, in fact, happening!
