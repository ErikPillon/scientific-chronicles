If you've ever wondered why some industries are dominated by a few big players or how companies compete, you've probably encountered the ideas of F.M. Scherer (he usually goes by F.M.). This American economist is a titan in the field of industrial organization, essentially studying how industries are structured and how firms behave within them. His textbooks are legendary, and his research has deeply influenced antitrust policy and how we think about competition, innovation, and corporate strategy. Since the latter half of the 20th century, Scherer has been the go-to guy for understanding the nitty-gritty economics of firms and markets, making sense of the business world with rigorous analysis.
