Arnold Sommerfeld was a German theoretical physicist who, despite never winning a Nobel Prize himself (a travesty, many would say!), mentored more Nobel laureates than almost anyone else – including Heisenberg, Pauli, and Bethe! He was a quantum mechanics rockstar, extending Niels Bohr's atomic model to include elliptical orbits and relativistic effects, introducing the fine-structure constant, and making huge strides in X-ray diffraction theory. His influence was immense, shaping the next generation of quantum physicists. So, while he might not have a golden medal, his legacy shines brightly through the scientific giants he guided.
