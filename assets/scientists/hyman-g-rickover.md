Meet Hyman G. Rickover, the no-nonsense, notoriously demanding, yet undeniably brilliant admiral who almost single-handedly dragged the U.S. Navy into the nuclear age. Born in Poland and immigrating to the U.S. as a child, Rickover graduated from the Naval Academy and became an electrical engineer. But his true calling came after WWII when he became the driving force behind the development of the nuclear submarine. He was a micro-manager, a perfectionist, and terrified people (including presidents!), but his relentless pursuit of excellence and safety meant that the USS Nautilus, the world's first nuclear submarine, launched flawlessly. He built a nuclear program from scratch, personally interviewing every officer for nuclear duty, ensuring a legacy of unmatched safety and operational success. Love him or fear him, Rickover reshaped naval power forever, proving that sometimes, being a bit of a curmudgeon is exactly what you need to make history.
