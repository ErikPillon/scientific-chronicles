Archie Carr was essentially the sea turtle's best friend. This American zoologist spent his life studying these ancient mariners, especially their epic migrations and nesting habits in places like Costa Rica. His groundbreaking research didn't just fill textbooks; it kicked off the entire global sea turtle conservation movement. Before Archie, few truly grasped the intricate lives and growing plight of these majestic creatures. Thanks to his relentless passion and advocacy, countless turtles got a fighting chance, proving that one person's dedication can really make monumental waves for an entire species.
