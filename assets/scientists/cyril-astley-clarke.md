Dr. Cyril Astley Clarke was a British physician and geneticist who practically invented a medical superpower: preventing Rhesus disease. His groundbreaking work, alongside Ronald Finn, led to the development of anti-D immunoglobulin, a treatment that transformed a previously devastating condition into a largely preventable one, saving millions of lives worldwide. Not content with just human genetics, he also had a fascinating sideline studying butterfly mimicry, proving that some scientific minds can effortlessly flit between saving babies and unraveling nature's evolutionary tricks. A true polymath and life-saver!
