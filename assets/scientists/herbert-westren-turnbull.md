Herbert Westren Turnbull was a Scottish mathematician who loved to dive deep into the elegant structures of algebra. As Regius Professor of Mathematics at the University of St Andrews, he became a leading authority on invariant theory, a branch of abstract algebra that deals with properties that remain unchanged under certain transformations. If that sounds complicated, it's because it is! His textbooks were legendary for their clarity and rigor, introducing generations of students to the beauty of higher algebra. Turnbull might not have discovered a new planet, but he certainly helped map out the universe of mathematical abstraction.
