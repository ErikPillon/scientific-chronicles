Meet Fang Lizhi, the brilliant Chinese astrophysicist who wasn't afraid to speak his mind, earning him the moniker "China's Sakharov." Born in 1936, Fang was a leading figure in cosmology, making significant contributions to our understanding of the early universe. But his scientific prowess was matched by his courage as a dissident and human rights activist. He openly advocated for democratic reforms and freedom of speech in China, inspiring student movements in the 1980s. After the Tiananmen Square crackdown in 1989, he and his wife sought asylum in the U.S. embassy, eventually moving to the West. Fang Lizhi's life was a powerful fusion of scientific genius and unwavering moral conviction, demonstrating that the pursuit of truth extends far beyond the laboratory. A true intellectual heavyweight with a conscience.
