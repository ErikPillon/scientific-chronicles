Heinrich Schliemann, born in 1822, was the ultimate go-getter: a self-made millionaire who decided to become an archaeologist *because he loved Homer*. Talk about passion projects! This German adventurer wasn't just digging for old pots; he was on a quest to find the legendary city of Troy, armed with ancient texts and a pickaxe. And guess what? He found it! Or at least, he found a significant Bronze Age settlement at Hisarlik, Turkey, that he confidently declared Troy. While his methods were... shall we say, a bit enthusiastic and destructive by modern standards, Schliemann's discoveries electrified the world and sparked a global fascination with classical archaeology. He proved that sometimes, you just gotta believe in ancient myths and start digging!
