Florence Barbara Seibert, born in 1897, was a chemist who made a colossal impact on public health, all thanks to a tiny protein. She's most famous for isolating and purifying the active agent for the tuberculosis skin test – a protein derivative called PPD (purified protein derivative). Before her meticulous work, TB tests were notoriously unreliable, leading to misdiagnoses. Seibert's biochemical wizardry meant that doctors could finally accurately detect TB, leading to earlier treatment and preventing the spread of this devastating disease globally. She literally standardized a test that saved millions of lives! Talk about making a difference with a bit of lab work. Her dedication to precision in an era of widespread TB was nothing short of heroic.
