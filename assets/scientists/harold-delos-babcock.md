Ever wonder why the Sun has spots and does its magnetic flip-flop? Thank Harold Delos Babcock, an American astronomer who spent his life staring at our star (safely, of course). Alongside his son Horace, he was a trailblazer in solar spectroscopy, meticulously mapping the Sun's magnetic fields. Their most famous brainchild is the Babcock model, which explains the Sun's 22-year magnetic cycle and the migration of sunspots. Basically, they gave us a cosmic peek under the Sun's magnetic hood, helping us understand the fiery processes that can even mess with our tech here on Earth. Not bad for someone who just wanted to watch the stars!
