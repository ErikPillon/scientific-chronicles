Frank J. Zamboni was the quiet genius who made ice hockey and figure skating possible for millions without anyone ever noticing the ice. Back when resurfacing a rink was a back-breaking, time-consuming chore involving shovels and squeegees, Zamboni looked at the problem and said, 'There has to be a better way!' In 1949, he unveiled his groundbreaking ice resurfacing machine, which transformed the bumpy, scuffed surface of a rink into a pristine sheet of glass in minutes. His invention, universally known by his surname, became an indispensable part of ice sports, proving that sometimes the greatest innovations are those that simply make life smoother, literally. He truly kept the ice nice!
