Ever been saved by a quick-thinking medic with a zappy device? You can thank Frank Pantridge for that! This Northern Irish cardiologist, a true hero of emergency medicine, practically revolutionized pre-hospital care. After serving as a POW during WWII, he brought his battlefield ingenuity to heart attacks, inventing the portable defibrillator in 1965. This game-changer meant life-saving treatment could start right at the scene, rather than waiting for the hospital. He’s the reason ambulances aren’t just taxis to the ER, but mobile life-support units. Pantridge's invention has zapped countless people back from the brink, proving that sometimes, the best ideas are the ones you can carry – especially when they restart a heart.
