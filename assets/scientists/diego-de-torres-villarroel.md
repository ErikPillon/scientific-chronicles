Ever met a person who's a mathematician, astronomer, astrologer, doctor, writer, and a bit of a rogue, all rolled into one? Say hello to Diego de Torres Villarroel, Spain's 18th-century answer to a Renaissance man with a twist. This Salamanca-born polymath held the chair of mathematics at his university, published popular almanacs filled with astronomical predictions (and some astrology, because why not?), and even practiced medicine without a formal degree – often with a surprising degree of success. His autobiography is a wild ride of adventures, misadventures, and encounters with both high society and the underworld. He was a true intellectual showman, proving that genius doesn't always come in a neat, specialized package.
