Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel was the rockstar of 19th-century astronomy who finally solved one of the universe's biggest riddles: how far away are the stars? In 1838, this German genius made history by being the first to successfully measure stellar parallax, determining the distance to the star 61 Cygni. This was a monumental feat, proving that stars weren't just fixed points on a celestial sphere but incredibly distant suns. Beyond that, he also developed 'Bessel functions,' which are still super useful in physics and engineering today, and meticulously studied the motions of stars, discovering anomalies that hinted at unseen companions. Talk about a true star player!
