Frank Henry Westheimer was one of those chemists who loved to figure out *how* molecules do what they do. He was a giant in physical organic chemistry, meticulously exploring reaction mechanisms and the forces that govern molecular behavior. His groundbreaking research provided deep insights into how enzymes work their magic in biological systems, essentially decoding nature's tiny machines. Beyond the lab, Westheimer was a celebrated educator, mentoring generations of chemists and inspiring them with his rigorous approach and intellectual curiosity. He wasn't just mixing chemicals; he was demystifying the fundamental dances of atoms and molecules, making complex processes understandable and elegant.
