Imagine being a miller's son, largely self-taught, and then inventing mathematical concepts so profound that they underpin massive swathes of modern physics. That's George Green for you! This brilliant British mathematician from the early 19th century gave us "Green's Theorem" and "Green's functions," which are indispensable tools in everything from electromagnetism to quantum mechanics. His work on potential theory essentially provided the mathematical language for understanding forces like gravity and electricity. He only published one major work in his lifetime, and it was initially ignored, but later championed by giants like Lord Kelvin. Green was a true genius who proved that formal education isn't always the only path to changing the world.
