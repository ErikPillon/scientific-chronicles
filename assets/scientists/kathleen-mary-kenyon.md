Meet Dame Kathleen Kenyon, the archaeology queen who wasn't afraid to get her hands dirty! She redefined archaeological methods, insisting on meticulous stratigraphic excavation – basically, digging down layer by layer, understanding that history isn't flat. Her starring role? Unearthing the real story of ancient Jericho, proving it was way older and more complex than anyone imagined. Forget biblical trumpets; Kenyon revealed a continuous settlement dating back to 9000 BC, making Jericho one of the world's oldest cities. She basically gave history a massive age-up, and did it with style, earning her damehood for her groundbreaking contributions.
