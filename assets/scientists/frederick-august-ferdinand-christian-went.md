Ever wonder how plants know which way is up and how to grow? Meet F.A.F.C. Went, the Dutch botanist who basically cracked the code on plant hormones, specifically auxins. His experiments in the early 20th century, particularly with oat coleoptiles, proved that these chemical messengers regulated plant growth. Before Went, it was all a bit of a leafy mystery. He didn't just discover auxins; he figured out how to isolate and quantify them, laying the groundwork for modern plant physiology. So, next time you see a plant bending towards the sun, give a nod to Went for explaining the molecular magic behind it. A true green thumb with a scientific mind!
