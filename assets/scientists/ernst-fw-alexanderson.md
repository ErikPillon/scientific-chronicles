If you're reading this on a screen or listening to music wirelessly, you can thank Ernst F.W. Alexanderson! This Swedish-American electrical engineering wizard, born in 1878, was a bona fide innovation machine, racking up over 300 patents. He basically had a hand in almost everything electric during the early 20th century. Alexanderson built the first Alexanderson alternator for continuous-wave radio transmission, making transatlantic voice broadcasting possible. He also developed critical technologies for television, including the Alexanderson mechanical television system. From making radio 'talk' across oceans to paving the way for our binge-watching habits, this guy was truly wired for greatness!
