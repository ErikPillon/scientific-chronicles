Ever enjoy crisp, clear audio or reliable electronic devices? You've got Harold Stephen Black to thank! While taking a ferry to work in 1927, this brilliant Bell Labs engineer had a eureka moment and scribbled down the concept for the negative feedback amplifier. Before Black's breakthrough, electronic signals were noisy and distorted, making long-distance communication a real headache. His invention dramatically reduced distortion in amplifiers, making everything from radio to telephone calls much clearer and more efficient. It's a foundational concept in almost all modern electronics, meaning Black basically made our gadgets sing better. What a commute!
