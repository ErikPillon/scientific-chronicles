Dennis Robert Hoagland was the ultimate plant whisperer, but instead of talking to them, he figured out exactly what they *ate*. This American plant physiologist, working at the University of California, Berkeley, became a legend for developing "Hoagland's solution" in the 1930s. It's a carefully balanced cocktail of mineral nutrients that allows plants to thrive without a single speck of soil, becoming the gold standard for hydroponics and plant research globally. Essentially, he gave botanists and gardeners a precise recipe for plant sustenance, unlocking new possibilities for agriculture and scientific study. He proved that sometimes, the best soil isn't soil at all!
