Georg Christian Füchsel, a German physician by trade, clearly found rocks more fascinating than prescribing tinctures! Born in 1722, he became an unsung hero of geology, often credited as one of the very first to truly grasp stratigraphy – the study of rock layers. Back when people thought the Earth was a few thousand years old, Füchsel was busy mapping the Thuringian Forest, carefully noting the order in which rock formations appeared. In 1761, he published what many consider to be the first genuine geological map, complete with cross-sections and descriptions of how layers were laid down over time. He wasn't just looking at rocks; he was reading Earth's diary, layer by sedimentary layer, proving that our planet had a much longer and more complex story than previously imagined.
