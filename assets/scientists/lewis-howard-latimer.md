Forget Edison – meet Lewis Latimer! This brilliant American inventor and draftsman was a key player in electrifying the world, literally. Born to formerly enslaved parents, Latimer's ingenuity helped make electric light practical and affordable. His 1881 patent for a durable carbon filament dramatically improved light bulbs, making them last longer and brighter. He also worked on telephone technology with Alexander Graham Bell and was the only Black member of Edison's 'Pioneers' team. A true polymath, Latimer didn't just invent; he also wrote a technical drawing textbook! He was the quiet force behind a lot of the bright ideas that shaped the modern age.
