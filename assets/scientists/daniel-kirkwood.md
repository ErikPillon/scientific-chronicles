Meet Daniel Kirkwood, the American astronomer who looked at the asteroid belt and said, 'Hold up, where'd all the asteroids go here?' He didn't just spot the gaps; he figured out why they were there! In the 1860s, he connected these asteroid-free zones to orbital resonances with Jupiter – basically, Jupiter's massive gravity was nudging rocks out of those specific paths. His 'Kirkwood Gaps' were a huge deal, showing that celestial mechanics were tidier than we thought and giving us a peek into the gravitational dance happening out in space. Not bad for a guy who was also a pretty successful math professor!
