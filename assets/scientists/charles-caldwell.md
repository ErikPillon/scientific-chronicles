Dr. Charles Caldwell was an early American physician with a personality as potent as his preferred remedies (which sometimes involved mercury, yikes!). Born in North Carolina, he was a fiery advocate for a rather... *heady* science: phrenology. This was the idea that personality traits and intelligence could be read from bumps on your skull. While we now know phrenology is pure bunk, Caldwell was its rockstar promoter in the US, carving out a big, albeit ultimately misguided, academic niche for himself. He founded the Louisville Medical Institute, leaving a complicated legacy of both pioneering medical education and championing pseudoscience. A true character, if not always scientifically sound!
