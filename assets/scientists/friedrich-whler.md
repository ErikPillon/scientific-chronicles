Before Friedrich Wöhler came along, many believed life possessed a mystical 'vital force' necessary to create organic stuff. Think of it as a biological secret sauce that only living things could whip up. Then, in 1828, Wöhler, a German chemist, casually synthesized urea (you know, the stuff in pee) from purely inorganic ingredients in his lab. Boom! Vitalism theory shattered, organic chemistry born, and the scientific world realized there was no magic required, just good old atoms and molecules. He basically taught chemistry how to pee without a bladder, and that's pretty legendary.
