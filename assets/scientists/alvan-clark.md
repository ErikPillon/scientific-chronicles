Alvan Clark started his career as a successful portrait painter, but his true calling lay in grinding glass and polishing mirrors – specifically, making world-class telescope lenses. With his sons, he founded Alvan Clark & Sons, a company that became synonymous with optical excellence. They crafted some of the largest and most powerful refracting telescope lenses of the 19th century, including the legendary 36-inch refractor for Lick Observatory and the 40-inch for Yerkes Observatory (the largest ever successfully operated). These weren't just big; they were incredibly precise, allowing astronomers to peer deeper into the cosmos than ever before. So, next time you see a stunning astronomical image, remember Clark, the painter who helped us truly see the stars.
