Ferruccio Lamborghini was no ordinary tractor manufacturer. This Italian engineer and industrialist was a man of passion, precision, and apparently, very strong opinions on clutches! Legend has it, he owned several Ferraris but found their clutches lacking. When he took his grievances directly to Enzo Ferrari, he was famously dismissed and told to stick to tractors. 'Oh, really?' thought Ferruccio, and thus, a legendary rivalry and an iconic car brand were born. He started building his own grand tourers, focusing on luxury, power, and distinct styling. Lamborghini vehicles became synonymous with daring design and incredible performance, forever cementing Ferruccio's place as a titan of the automotive world. Talk about turning a personal slight into a global phenomenon!
