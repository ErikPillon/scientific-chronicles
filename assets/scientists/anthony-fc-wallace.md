Anthony F.C. Wallace was an American anthropologist who dove deep into the complex psychology of cultural change. He was fascinated by 'revitalization movements' – those periods when a community feels threatened and tries to build a more satisfying culture. His work with the Iroquois, especially his biography of Handsome Lake, a Seneca prophet, offered incredible insights into how individuals and groups adapt, innovate, or sometimes resist change. Wallace brilliantly blended anthropology with psychology, showing that culture isn't just a set of rules, but a dynamic, psychological phenomenon. His contributions helped shape the field, reminding us that behind every cultural shift are real people grappling with big ideas.
