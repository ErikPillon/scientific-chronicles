Frank Duryea was one half of the pioneering duo who kickstarted the American automobile industry. While his brother Charles often gets more credit, it was Frank, the mechanical wizard, who largely designed and built America's first successful gasoline-powered car in 1893. He then drove it in the first American automobile race, proving that these noisy, horseless carriages were more than just a novelty. Frank was the hands-on genius who translated theoretical ideas into working machines that would change transportation forever. Without his practical engineering skills, the Duryea Motor Wagon Company might never have rolled off the ground, leaving us all still waiting for a carriage.
