Ever wonder who taught trees how to tell time? That'd be Andrew Ellicott Douglass! This American astronomer was happily stargazing when he stumbled upon a cosmic truth closer to Earth: tree rings are like nature's almanacs. He didn't just look up; he looked sideways, founding dendrochronology – the science of dating and climate studies using tree rings. Suddenly, ancient ruins and forgotten climates weren't so mysterious. Thanks to Douglass, we can now peek into the past, one growth ring at a time, proving that sometimes, the biggest discoveries are literally rooted in the ground beneath our feet. Who knew logs could hold so many secrets?
