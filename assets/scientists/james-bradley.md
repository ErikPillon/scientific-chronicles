Before GPS, there was James Bradley, the British astronomer who basically invented cosmic triangulation! He spent years squinting through telescopes, observing faint stellar wobbles. His big 'Aha!' moment? Realizing these tiny shifts were stellar aberration, caused by Earth's own jaunty trip around the Sun. Oh, and he also figured out the nutation (a fancy word for a small wobble) of Earth's axis. So, next time you marvel at our planet's consistent orbit, give a silent nod to Bradley, who proved we're definitely on the move, long before anyone felt the g-force.
