Ashley Montagu was the kind of anthropologist who didn't just study humanity; he championed it. Born in London as Israel Ehrenberg, he later became a naturalized American and spent his career dismantling pseudoscientific notions of race. He argued passionately that 'race' is a social construct, not a biological reality, significantly influencing the UNESCO statement on race. A true humanist, he also wrote extensively on the importance of cooperation, touch, and love in human development. He was basically the scientific mic drop to outdated, harmful racial theories, proving that kindness and rigorous scholarship can change the world.
