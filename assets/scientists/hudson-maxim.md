Forget the machine gun, that was his brother Hiram. Hudson Maxim was the brains behind the *bang*! This American inventor was a true explosives enthusiast, responsible for significant advancements in smokeless powder and other propellants in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. His inventions were crucial for modernizing artillery and ammunition, making them more powerful and reliable. He wasn't just about destruction, though; he also dabbled in torpedoes and other military innovations. Hudson was a prolific patent-holder, always tinkering and pushing the boundaries of explosive chemistry. He basically helped define the sound and fury of modern warfare.
