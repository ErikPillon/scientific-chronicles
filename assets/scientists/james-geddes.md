James Geddes was one of those brilliant but often unsung heroes who quietly laid the groundwork for future technological marvels. This Scottish engineer was dabbling in electrical telegraphy long before Samuel Morse made it a household name, even building one of the earliest working telegraph systems for communication within mines in the late 18th century. He was essentially a 19th-century tech pioneer, using his formidable ingenuity to solve practical problems in burgeoning industries like mining and communication. While he might not have the widespread name recognition of later inventors, his early contributions helped spark the communication revolution, proving that sometimes, the quiet innovators are the ones who truly set the stage for monumental change.
