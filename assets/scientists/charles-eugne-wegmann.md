Charles Eugène Wegmann was a Swiss geologist who delved deep into the Earth's crust, quite literally. He spent his career unraveling the mysteries of structural geology, especially how crystalline rocks form and deform under immense pressure and heat. His field work often took him to the rugged terrains of Scandinavia and Greenland, where he meticulously mapped and interpreted complex geological structures. Wegmann wasn't just a rock-hound; he was a master interpreter of the Earth's ancient stories, helping us understand the immense forces that shape our planet over millions of years. If rocks could talk, they'd probably tell you Charles Wegmann knew their secrets best.
