Meet Ernst Engel, the German statistician and economist who knew a thing or two about your grocery bill! Born in 1821, Engel wasn't just crunching numbers; he was uncovering fundamental truths about how we spend our hard-earned cash. He's famous for 'Engel's Law,' which observed that as a household's income increases, the proportion of income spent on food decreases, even if the absolute amount spent on food rises. Basically, he figured out why you might buy more organic kale when you get a raise, but probably not twice as much kale. His work provided critical insights into consumer behavior and living standards, making him a foundational figure in economic statistics. Who knew budgeting could be so historically significant?
