Herbert Hall Turner was a man who looked at the stars and deep into the Earth. This British astronomer was a major player at the Royal Greenwich Observatory and later as Savilian Professor of Astronomy at Oxford. He's famously credited with helping coin the term 'parsec' (a distance unit for the truly massive cosmic scale). But he wasn't just about distant stars; he also applied his mathematical prowess to seismology, analyzing earthquake data. From measuring vast cosmic distances to understanding Earth's shivers, Turner proved that sometimes the biggest thinkers have the broadest interests!
