Charles Dillon Perrine was an American astronomer with a truly cosmic resume! Working at Lick Observatory and later directing the Argentine National Observatory, Perrine was a comet-hunting, moon-discovering machine. He's credited with the discovery of two of Jupiter's irregular moons, Himalia and Elara, back in 1904-1905, proving there was more to our solar system than previously thought. He also found a string of comets and contributed to early photography of the solar corona during eclipses. Perrine was a true pioneer in astrophotography and telescopic observation, staring into the dark vastness and continually pulling out shiny new pieces of the universe for us to marvel at.
