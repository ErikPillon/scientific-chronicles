Herbert A. Simon was the kind of genius who made other geniuses feel a bit… specialized. This American polymath was a Nobel laureate (Economics, 1978) whose work spanned cognitive science, AI, computer science, psychology, and more! He challenged the idea of perfectly rational decision-making with his concept of 'bounded rationality,' showing how real humans make decisions with limited information and time. He also co-developed some of the earliest AI programs, like Logic Theory Machine and GPS (General Problem Solver). Simon didn't just think across disciplines; he built bridges between them, fundamentally changing how we understand minds – both human and artificial.
