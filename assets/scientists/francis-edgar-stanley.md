Forget gasoline; for a while, steam ruled the roads thanks to inventors like Francis Edgar Stanley. Along with his identical twin brother, Freelan Oscar Stanley, these American polymaths were first successful photographers (they invented the dry plate photographic process!). But their real fame came from their passion for automobiles. They founded the Stanley Motor Carriage Company, producing the iconic 'Stanley Steamer,' a magnificent, quiet, and powerful steam-powered car that set speed records and was beloved by early motorists. Francis was the engineering brain, constantly tinkering and improving their designs. While internal combustion eventually won the day, the Stanley Steamer was a testament to their innovative spirit and mechanical genius. They were basically the Elon Musks of the early 20th century, but with more steam and less tweeting.
