George Carruthers was an astrophysicist whose work literally gave us a new way to see the universe. This brilliant mind, who just happened to be Black, engineered the groundbreaking far-ultraviolet camera/spectrograph. Why was it a big deal? Because it allowed us to look at celestial objects and Earth's own atmosphere in ultraviolet light, a spectrum invisible to the human eye. His crowning achievement? Getting his invention flown on Apollo 16 in 1972, making it the first instrument to capture images of Earth's geocorona and deep space in UV from the Moon. He truly expanded our cosmic vision!
