Henry Darwin Rogers, born in 1808, was the sort of geologist who wasn't afraid to get his hands dirty – or his boots muddy – charting entire states. As the first state geologist of Pennsylvania and later New Jersey, he produced monumental geological surveys and maps, giving folks an unprecedented look at what lay beneath their feet. He was also a fierce advocate for the then-radical idea of an "ice age" (glacial theory) long before it was widely accepted. While his name might not be as common as some other scientific rockstars, his dedication to mapping and understanding Earth's history was truly foundational for American geology.
