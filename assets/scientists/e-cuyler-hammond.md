Before E. Cuyler Hammond, the link between puffing away and poor health was a bit... hazy. This American epidemiologist, a true detective of public health, spent his career meticulously gathering data to prove what many suspected. In the 1950s and beyond, his groundbreaking studies (like the famous Hammond-Horn study) conclusively demonstrated the devastating effects of smoking, linking it directly to lung cancer, heart disease, and countless other ailments. His work wasn't just academic; it provided the irrefutable evidence that fueled public health campaigns and eventually led to warning labels and smoking restrictions. Hammond wasn't just a scientist; he was a lifesaver, arming the world with the facts needed to choose health over habit. Talk about impactful research!
