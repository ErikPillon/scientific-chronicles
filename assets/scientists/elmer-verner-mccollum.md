Elmer McCollum was a biochemist with a serious detective streak, always on the hunt for the hidden ingredients that make us tick. While working with rats (the unsung heroes of nutritional science!), he systematically uncovered what he initially called 'accessory food factors.' These weren't just any factors; they were vitamins A, B, and D! His discoveries in the early 20th century were nothing short of revolutionary, proving that tiny amounts of specific compounds were absolutely crucial for health, growth, and preventing nasty diseases like rickets and scurvy. McCollum's work launched the 'age of vitamins,' forever changing how we think about diet, health, and what goes into our breakfast cereal. He literally put the 'V' in vital nutrition, making sure we all know our kale from our cod liver oil.
