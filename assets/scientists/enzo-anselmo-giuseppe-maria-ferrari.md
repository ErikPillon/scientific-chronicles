Enzo Ferrari wasn't just a car guy; he was *the* car guy, a genuine titan of automotive engineering and racing. Born in Modena, Italy, in 1898, he started out as a race driver, but quickly realized his true calling was building faster, more beautiful machines. In 1929, he founded Scuderia Ferrari, initially to provide race cars for gentlemen drivers, eventually evolving into the iconic Ferrari brand we know today. His obsession with speed, performance, and design led to some of the most legendary vehicles ever conceived, both on the track and on the road. A complex, often demanding character, Enzo's relentless pursuit of perfection cemented Ferrari's place in history as a symbol of luxury, innovation, and unparalleled engineering prowess. He didn't just build cars; he built dreams on four wheels!
