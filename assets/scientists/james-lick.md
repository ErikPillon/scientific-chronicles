James Lick was less of a scientist and more of a science enabler, a true visionary with incredibly deep pockets! This eccentric American millionaire and real estate mogul didn't discover a new comet, but he made it possible for others to do so. He famously willed a massive portion of his fortune to public works and scientific endeavors, with his most significant legacy being the Lick Observatory. He insisted it be 'superior to and more powerful than any in the world,' and indeed, it housed the largest refracting telescope of its time. Lick even demanded he be buried at the observatory site, right under the telescope pier, proving his commitment to the stars was literally lifelong. Without his incredible generosity, the cosmos might have remained a little more mysterious for a while longer.
