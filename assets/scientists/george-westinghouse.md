Move over, Edison, there was another electrification titan in town! George Westinghouse was an American mechanical and electrical engineer, and a true industrial pioneer. While Edison was pushing direct current (DC), Westinghouse championed alternating current (AC) – a system that proved far more efficient for transmitting power over long distances. This 'War of the Currents' was epic, but Westinghouse's AC system ultimately won, quite literally lighting up America and the world. Beyond electricity, he also invented the railway air brake, making train travel infinitely safer. His knack for innovation wasn't just about gadgets; it was about building entire systems that transformed daily life and industrial progress. He truly harnessed the power of big ideas!
