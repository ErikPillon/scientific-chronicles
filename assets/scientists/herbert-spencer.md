Herbert Spencer was the kind of intellectual powerhouse who had opinions on *everything* – and published them extensively. This British philosopher and polymath was a towering figure in Victorian thought, applying evolutionary principles not just to biology but to sociology, psychology, and even ethics. He's famous for coining the phrase "survival of the fittest" (yes, before Darwin published *Origin of Species*!) and for his grand vision of universal evolution. His "synthetic philosophy" aimed to unify all knowledge, making him a truly ambitious and influential, if sometimes controversial, figure in intellectual history. He basically tried to explain the entire universe from A to Z!
