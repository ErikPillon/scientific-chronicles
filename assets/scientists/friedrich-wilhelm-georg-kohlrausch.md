Friedrich Wilhelm Georg Kohlrausch was a physicist who made electricity behave! This German scientist became a guru of precision measurement, especially when it came to understanding how well different solutions conduct electricity. He developed incredibly accurate methods and instruments to measure electrical conductivity, and in doing so, formulated 'Kohlrausch's Law.' This nifty rule explains how the conductivity of an electrolyte solution is the sum of the individual contributions of its ions, independent of each other. His work was fundamental to the development of electrochemistry, helping us understand everything from batteries to biological processes. He truly got to the bottom of how ions do their electric dance!
