If you ever wondered who kept time for the British Empire, Edward John Dent was your man! This 19th-century horological genius crafted some of the most accurate marine chronometers, which were absolutely vital for naval navigation and exploration. His instruments were so good, they helped expeditions map the world and won him acclaim from the Admiralty. Oh, and his company, E. J. Dent & Co., won the contract to build the clock for the iconic Big Ben at the Houses of Parliament, though he sadly passed away before its completion. Talk about a lasting legacy!
