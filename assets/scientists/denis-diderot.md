Denis Diderot, the original curator of knowledge, was a French philosopher who basically invented the internet before electricity. He was the driving force behind the Encyclopédie, a massive, multi-volume beast of a publication that aimed to compile all human knowledge – from philosophy and arts to, crucially, science and technology – and make it accessible. Talk about an ambitious project! In an era when knowledge was often hoarded, Diderot and his co-conspirators fought censorship and opposition to spread enlightenment far and wide. He believed that understanding the world, including its scientific workings, was the key to human progress. So, next time you Google something, give a little nod to Diderot – he got the ball rolling on democratizing information.
