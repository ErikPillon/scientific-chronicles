Henri-Gaston Busignies, a French-American electrical engineering wizard, was the kind of guy who made planes talk to towers (and each other) much more reliably. Born in France, he crossed the pond and ended up shaping modern air traffic control with his brilliant radio and radar systems. He made sure pilots didn't get lost, even before GPS was a twinkle in anyone's eye. His work with ITT was pure genius, making the skies a safer, less confusing place. This fellow truly had a knack for bringing clarity to the airwaves.
