Herbert Spencer Gasser, an American physiologist, was like the ultimate detective for your nervous system. Along with Joseph Erlanger, he snagged a Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 1944 for their discoveries concerning the functional differentiation of single nerve fibres. Basically, they figured out that not all nerve fibers are created equal; they have different speeds and characteristics. Using fancy oscilloscopes (for the time!), they could measure the tiny electrical impulses that zoom along nerves, revealing how our brains and bodies communicate. Gasser helped us understand the electrical symphony happening inside us!
