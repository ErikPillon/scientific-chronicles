Before penicillin, there was George Miller Sternberg, a true germ warfare hero (the good kind!). This American bacteriologist and physician was practically the father of bacteriology in the U.S. He was one of the first to photograph bacteria and, get this, he independently discovered the pneumococcus, the bug responsible for pneumonia, just a year after Pasteur! He even experimented with vaccines, injecting himself with attenuated yellow fever cultures. Later, as U.S. Army Surgeon General, he modernized military medicine, emphasizing hygiene and disease prevention. Sternberg proved that sometimes, the biggest battles are fought against the tiniest enemies, armed with a microscope and immense courage.
