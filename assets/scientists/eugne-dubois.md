Talk about a doctor who wasn't content with just patching people up! Eugène Dubois was a Dutch army surgeon who, in the late 19th century, decided he'd rather hunt for the 'missing link' between apes and humans than treat sore throats. Off he went to Indonesia (then the Dutch East Indies) and, lo and behold, found what he called *Pithecanthropus erectus* – later known as Java Man, or *Homo erectus*. It was a massive discovery, proof of early human ancestors walking upright outside of Africa, though his findings were quite controversial at the time. He literally put the 'paleo' in paleoanthropology with his tenacious quest!
