Before touchscreens and keyboards, there was the mighty pen, and George Safford Parker was its ultimate champion! This American inventor wasn't just selling pens; he was crafting precision instruments that changed how people wrote. Frustrated with leaky, unreliable pens as a telegraphy instructor, he decided to fix them himself, eventually inventing numerous improvements like the "Lucky Curve" feed system. He founded the iconic Parker Pen Company, turning elegant design and engineering prowess into a global brand. Parker proved that even in an everyday object, there's room for genius and innovation, making writing a smoother, cleaner, and much more enjoyable experience. He literally put the "flow" in fountain pens!
