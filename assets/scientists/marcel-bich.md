Marcel Bich, born in Turin but making his fortune in France, was the sharp mind behind the 'Bic' empire. In an era when fountain pens reigned supreme and were often leaky, temperamental affairs, Bich saw potential in the ballpoint. He didn't invent it, but he perfected the manufacturing process to make it cheap, reliable, and oh-so-disposable. He even famously shortened his surname to 'Bic' to avoid an unfortunate English pronunciation. From pens to lighters and razors, his philosophy was simple: mass-produce quality, affordable goods that everyone could use. He changed how we write, light up, and shave, all with a flick of his industrial wand.
