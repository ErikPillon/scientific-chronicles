George Boole was a self-taught mathematical genius who, despite humble beginnings, pretty much laid the groundwork for every digital device you've ever touched. His big idea? Boolean algebra. It's not just some dusty math concept; it's the elegant system of logic (true/false, 0/1) that makes computers tick. He showed how logical propositions could be expressed mathematically, a revolutionary concept that became the backbone of computer programming, circuit design, and information theory. So, next time your phone works, give a little nod to Mr. Boole – he's the silent hero behind the digital age.
