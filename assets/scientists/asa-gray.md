Meet Asa Gray, the undisputed 'Father of American Botany' and the guy who brought Darwin's revolutionary ideas to the New World. While Darwin was busy with finches, Gray was meticulously classifying North American flora, creating comprehensive works like *Manual of the Botany of the Northern United States*. He became a close friend and confidant of Darwin, defending and advocating for natural selection in America's scientific circles when it wasn't exactly popular. His legacy? A monumental understanding of plant life and a pivotal role in shaping evolutionary thought.
