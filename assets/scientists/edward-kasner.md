Edward Kasner was a Columbia University mathematician who didn't just understand big numbers, he literally invented names for them! In 1920, with the help of his nine-year-old nephew Milton Sirotta, he coined the term 'googol' for 1 followed by 100 zeros, and 'googolplex' for 1 followed by a googol of zeros. These playful terms made complex mathematical concepts accessible and, dare we say, cool. While his work in differential geometry was rigorous, Kasner's legacy is often remembered for making numbers so mind-bogglingly large they eventually inspired the name of a certain search engine. How's that for impact?
