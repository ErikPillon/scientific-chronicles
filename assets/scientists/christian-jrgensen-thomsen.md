This Danish antiquarian was the ultimate history detective, but without a time machine! Christian Jürgensen Thomsen didn't just collect old stuff; he created a system to understand it. In 1836, he formalized the 'Three-Age System' – Stone Age, Bronze Age, and Iron Age – based on the materials used for tools. Before Thomsen, archaeological collections were often a chaotic jumble. His brilliant idea to classify artifacts by material culture provided a clear, logical framework that revolutionized the study of prehistory and is still used by archaeologists today. Basically, he taught us how to tell how old something was, even when nobody left a date on it. A true pioneer in making sense of our distant past!
