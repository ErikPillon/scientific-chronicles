Before the Holland Tunnel was, well, *the Holland Tunnel*, it was a monumental challenge, and Clifford M. Holland was the man who bravely tackled it! This American civil engineer was a master of underground construction, supervising the planning and initial construction of several innovative tunnels. His most famous legacy is undoubtedly the vehicular tunnel under the Hudson River connecting New York and New Jersey, which tragically bears his name because he died before its completion. Holland pioneered innovative ventilation systems and construction techniques that were absolutely cutting-edge for his time. He literally moved mountains (or, more accurately, rivers!) to connect people and goods, leaving behind a crucial piece of modern infrastructure.
