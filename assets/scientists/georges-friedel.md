Meet Georges Friedel, the French brainiac who wasn't afraid to get down and dirty with rocks, but in the most sophisticated way possible. Born in 1865, Friedel inherited a passion for mineralogy from his dad (Auguste, also a famous mineralogist – talk about family tradition!). Georges carved out his own niche by becoming a leading light in crystallography. He meticulously studied crystal symmetries and forms, even delving into the enigmatic world of liquid crystals. Before Friedel, these 'liquids that act like solids' were a bit of a scientific head-scratcher, often dismissed as mere emulsions. But Georges, with his keen eye and even keener mind, proved they were true crystalline phases, ushering in a whole new field of study. So, the next time you marvel at an LCD screen (liquid crystal display, get it?), spare a thought for Georges. He laid some serious groundwork, making him a true rock star of crystallography!
