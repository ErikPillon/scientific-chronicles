Donald Wills Douglas wasn't content with just dreaming about flying; he wanted to make the world fly. Starting his own aircraft company in 1921, he went from building experimental torpedo planes to designing the iconic DC (Douglas Commercial) series. The DC-3, in particular, was a game-changer, reliable and affordable enough to make commercial air travel profitable and popular. It practically *invented* the airline industry as we know it! During WWII, his factories churned out planes at an astonishing rate, helping the Allies win the war. Douglas wasn't just an engineer; he was an industrial titan who shaped the skies, proving that big dreams, meticulous design, and a lot of metal can shrink the world and connect humanity in ways never before imagined.
