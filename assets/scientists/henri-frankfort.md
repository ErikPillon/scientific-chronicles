Henri Frankfort was a Dutch archaeologist who dug deep, not just into the earth, but into the minds of ancient civilizations. He wasn't just interested in pots and broken statues; he wanted to understand the worldview, the myths, and the 'whys' behind the art and architecture of ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, and the Near East. His influential works, like 'Kingship and the Gods' and 'The Birth of Civilization in the Near East,' were game-changers, offering profound insights into the symbolic language and religious frameworks of these early societies. He taught us that to truly grasp an ancient culture, you have to try and think like them, to understand their unique ways of making sense of the world. He basically gave a voice to civilizations that had been silent for millennia.
