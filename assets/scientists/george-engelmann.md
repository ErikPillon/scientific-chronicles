Meet George Engelmann, the German-American polymath who was a doctor by trade but a botanist by passion. After moving to the US, he became the go-to guy for all things plants, meticulously cataloging the flora of western North America. He was particularly keen on cacti, oaks, and conifers, describing countless new species and even getting a majestic tree, the Engelmann spruce, named after him. He also dipped his toes into meteorology and published a medical journal – because why stick to just one groundbreaking field? His systematic work was crucial for understanding the vast botanical diversity of the continent, proving that some doctors really do have green thumbs.
