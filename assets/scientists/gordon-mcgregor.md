Gordon McGregor was a true high-flyer in Canadian history, literally! Born in 1903, he didn't just love planes; he *defined* Canadian air travel. After a distinguished career as a fighter pilot and commanding officer in the RCAF during WWII, McGregor became the first president of Trans-Canada Airlines in 1947, which later transformed into Air Canada. He navigated the airline through its formative years, expanding its routes and modernizing its fleet, making air travel a reality for many Canadians. He was basically the architect of modern Canadian commercial aviation. He passed away in 1961, leaving behind a sky-high legacy.
