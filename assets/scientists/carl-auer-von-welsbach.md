If you've ever flicked a lighter or marveled at bright streetlights, you have Carl Auer von Welsbach to thank! Born in Austria in 1858, this inventive chemist was a master of rare earth elements, and he wasn't afraid to put them to practical use. He basically invented modern lighting! First, he gave us the gas mantle (the 'Welsbach mantle') in the 1880s, which made gas lamps far brighter and more efficient, turning dim streets into glowing avenues. Not content with just that, he then developed the metal-filament incandescent light bulb (using osmium, a super-tough metal), a key step towards the tungsten bulbs we know today. Oh, and he also invented the ferrocerium 'flint' used in virtually all lighters. From brightening your city to sparking your cigar, Auer von Welsbach was a true illumination pioneer, making daily life literally brighter for millions!
