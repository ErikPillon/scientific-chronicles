Before there were cars zipping around, there were wagons, and Clement Studebaker was at the forefront of getting America moving! As a co-founder of the legendary Studebaker Corporation, Clement, alongside his brothers, started with blacksmithing and quickly pivoted to building some of the most durable wagons and carriages in the country. From supplying Union armies during the Civil War to equipping pioneers heading West, Studebaker vehicles were synonymous with reliability. Clement’s engineering smarts and business acumen laid the groundwork for a company that would eventually transition to manufacturing automobiles, making him a pivotal figure in the evolution of American transportation. He literally helped pave the way for modern travel!
