Before jets zoomed across the sky, someone had to imagine them, and that someone was Ernst Heinrich Heinkel! This German aircraft designer, born in 1888, was a true aviation visionary. He founded Heinkel Flugzeugwerke, a company responsible for building some of the world's most innovative aircraft, including the He 178, the first aircraft to fly purely on jet power, and the He 176, the first rocket-powered aircraft. Talk about pushing boundaries! While his career was entangled with a difficult historical period, his contributions to aeronautical engineering were undeniable, fundamentally shaping the future of flight and making the skies a much faster place.
