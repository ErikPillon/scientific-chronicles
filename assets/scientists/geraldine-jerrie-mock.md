In 1964, while some were still wondering if women belonged in the cockpit, Jerrie Mock quietly packed her bags, fueled her Cessna 180 "Spirit of Columbus," and took off to circumnavigate the globe. Born in 1925, this mother of three became the first woman to complete a solo flight around the world, taking 29 days, 21 stops, and a whole lot of pluck. She battled mechanical issues, sketchy runways, and unforeseen detours with grace and determination. Her achievement wasn't just a record-breaker; it was a powerful testament to personal ambition and skill, proving that a woman, a "housewife who loved to fly," could conquer the world, one challenging airstrip at a time. Forget the kitchen, Jerrie was in the clouds!
