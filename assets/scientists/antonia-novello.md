Antonia Novello isn't just a doctor; she's a trailblazer with a capital T! Born in Puerto Rico, she shattered glass ceilings to become the first woman and first Hispanic to serve as Surgeon General of the United States, appointed by President George H.W. Bush in 1990. During her tenure, she championed children's health, fought against underage drinking and smoking, and tirelessly advocated for improved healthcare for minorities and women. Her career is a masterclass in public health leadership, proving that one determined individual can truly make a difference in the health of a nation. Dr. Novello didn't just prescribe medicine; she prescribed progress!
