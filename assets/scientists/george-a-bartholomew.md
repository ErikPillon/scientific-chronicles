George A. Bartholomew was the ultimate wildlife detective, always asking "how do animals *actually* do that?" This American zoologist practically invented the field of physiological ecology, which explores how animals cope with their environment using their bodies' amazing adaptations. He studied everything from desert birds to marine mammals, revealing the clever tricks nature uses for survival. His work at UCLA shaped generations of ecologists, proving that understanding the 'how' is just as important as the 'what' in the animal kingdom.
