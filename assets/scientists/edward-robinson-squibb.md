Edward Robinson Squibb was a physician who got seriously fed up with the shoddy quality of medicines in the mid-19th century. So, what did he do? He didn't just complain; he started his own pharmaceutical company, E. R. Squibb and Sons, in 1858, with a radical idea: make drugs pure and consistent! A true champion of patient safety, he developed improved methods for manufacturing everything from ether (crucial for surgery) to vaccines. His relentless pursuit of quality set a new standard in the industry, proving that ethics and science could—and should—go hand-in-hand in the world of medicine. No wonder his legacy is still curing headaches today!
