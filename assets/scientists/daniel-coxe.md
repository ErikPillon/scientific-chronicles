Daniel Coxe was a bit of a polymath – a physician to British royalty, a shrewd businessman, and a colonial enthusiast with a taste for scientific exploration. While he's often remembered for his vast land claims in North America and his early ideas for unifying the colonies (way before it was cool!), he was also genuinely interested in mapping and cataloging the natural resources and geography of the New World. He saw the scientific potential in the wilderness, alongside the economic. Coxe was blending medicine, politics, and a touch of early natural history, proving that some folks just can't stick to one lane and are keen to understand the world around them!
