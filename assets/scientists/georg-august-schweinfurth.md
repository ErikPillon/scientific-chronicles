Georg August Schweinfurth wasn't content with just watering his houseplants; he wanted to find all the world's plants! This intrepid German botanist and explorer embarked on epic journeys through East and Central Africa in the mid-19th century. Imagine him, trekking through unknown territories, meticulously cataloging every leaf, every flower, and every cultural detail he encountered. He wasn't just a plant guy; he was an ethnographer too, making significant observations about the local peoples, including the Azande and their rumored cannibalistic practices (spoiler: he dispelled some myths). Schweinfurth's maps and collections were game-changers, opening up a whole new botanical world to European science and proving that sometimes, you just gotta go find the plants yourself.
