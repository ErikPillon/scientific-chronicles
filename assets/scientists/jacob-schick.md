Jacob Schick was a true innovator in personal grooming, forever changing morning routines with his brilliant invention: the electric razor. A former U.S. Army lieutenant colonel, Schick started tinkering with the idea of a dry shaver in the 1920s, after experiencing the challenges of shaving in rough conditions. He patented his first electric razor in 1928, and by 1931, the Schick Dry Shaver was on the market, promising a quick, clean, and lather-free shave. He basically freed millions from the tyranny of blades and shaving cream, becoming a household name in the process and kickstarting an entire industry.
