Florence Bascom wasn't just a woman in science; she was a force of nature in geology! Born in 1862, she became the first woman to earn a Ph.D. in geology from Johns Hopkins University and the first woman hired by the U.S. Geological Survey. Talk about breaking new ground! Bascom specialized in petrology, the study of rocks, meticulously mapping and analyzing the ancient crystalline rocks of the Appalachian Mountains. She taught at Bryn Mawr College, inspiring generations of female geologists who went on to make their own marks. Her precise, rigorous scientific approach set new standards, proving that intellect and hard work recognized no gender boundaries in the rough-and-tumble world of geology. She truly carved her own path, one rock sample at a time.
