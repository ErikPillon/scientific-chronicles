Forget what you think you know about Freud – Anna, Sigmund’s youngest daughter, was the one who really got into the minds of children! An influential Austrian-British psychoanalyst, Anna took her famous father's groundbreaking theories and applied them to the developing world of kids. She championed child psychoanalysis, realizing that little humans weren't just miniature adults; they needed their own methods of understanding and support. During World War II, she even ran nurseries in London, observing how trauma affected children. Her work on ego defense mechanisms is legendary, showing how our minds cope with stress. Anna Freud wasn't just living in her father's shadow; she carved out her own monumental space, becoming a foundational figure in child psychology.
