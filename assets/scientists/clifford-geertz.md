Clifford Geertz wasn't your average "Indiana Jones" anthropologist; he was more of a philosophical detective, digging into the *meanings* of culture rather than just its artifacts. This American intellectual powerhouse championed "interpretive anthropology," arguing that cultures are like texts to be read and understood, not just observed. His concept of "thick description" – diving deep into the context and layers of meaning behind human actions – revolutionized the field. From Balinese cockfights to Moroccan bazaars, Geertz meticulously unwound cultural symbols to reveal the intricate "webs of significance" people weave. He literally taught us to look beyond the surface and truly grasp what makes different societies tick.
