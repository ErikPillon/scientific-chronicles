Georg von Reichenbach wasn't just an engineer; he was the German maestro of precision instruments during the early 19th century. He took the art of telescope and survey instrument making from good to *great*, introducing innovations like precision dividing engines that allowed for incredibly accurate scales. His instruments were so good, they were practically the iPhones of their day for astronomers and surveyors, pushing the boundaries of observation and measurement. A true visionary who brought clarity to the scientific gaze!
