Meet Yang Liwei, the man who strapped into the Shenzhou 5 spacecraft on October 15, 2003, and became China's first-ever 'taikonaut' to orbit Earth! This former fighter pilot from the People's Liberation Army Air Force soared into history, circling our planet 14 times in just under 22 hours. His successful mission didn't just put China in an exclusive club of spacefaring nations; it ignited the imaginations of millions and cemented China's ambition to become a major player in space exploration.
