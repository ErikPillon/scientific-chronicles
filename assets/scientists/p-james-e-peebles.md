If the universe had a grand architect, James Peebles would be its most brilliant historian. This Canadian-American theoretical cosmologist, a Nobel Prize winner, basically wrote the textbook on how the cosmos got its groove. From dark matter to dark energy, and the Big Bang's afterglow (the cosmic microwave background), Peebles provided the theoretical scaffolding that built our modern cosmological model. He's been quietly unravelling the universe's biggest mysteries for decades, showing us how everything from the tiniest particles to the largest galaxies came to be. Not bad for a day's work, eh?
