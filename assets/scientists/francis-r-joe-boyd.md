When it came to the Earth's fiery insides, Francis R. 'Joe' Boyd was the guy who literally put the pressure on! Born in 1928, this American geochemist spent his career creating laboratory conditions that mimicked the intense heat and immense pressure found deep within our planet. Why? To figure out how rocks and minerals behave down there. His pioneering work in high-pressure experimental petrology reshaped our understanding of the Earth's mantle and the processes that drive volcanism and plate tectonics. Joe didn't just speculate about what was beneath our feet; he brought a piece of it into the lab, under incredible stress, to reveal its secrets. Talk about a heavyweight in geology, whose work provided critical data for models of planetary evolution!
