Ever wonder what's really going on beneath the waves? Georg Wüst did! This German oceanography legend spent his career peering into the mysteries of the Atlantic, particularly its deep-sea currents and circulation patterns. He was a key figure on the "Meteor" expedition, mapping out the ocean's hidden highways with unprecedented detail. Wüst's meticulous work laid much of the foundation for modern physical oceanography, showing us that the ocean's depths are anything but still.
