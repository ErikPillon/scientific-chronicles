Ever taken an IQ test that didn't feel like a dusty old parlor game? You probably have David Wechsler to thank! This Romanian-born American psychologist developed the widely used Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale (WAIS) and Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children (WISC). He didn't just create tests; he redefined how we think about intelligence, arguing it wasn't just about book smarts but a mix of verbal, performance, and practical abilities. He was all about understanding the whole person, not just a single score. His work changed clinical psychology, helping identify learning difficulties and psychological conditions, and proving that being smart isn't a one-size-fits-all deal. A true mind reader, without the crystal ball!
