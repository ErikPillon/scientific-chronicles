When some Bedouin shepherds stumbled upon ancient scrolls in a cave near the Dead Sea in 1947, it was Eliezer Sukenik, a professor of archaeology at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, who quickly realized their immense importance. While initial reactions varied, Sukenik acted decisively, acquiring some of the scrolls and immediately understanding their connection to the ancient Essenes. His swift identification helped secure these invaluable texts for study, ushering in a new era of biblical and historical scholarship. Without his early insight, who knows how long the Dead Sea Scrolls might have remained a perplexing mystery!
