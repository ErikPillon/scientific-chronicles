Step aside, modern smartwatches! Back in the late 17th and early 18th centuries, Daniel Quare was the rockstar of timekeeping. This brilliant English horologer was not just making pretty clocks; he was innovating like crazy! He's famously credited with inventing the repeating watch, which could chime the time on demand – super handy before glow-in-the-dark dials. Quare also improved pendulum clocks, making them more accurate and reliable. His craftsmanship and mechanical genius caught the eye of royalty and discerning collectors, cementing his place as a true pioneer in precision engineering. If you owned a Quare, you knew you had a piece of cutting-edge tech (and art!).
