Born into slavery, George Washington Carver blossomed into an agricultural superstar who transformed farming in the American South. This brilliant botanist and inventor taught farmers how to rotate crops, specifically advocating for peanuts, sweet potatoes, and soybeans, to enrich soil depleted by continuous cotton cultivation. But he didn't stop there! He then developed hundreds of innovative uses for these crops, from peanut butter (yes, partially him!) and plastics to dyes and cosmetics. He didn't just tell farmers what to grow; he showed them how to create economic opportunity and self-sufficiency. Carver truly planted the seeds for a more sustainable and prosperous future, proving that genius knows no bounds.
