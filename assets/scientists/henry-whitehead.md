Forget Sherlock Holmes, meet Henry Whitehead, the real-life detective who helped crack a deadly medical mystery! This unassuming London curate played a pivotal role in the 1854 Broad Street cholera outbreak. While Dr. John Snow famously suspected the contaminated water pump, it was Whitehead's intimate knowledge of the local community, his dogged interviews, and his detailed mapping of cases that provided the crucial evidence. He tracked down unique circumstances, like a baby's infected nappies being washed into a cesspit near the pump, convincing even Snow's skeptics. A true unsung hero of public health!
