Earl S. Tupper was a real character who turned plastic scraps into a kitchen empire. Originally an arborist and then a DuPont employee, he started his own plastics company after WWII. His stroke of genius? Those now-iconic, airtight, burp-sealed containers. He wasn't just about the product; he pioneered the 'Tupperware Party,' a direct-selling marvel that empowered women to become entrepreneurs right from their living rooms. So, next time you're sealing leftovers, give a silent nod to Earl, who made sure your food stayed fresh and your grandmother could host a killer party.
