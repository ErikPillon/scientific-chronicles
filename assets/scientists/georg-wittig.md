A German organic chemistry wizard who basically gave chemists a superpower: the Wittig reaction. This ingenious method turns aldehydes and ketones into alkenes, making complex molecules achievable with surprising elegance. He nabbed a Nobel Prize in 1979 for his genius, sharing it with Herbert C. Brown. Wittig wasn't just a reaction-maker; he was a reaction-shaper, pushing the boundaries of what was possible in the lab and leaving an indelible mark on synthetic chemistry.
