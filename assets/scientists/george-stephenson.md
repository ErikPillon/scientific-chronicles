If you've ever taken a train, you owe a nod to George Stephenson, the undisputed 'Father of Railways.' This British mechanical engineer was a self-made man who started out overseeing coal mine machinery and ended up kickstarting a global transport revolution. His most famous creation, the 'Rocket,' won the Rainhill Trials in 1829, proving that steam locomotives could be both fast and reliable. He didn't just build engines; he designed entire railway systems, including the groundbreaking Stockton and Darlington Railway and the Liverpool and Manchester Railway. Stephenson's ingenuity didn't just move coal; it moved people, goods, and ideas, shrinking the world and shaping the industrial age. All aboard for a legend!
