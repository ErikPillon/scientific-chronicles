Godefroy Wendelin, often known by his Latinized name Godefridus Wendelinus, was a 17th-century Flemish polymath who wasn't afraid to look up and challenge the cosmos. This astronomer and mathematician was an early and ardent supporter of Copernicus's heliocentric model, a brave stance for his time! He made remarkably precise observations of astronomical phenomena, including the transit of Mercury and the occultation of Venus. Essentially, he was one of those meticulous early modern scientists who meticulously charted the heavens, helping to nudge humanity's understanding of the universe into a new, more accurate orbit.
