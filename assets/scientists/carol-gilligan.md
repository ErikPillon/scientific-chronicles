Carol Gilligan basically told the male-dominated field of psychology to take a seat and listen up! Her seminal work, *In a Different Voice*, published in 1982, dared to question long-held theories of moral development (like Kohlberg's), pointing out that they were largely based on studies of boys and men. She argued that women often approach ethical dilemmas with an 'ethic of care,' prioritizing relationships and responsibilities, rather than solely a 'justice' orientation. Gilligan's work wasn't just a critique; it was a powerful reframe, opening up critical conversations about gender, morality, and how we understand human development. She's a true legend for expanding our understanding of the human psyche beyond a single, narrow lens.
