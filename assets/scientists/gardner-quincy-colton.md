Gardner Quincy Colton was a showman, quite literally! He toured the country, giving public lectures and demonstrating the exhilarating (and sometimes silly) effects of nitrous oxide, or "laughing gas." In 1844, during one of his public demonstrations, a man injured himself but felt no pain due to the gas. A young dentist named Horace Wells, who was in the audience, immediately saw the medical potential. Wells then successfully used Colton's gas for a tooth extraction. Colton later established the Colton Dental Association, pioneering the widespread use of nitrous oxide in dentistry and making painful extractions a thing of the past for countless patients. He essentially brought "painless dentistry" to the masses, turning a party trick into a genuine medical marvel. Talk about a brilliant career pivot!
