Charles Wilkes was an American naval officer and explorer who basically put America on the map of global scientific exploration. He notoriously led the U.S. Exploring Expedition (1838-1842), a colossal voyage that circumnavigated the globe, surveyed countless islands, and collected a treasure trove of scientific specimens. The expedition charted vast stretches of the Pacific, but its most iconic achievement was confirming that Antarctica was indeed a continent, not just an ice floe. Wilkes was a tough, often abrasive leader, which led to numerous courts-martial, but his scientific contributions were undeniable, laying the groundwork for American oceanography and geology. He might have been a bit of a curmudgeon, but he certainly delivered the goods for science!
