Meet Allen K. Breed, the unsung hero who helped make your car a whole lot safer. Before airbags were standard, car crashes were even more brutal. Breed, an American mechanical engineer, wasn't just interested in inventing things; he wanted to solve real-world problems. In 1968, he created the first *electronic* sensor and safety system for airbags, a crucial step that made them practical and reliable for widespread use. His work transformed automotive safety, pushing airbags from a futuristic concept to a life-saving reality found in almost every car today. So, next time you buckle up, remember Breed – the guy who gave your car an extra layer of soft, inflatable protection.
