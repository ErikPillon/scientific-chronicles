Edward Sapir was a linguistic rockstar who believed language wasn't just a tool for communication, but a lens through which we perceive reality itself. Born in Germany and raised in the US, he became a towering figure in American anthropology and linguistics in the early 20th century. He meticulously documented and classified numerous Indigenous North American languages, many of which were on the brink of extinction. His work, particularly his collaboration with Benjamin Whorf (leading to the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis), argued that the language we speak influences our thought patterns. So, next time you think in a certain way, blame (or thank!) your mother tongue, thanks to Sapir!
