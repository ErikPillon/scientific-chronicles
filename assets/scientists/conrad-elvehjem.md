Meet Conrad Elvehjem, the biochemical detective who solved a medical mystery that had plagued millions! Born in Wisconsin in 1901, Elvehjem spent his career at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where his relentless research in nutrition led to groundbreaking discoveries. His most famous feat? In 1937, he definitively identified niacin (vitamin B3) as the key factor in preventing pellagra, a devastating disease that caused severe skin, digestive, and neurological problems. His work directly led to the enrichment of flour with niacin, effectively eradicating pellagra in the developed world. A true hero who used science to make lives healthier and happier!
