Jean Louis Pons started his career as a lowly caretaker and doorman at the Marseille Observatory, a job with essentially zero scientific standing. But he had sharp eyes, an uncanny memory for the night sky, and endless patience, and he taught himself to spot the faint smudges of comets against the star field better than almost anyone alive. Over his career he discovered or co-discovered 37 comets, a record that stood for well over a century, including comets now bearing his name and the short-period comet later identified with Johann Encke's calculations. He eventually earned real observatory directorships in Marseille, Lucca, and Florence, an extraordinary rise for someone who started out just sweeping the floors. Never underestimate a guy who really likes looking up. ☄️🔭
