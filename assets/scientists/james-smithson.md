Ever wonder why there's a Smithsonian Institution in Washington D.C., founded by a British guy? Meet James Smithson, a brilliant mineralogist and chemist with a fascinating backstory (he was the illegitimate son of the Duke of Northumberland). Though he never even visited the U.S., his will, made in a fit of pique or ambition, decreed his fortune be used to establish an institution 'for the increase and diffusion of knowledge among men' in Washington. It was an astonishing, visionary gift that created one of the world's greatest museum and research complexes, ensuring his name would indeed live on, far beyond any aristocratic title. Talk about a posthumous mic drop!
