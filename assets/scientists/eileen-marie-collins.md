Eileen Collins isn't just an astronaut; she's a trailblazer who soared past the glass ceiling straight into orbit! This American aeronautical engineer and former Air Force pilot made history not once, but twice. In 1995, she became the first woman to pilot a Space Shuttle, and then, in 1999, she commanded a Space Shuttle mission, becoming the first female commander. Talk about breaking barriers! Her career, spanning four spaceflights, inspired countless aspiring astronauts and proved that with skill, determination, and a whole lot of courage, the sky's not the limit – it's just the beginning. She truly embodied "girl power" at stratospheric altitudes!
