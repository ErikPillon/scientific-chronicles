Henry Gellibrand (1597-1636) might not be a household name, but this English mathematician and astronomer dropped a scientific bombshell way back in the 17th century. While most folks thought the Earth's magnetic field was pretty stable, Gellibrand, through meticulous observation and comparison of historical records, definitively proved that magnetic declination (the difference between true north and magnetic north) actually changes over time. This wasn't just a quirky factoid; it was crucial for navigation, helping sailors keep their bearings more accurately. His work was a quiet revolution, showing that even Earth's fundamental forces aren't as constant as we might assume. A true magnetic personality!
