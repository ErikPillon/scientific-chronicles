Before selfies were a thing, there was Hércules Florence, a French artist and inventor who, while living in Brazil, figured out how to capture images on paper using chemicals. Yep, he essentially invented photography in 1833, a few years *before* Daguerre and Fox Talbot got their much-hyped recognition. His 'photographie' (a term he coined!) system involved silver nitrate on paper, a true pioneer in a tropical setting. Talk about being ahead of the curve! His work remained largely unknown outside Brazil for decades, proving that sometimes genius just needs a better PR agent.
