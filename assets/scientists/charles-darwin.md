Before he became 'THE Darwin' who ruffled feathers with evolution, Charles Darwin was a keen but somewhat unenthusiastic student of medicine and theology. But his true calling lay outdoors, poking around in nature. His real adventure began in 1831, when a timely invitation to join HMS Beagle as a naturalist changed his life (and ours). This five-year voyage, especially his observations in the Galápagos, transformed a budding collector into a scientific giant, providing the raw material for his revolutionary ideas on natural selection. So, 'Early Darwin' was less about sermons and more about seashells, setting the stage for one of history's biggest scientific shake-ups!
