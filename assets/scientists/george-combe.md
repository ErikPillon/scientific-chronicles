George Combe was a Scottish thinker who, in the 19th century, made a big splash as a proponent of phrenology. Yep, that's the now-debunked idea that your skull bumps tell all about your personality and intellect. But don't write him off just yet! Beyond the bumps, Combe was a significant figure in social reform and education, arguing for better public health, progressive education, and a more rational approach to life, heavily influenced by scientific principles of his era. His book 'The Constitution of Man' was a bestseller, showing how impactful his ideas were, even if one of his core theories eventually hit a bumpy road.
