Meet Henry Palmer, a 19th-century engineering dynamo who left his mark on both tracks and roofs! This British civil engineer isn't just a footnote; he's the guy who developed the first practical monorail system in 1821 – way before it became a sci-fi staple. And if that wasn't cool enough, he also patented corrugated iron in 1829, revolutionizing construction with its strength and lightness. So next time you see a sturdy shed or a futuristic train, give a nod to Henry. He was quietly building the future, one corrugated sheet and one rail at a time.
