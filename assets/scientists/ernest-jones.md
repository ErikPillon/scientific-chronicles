Ernest Jones was basically Freud's right-hand man and ultimate hype-man for psychoanalysis in the English-speaking world. This Welsh physician-turned-psychoanalyst didn't just practice; he preached, organized, and institutionalized the Freudian revolution. He was instrumental in founding the British Psycho-Analytical Society and the International Psychoanalytical Association, making sure Freud's ideas weren't just a Viennese curiosity. But his magnum opus was the three-volume, definitive biography of Sigmund Freud, 'The Life and Work of Sigmund Freud.' It's like writing the official history of a legendary rock band, complete with all the behind-the-scenes drama and intellectual genius. Without Jones, the couch might have taken a lot longer to make its way into popular culture. He made sure Freud's legacy was meticulously documented and widely disseminated.
