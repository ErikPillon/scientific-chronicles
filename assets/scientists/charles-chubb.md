In the rough-and-tumble world of 19th-century Britain, security was a big deal, and Charles Chubb was the guy who literally locked it down. This English inventor wasn't just any locksmith; in 1818, he patented his 'detector lock,' a marvel of mechanical engineering that revolutionized home and business security. What made it special? If someone tried to pick it, the lock would 'detect' the attempt and effectively jam, requiring a special key to reset it. This brilliant anti-tampering feature made Chubb locks almost unpickable for its time, earning him royal patronage and making 'Chubb' synonymous with impenetrable safety. He built an empire out of ingenuity and a very clever spring!
