Emil Rathenau was the kind of guy who saw an electric light bulb and didn't just marvel at it; he saw an entire industry. This shrewd German engineer and entrepreneur famously acquired the German patent rights to Thomas Edison's electric lamp in 1883 and promptly went on to found Allgemeine Elektricitäts-Gesellschaft (AEG) in 1887. Under his leadership, AEG didn't just make light bulbs; it built power plants, streetcars, and pretty much anything else that hummed with electricity, rapidly transforming Germany's industrial landscape. He was a master of vertical integration, controlling everything from raw materials to final products. Think of him as Germany's answer to Edison meets Henry Ford, but with more emphasis on keeping the lights on. A true titan of early electrical engineering and industry!
