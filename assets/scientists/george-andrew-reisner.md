George Andrew Reisner was the American archaeological superstar who basically owned Egyptian and Nubian history for half a century. Leading Harvard University-Boston Museum of Fine Arts expeditions, he dug up pyramids, tombs, and settlements, uncovering treasures and deciphering the lives of pharaohs and ancient Nubian kings. His meticulous methods and exhaustive publications set new standards for archaeological fieldwork. Reisner wasn't just unearthing artifacts; he was resurrecting entire civilizations, making ancient history spring back to life, one meticulous dig at a time.
