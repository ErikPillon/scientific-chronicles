Don't get him mixed up with the telescope guy! Galileo Ferraris was an Italian electrical genius who, in 1888, independently invented the polyphase alternating current (AC) induction motor. While Nikola Tesla often gets all the glory for AC power, Ferraris was right there, demonstrating his rotating magnetic field principles a year earlier in 1885. His work was fundamental to developing modern power systems, literally setting the world in motion with electricity. He was also a passionate educator, shaping a generation of engineers. So next time you flip a switch, remember Galileo Ferraris – the lesser-known but equally brilliant AC pioneer! Without his insights, our electrified world might look a whole lot different.
