David Sarnoff was less a pure scientist and more a visionary general who marshaled technology into a cultural revolution. A Russian immigrant who rose from humble beginnings, Sarnoff became the long-time head of RCA (Radio Corporation of America) and was instrumental in shaping the early landscapes of radio and television. Legend has it he was the guy who picked up the distress calls from the Titanic, catapulting him into the spotlight. He pushed tirelessly for the commercialization of radio and later, TV, seeing their potential not just as gadgets but as powerful forces for information and entertainment. Sarnoff didn't invent the technologies, but he certainly put them in everyone's living room, earning him the moniker "The General." He was a titan of industry who truly believed in the power of the airwaves to change the world.
