Ivan Matveyevich Vinogradov was a mathematical titan from Russia, born in 1891. He was the undisputed master of *analytic number theory*, a fancy way of saying he used tools from calculus to solve sticky problems about integers. His most famous achievement, the Vinogradov theorem, proved that every sufficiently large odd integer can be expressed as the sum of three prime numbers – a massive step towards Goldbach's conjecture! Known for his no-nonsense approach and incredible focus, Vinogradov was a powerhouse who led the Steklov Institute of Mathematics for decades. He passed away in 1983, leaving behind a legacy of deep insights that continue to influence number theorists today.
