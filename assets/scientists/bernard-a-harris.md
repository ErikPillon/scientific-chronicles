Dr. Bernard A. Harris Jr., born in 1956, isn't just a physician; he's an American hero who dared to reach for the stars – and touched them! After earning his medical degree, Harris traded his stethoscope for a spacesuit, joining NASA's astronaut corps. On February 9, 1995, during the STS-63 mission aboard the Space Shuttle Discovery, he etched his name into history by becoming the first African American to perform a spacewalk. Imagine floating freely in the vastness of space, a tiny human orbiting Earth! His achievement wasn't just a personal triumph; it was a beacon of inspiration, breaking barriers and showing countless others that the sky is not the limit when it comes to dreams. Today, Dr. Harris continues to inspire as a proponent of STEM education, ensuring the next generation can also reach for extraordinary heights. Talk about a career trajectory!
