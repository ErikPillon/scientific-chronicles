George Sarton wasn't just a historian; he was *the* historian of science, effectively creating the field as we know it! Born in Belgium and later making his home in America, Sarton had a grand vision: to systematically document and understand the evolution of scientific thought throughout history. He founded the iconic journal 'Isis' in 1913, giving a voice to this nascent discipline, and later launched 'Osiris.' His monumental 'Introduction to the History of Science' was an unparalleled effort to chart scientific progress from antiquity to the 14th century. He basically laid the intellectual groundwork, ensuring that science's journey, not just its destinations, would be rigorously studied. What a legacy!
