Henry Bell was a Scottish engineer who truly made waves, literally! He's famous for designing and building the Comet, which, in 1812, became the first commercially successful steamboat in Europe. Before the Comet, river travel was often slow and reliant on sails or muscle. Bell's innovative vessel, chugging along the River Clyde, proved that steam power was the future of water transport, kickstarting an era of industrial and passenger shipping. He basically launched the steamboat age in Europe, proving that a little bit of steam could change everything.
