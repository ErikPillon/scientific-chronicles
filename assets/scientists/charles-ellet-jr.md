Charles Ellet Jr. was an American civil engineer who literally built bridges—and then, when duty called, designed ram ships to smash through others! A true Renaissance man of engineering, he designed some of the earliest wire-cable suspension bridges in the U.S., including early plans for the Niagara Falls Suspension Bridge. When the Civil War broke out, he turned his brilliant engineering brain to naval warfare, leading a fleet of converted steamships as rams for the Union. He famously defeated a Confederate fleet at Memphis, but tragically succumbed to a wound received during the battle. From elegant bridges to battle-ready rams, Ellet proved engineers can be pretty handy in a fight.
