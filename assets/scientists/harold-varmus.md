Harold Varmus is basically a cancer detective, and a Nobel Prize-winning one at that! This American molecular biologist, along with J. Michael Bishop, cracked open a huge mystery in 1976: they discovered oncogenes. These aren't just random bits of DNA; they're normal genes that, when messed up, can turn healthy cells into cancer cells. Their discovery totally reshaped our understanding of cancer, shifting it from being purely an environmental disease to one with a strong genetic component. Varmus didn't just win a Nobel for this in 1989; he's also led major scientific institutions like the NIH and NCI, pushing the boundaries of medical research to keep us healthier.
