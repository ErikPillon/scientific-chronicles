Ever heard of 'shrapnel'? You can thank (or blame!) British artillery officer Henry Shrapnel for that! Back in the late 18th century, cannonballs were... well, just cannonballs. But Henry, a man clearly thinking outside the ordnance box, developed a new type of projectile that exploded mid-air, scattering lethal balls and turning battlefields into far more perilous places. First tested in 1784, his 'spherical case shot' was a game-changer for the British Army during the Napoleonic Wars. It was so effective, his name became synonymous with the fragments it unleashed. A rather explosive legacy, wouldn't you say?
