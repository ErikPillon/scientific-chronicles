Edward Herbert Thompson wasn't your average desk-bound academic. This American archaeologist and diplomat became obsessed with Mayan civilization, particularly the ancient city of Chichen Itza, where he served as U.S. consul. His most famous (and controversial) feat? Dredging the Sacred Cenote between 1904 and 1910, pulling out gold, jade, pottery, and even human remains offered as sacrifices. He literally bought the hacienda surrounding the ruins to conduct his excavations, sparking debates that still echo today about cultural heritage and who 'owns' history's treasures. Talk about a hands-on approach!
