George Davis Snell, an American geneticist, was the unsung hero behind understanding why your body accepts or rejects a new organ. He shared a Nobel Prize for his groundbreaking work on the major histocompatibility complex (MHC), those crucial genes that dictate how your immune system recognizes 'self' from 'non-self.' Using mice as his tiny test subjects, he uncovered the genetic basis for tissue compatibility and rejection. His discoveries were absolutely fundamental, not just for successful organ transplants but also for our broader understanding of immunology and the fight against autoimmune diseases and cancer. Talk about saving lives with science!
