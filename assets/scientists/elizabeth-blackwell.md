Elizabeth Blackwell wasn't one to take 'no' for an answer, especially when it came to becoming a doctor. Born in England but making her groundbreaking mark in the U.S., she endured ridicule and rejection before finally being accepted into medical school – a move that shocked pretty much everyone. Not only did she snag her M.D. in 1849, becoming the first woman in America to do so, but she didn't stop there. She went on to establish the New York Infirmary for Indigent Women and Children, run entirely by women, proving that ladies weren't just good for nursing boo-boos but could run the whole darn show. Blackwell was a true pioneer, paving the way for countless women in medicine, all while probably thinking, 'Told you so!'
