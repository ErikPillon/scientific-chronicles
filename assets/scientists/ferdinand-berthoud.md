Ferdinand Berthoud was the 18th-century equivalent of a Swiss watchmaking rock star! Born in Switzerland but making his mark in France, Berthoud dedicated his life to creating incredibly accurate timepieces, especially marine chronometers. In an era when reliable timekeeping at sea was the holy grail for navigation, his precision instruments helped sailors pinpoint their longitude with unprecedented accuracy. He wasn't just making pretty watches; he was literally changing how the world was explored. His work was so significant that he was appointed 'Clockmaker of the Navy and Arts' by King Louis XV. Without innovators like Berthoud, global travel would have been a lot more, well, lost!
