Ever bitten into a perfectly fluffy potato? Thank Antoine-Augustin Parmentier! This French pharmacist, nutritionist, and agronomist wasn't just dabbling in medicine; he took on a monumental challenge: convincing 18th-century France that the lowly potato wasn't just animal fodder or a cause of leprosy. Nope, it was a nutritious, famine-fighting powerhouse! Parmentier used clever PR tactics, like having King Louis XVI wear potato flowers and staging guarded potato fields (which, of course, encouraged "theft" of the supposedly valuable spuds). He even hosted dinners featuring only potato dishes. His relentless campaigning turned a shunned tuber into a national treasure, preventing widespread starvation and forever changing European diets. A true veggie evangelist before his time!
