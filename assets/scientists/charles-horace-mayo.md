Charles Horace Mayo, born in 1865, wasn't just a doctor; he was half of the dynamic duo behind one of the most famous medical institutions on Earth: the Mayo Clinic. Along with his brother William, Charles transformed rural Minnesota into a beacon of medical innovation and patient care. Charles was a gifted surgeon, often specializing in operations on the thyroid, stomach, and nervous system, and always advocating for sterile practices and organized hospital management. His practical skills and forward-thinking approach to medicine helped elevate surgery into a sophisticated science. He wasn't just cutting people open; he was building a system that would revolutionize healthcare for generations.
