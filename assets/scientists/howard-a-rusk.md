Meet Howard A. Rusk, the ultimate medical trailblazer who basically invented modern physical medicine and rehabilitation. During WWII, he saw firsthand how soldiers with severe injuries needed more than just surgical fixes; they needed a pathway back to independent living. Rusk wasn't content with just patching people up; he wanted them to thrive. He founded rehabilitation centers, like the one at Bellevue Hospital, and fought tirelessly for the rights and care of people with disabilities, transforming what was once seen as a lost cause into a field focused on restoration and hope. Thanks to his vision, 'rehab' went from an afterthought to an essential part of healthcare, giving millions a shot at a fuller, more active life.
