J. Craig Venter, born in 1946, is the ultimate biological disruptor. This American biologist and entrepreneur didn't just play in the sandbox of genomics; he built the whole playground! He famously led the private effort at Celera Genomics to sequence the human genome, racing against the public Human Genome Project – talk about scientific drama! Not content with just reading life's code, Venter's team later made headlines by creating the first self-replicating synthetic bacterial cell, essentially synthesizing life from chemical components. His work constantly pushes the boundaries of what's possible in biology, sparking both awe and ethical debates. He's still kicking, probably pondering what biological marvel to create next.
