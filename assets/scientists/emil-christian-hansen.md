Emil Christian Hansen was the unsung hero of happy hour, a Danish mycologist who quite literally purified beer for the masses! Before Hansen, brewers battled "beer diseases" caused by wild, unpredictable yeasts and bacteria, often resulting in sour or spoiled batches. Working at the Carlsberg Laboratory, Hansen became a scientific detective, meticulously studying different yeast strains. In 1883, he made a breakthrough: he isolated a single, pure strain of *Saccharomyces carlsbergensis* (now *Saccharomyces pastorianus*) and developed methods to cultivate it on an industrial scale. This meant consistent, high-quality beer every time, transforming brewing from an art reliant on luck into a precise science. His work laid the foundation for industrial microbiology, proving that understanding microscopic organisms could have massive economic and social impacts. Thanks to Hansen, we can all enjoy a consistently good pint, without worrying if our brew is going to be a biological experiment gone wrong. Cheers to that!
