Ever heard of Gregor Mendel? Well, Carl Correns was one of the scientific heroes who, completely independently, dusted off Mendel's genius work on heredity in 1900. While everyone else was busy doing… other things, Correns, a brilliant German botanist, was meticulously studying heredity in plants like maize, proving that those "factors" Mendel talked about really were passed down in predictable patterns. Imagine the "aha!" moment when he realized his findings echoed those of an obscure Augustinian friar from decades earlier! Correns's meticulous experiments and rediscovery didn't just confirm Mendel's legacy; they ignited the field of genetics, pushing biology into a whole new era of understanding how traits are inherited. He played a massive role in kickstarting modern genetics, helping us unravel the very blueprints of life.
