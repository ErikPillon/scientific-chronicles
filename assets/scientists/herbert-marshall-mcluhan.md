Herbert Marshall McLuhan was the kind of thinker who made you rethink everything you thought you knew about, well, thinking! This Canadian philosopher and communication theorist dropped bombshells like "The medium is the message" and predicted the "global village" long before the internet made it a reality. His ideas on how technology shapes human consciousness and society were mind-blowing in the 1960s and remain incredibly relevant today. He wasn't afraid to challenge assumptions, making him a true rockstar of media studies. So, next time you're doom-scrolling, remember McLuhan was probably onto something!
