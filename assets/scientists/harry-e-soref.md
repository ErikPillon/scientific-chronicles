Back in the early 20th century, if you wanted to secure something, you often needed a hefty, custom-made lock. That is, until Harry E. Soref came along. This American inventor and entrepreneur was a bit of a security savant. After working with various lock manufacturers, he spotted a gap in the market: affordable, strong, and standardized padlocks for everyday folks. In 1921, he founded Master Lock Company, initially operating out of a small garage in Milwaukee. His genius was in streamlining manufacturing processes, creating robust laminated steel padlocks that were tough to crack but easy on the wallet. Soref basically put reliable security into the hands of millions, making sure your bike, locker, or shed stood a better chance against sticky fingers. He was the original lock whisperer!
