When you hike through Yosemite National Park and marvel at its stunning glacial features, you have François-Emile Matthes to thank for understanding how they got there. This American geologist and topographer, working for the U.S. Geological Survey in the early 20th century, was obsessive about details. He spent years meticulously mapping the park, revealing the powerful sculpting hand of ancient glaciers with unprecedented precision. His work wasn't just about pretty maps; it was fundamental to understanding glacial erosion and shaping our knowledge of one of America's most iconic landscapes. He saw a story in every rock and ridge, and he told it beautifully through his science.
