Before Elias Howe, making clothes was a real stitch-by-stitch slog. Then came Howe, who in 1846 patented the lockstitch sewing machine, complete with an eye-pointed needle and an automatic feed. His invention was a game-changer, dramatically increasing efficiency in garment manufacturing and forever altering the textile industry. He faced patent battles, most notably with Isaac Singer, but eventually won recognition and royalties for his groundbreaking work. Thanks to Howe, the world got dressed a whole lot faster, and a new era of ready-to-wear fashion began!
