George Ellery Hale was basically the Tony Stark of early 20th-century astronomy, but with less iron suit and more giant mirrors. This American astrophysicist was obsessed with the sun and built not one, but *several* record-breaking telescopes – Yerkes, Mount Wilson, and Palomar – practically single-handedly. He invented the spectroheliograph to study the sun's surface and was instrumental in proving that sunspots are magnetic. Hale didn't just look at the stars; he created the institutions and instruments that made modern astrophysics possible, turning the US into a global powerhouse for peering into the cosmos. He was a true titan who dreamt big and built even bigger!
