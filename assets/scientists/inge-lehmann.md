Inge Lehmann, a Danish seismologist, totally rocked the world of geology – literally! Back in the 1930s, when everyone thought Earth's core was just one big molten blob, Lehmann looked at seismic wave data (the wiggles from earthquakes) and said, "Hold up, folks, there's more to it!" She deduced that hidden within the liquid outer core was a solid inner core. Talk about an 'aha!' moment that completely reshaped our understanding of our own planet's insides. She kept on working and publishing well into her 90s, proving that curiosity has no retirement age. What a legend!
