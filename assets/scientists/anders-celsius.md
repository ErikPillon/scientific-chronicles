Ever wonder who gave us the super sensible temperature scale? Meet Anders Celsius, the Swedish astronomer who basically owned the 18th-century observatory scene. But his real claim to fame? He cooked up the 'centigrade' scale – though initially, he had it backward! He set 0 degrees for water's boiling point and 100 for its freezing point. Talk about a chilly inverse! It took his colleagues a bit of nudging (and a posthumous flip) to get it to the 0 for freezing, 100 for boiling we know and love today. Beyond playing with thermometers, Celsius was a big deal in astronomy, leading expeditions to measure the Earth's flattening and generally gazing at the stars with serious scientific intent. He's the guy who made sure your weather app makes sense, just with a little help from his friends.
