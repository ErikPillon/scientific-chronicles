Charles Joseph Minard wasn't just building bridges; he was building bridges to understanding complex data! This French civil engineer is a legend in the world of information visualization, long before "infographics" were even a twinkle in anyone's eye. His most famous masterpiece is the incredibly powerful "carte figurative" depicting Napoleon's disastrous Russian campaign of 1812. This single graphic masterfully combines six different variables – army size, direction, latitude, longitude, temperature, and time – to tell a devastating story of loss. It's often called the greatest statistical graphic ever drawn, a poignant and effective way to convey historical tragedy. Minard proved that sometimes, a picture really is worth a thousand numbers, especially when you want to show how a grand army dwindled to almost nothing.
