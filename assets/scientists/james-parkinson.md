James Parkinson was far more than just the guy whose name stuck to a neurological condition (though that's pretty major!). This British polymath juggled careers as a surgeon, apothecary, geologist, paleontologist, *and* a political reformer. He was the kind of person who could diagnose a rare ailment, then dig up a fossil, and *then* agitate for social change, all before lunch. His detailed 1817 'An Essay on the Shaking Palsy' was a medical masterpiece, meticulously documenting the symptoms of a condition no one had properly understood before. A true intellectual heavyweight who literally shaped our understanding of human health and the Earth's ancient past.
