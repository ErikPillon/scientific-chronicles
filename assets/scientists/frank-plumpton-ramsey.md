Frank P. Ramsey was a British brainiac who crammed a lifetime of genius into just 26 years. This astonishing polymath worked across mathematics, philosophy, and economics with such profound insight that his ideas still echo today. He tackled everything from the foundations of probability and utility theory to the concept of truth and even contributed to the development of game theory (yes, that game theory!). His work on 'Ramsey numbers' in combinatorics is legendary, inspiring the whimsical thought that 'complete disorder is impossible.' Tragically, he died young, leaving us to wonder what more he might have achieved. A true intellectual supernova whose light shone incredibly bright, even if briefly.
