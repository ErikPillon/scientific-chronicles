Before James Hutton, most people thought Earth was a geological toddler. Then this Scottish gentleman farmer and physician came along, peered at rocks, and declared, 'Hold on, folks, this planet is ANCIENT!' He's justly crowned the 'Father of Modern Geology' for his radical ideas of uniformitarianism and deep time. Hutton proposed that the slow, consistent geological processes we observe today – erosion, sedimentation, volcanism – have always been at work, shaping Earth over unimaginably vast eons, not just a few thousand years. His observations, especially at places like Siccar Point, were a cosmic revelation, laying the bedrock for how we understand geological change. Essentially, he gave Earth its proper, incredibly old, birth certificate.
