When it comes to making things quieter (or louder, if you're into radio), Hiram Percy Maxim was your guy! Born into an inventing dynasty (his dad, Sir Hiram Stevens Maxim, invented the machine gun), young Hiram decided to make a different kind of noise – or rather, un-noise. In 1908, he patented the firearm sound suppressor, or 'silencer,' a device that’s become legendary in spy thrillers and action movies. But his quiet genius didn't stop there. He also developed the first successful mufflers for internal combustion engines, making early automobiles a lot less racket-y. And if that wasn't enough, this polymath was a huge radio enthusiast, co-founding the American Radio Relay League (ARRL), essentially creating the backbone for amateur radio communication in the US. Talk about a man who knew how to control sound waves, whether for stealth, comfort, or conversation!
