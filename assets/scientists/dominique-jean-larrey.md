Meet Dominique-Jean Larrey, the rockstar surgeon of the Napoleonic Wars who quite literally invented speed in battlefield medicine. While others were still debating where to put the wounded, Larrey was rolling out his 'flying ambulances' – horse-drawn carriages designed to scoop up soldiers right from the front lines. Talk about house calls! He championed triage, operating on those who needed it most, regardless of rank, and performed countless amputations with impressive speed and (for the era) surprisingly success. Napoleon himself admired Larrey, declaring him 'the most virtuous man I have ever known.' This guy didn't just mend broken bones; he pioneered humane and efficient military healthcare, proving that even amidst chaos, compassion and innovation can save lives and forever change how we approach war casualties.
