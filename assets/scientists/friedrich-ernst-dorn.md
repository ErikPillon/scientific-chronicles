Ever heard of radon? That naturally occurring radioactive gas that you sometimes need to test for in your basement? Well, you can thank Friedrich Ernst Dorn for finding it! This German physicist got curious about radium's decay products and, in 1900, identified a brand new, heavier gas emanating from it. He initially called it 'Radium Emanation,' which is a bit of a mouthful, but it was later christened radon. It was a crucial discovery that shed more light on radioactivity and the periodic table's most aloof elements – the noble gases. Talk about a groundbreaking gas find!
