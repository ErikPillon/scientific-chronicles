Frank C. DeSua was one of those unsung heroes of technology who helped shape modern life, even if his name isn't on every history book cover. An electrical engineer, he was instrumental in developing magnetrons – those clever devices that power everything from wartime radar (helping win WWII!) to the microwave oven in your kitchen. His work at Westinghouse wasn't just about making gadgets; it was about harnessing electromagnetic waves to solve big problems and invent new conveniences. So, the next time you zap a snack, give a silent nod to folks like DeSua, who made that speedy warmth possible!
