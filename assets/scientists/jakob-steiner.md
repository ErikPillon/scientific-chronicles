This Swiss mathematical wizard was so good at geometry, some called him 'the greatest geometer since Euclid.' Jakob Steiner had a serious beef with algebra, preferring to tackle complex problems purely with geometric intuition, using only a straightedge and compass (well, conceptually!). He practically wrote the book on synthetic geometry, proving theorems about conic sections and surfaces with an elegance that left his contemporaries in awe. His work on Steiner's Porism and the Steiner tree problem still makes mathematicians scratch their heads and then smile in appreciation. Forget the numbers; Steiner was all about shapes and their beautiful, intricate properties, making complex geometric problems look easy. What a guy!
