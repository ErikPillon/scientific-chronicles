Gordon Gould was the physicist who basically said, "Let there be light... amplified!" While often overshadowed in the public eye, Gould was the brains behind the term "LASER" (Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation) and described how one could actually build such a device in a notebook in 1957. Unfortunately, he delayed filing his patent and got tangled in a nearly three-decade-long legal battle over its invention. He eventually won significant patent rights, but not without immense personal and financial cost. His story is a wild ride of scientific brilliance, bureaucratic headaches, and proving that sometimes, being first to describe something is just as important as being first to build it. What a saga!
