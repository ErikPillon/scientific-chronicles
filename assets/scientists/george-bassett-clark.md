George Bassett Clark wasn't just an astronomer; he was the master craftsman who gave astronomers their eyes to the universe. As part of the famed Alvan Clark & Sons firm, this American optical genius produced some of the finest refracting telescopes of the 19th century. His lenses were so precise, they allowed for groundbreaking discoveries, including the companion star of Sirius. Clark's work wasn't about theories, it was about creating the tools that made new theories possible, helping humanity peek further and clearer into the cosmos.
