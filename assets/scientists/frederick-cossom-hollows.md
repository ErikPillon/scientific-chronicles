If there was ever a doctor who truly lived by the Hippocratic Oath with a hefty dose of grit and cheeky irreverence, it was Fred Hollows. This New Zealand-born, Australian-made ophthalmologist was a force of nature, famous not just for his surgical skill but for his fierce belief that everyone, regardless of their postcode or bank balance, deserved to see. He didn't just treat patients in comfortable clinics; he took his expertise to remote Aboriginal communities and developing nations, often setting up makeshift operating theatres under trees or in tents. Hollows championed affordable eye care and was instrumental in training local people to continue his work, quite literally giving sight back to millions. His enduring legacy lives on through The Fred Hollows Foundation, proving that one determined doctor, with a clear vision and no patience for excuses, can indeed change the world, one pair of eyes at a time.
