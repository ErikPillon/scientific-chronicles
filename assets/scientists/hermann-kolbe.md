Hermann Kolbe was one of those chemists who loved to build molecules from scratch, making him a true architect of organic chemistry. This influential German chemist was a pioneer in organic synthesis, famously developing the 'Kolbe electrolysis' for producing alkanes and the 'Kolbe-Schmitt reaction' for synthesizing salicylic acid (a key component of aspirin). He was a staunch advocate for the idea that organic compounds could be formed from inorganic ones, challenging vitalistic theories of the time. Kolbe's methodical approach and practical syntheses not only expanded the repertoire of known organic compounds but also solidified the foundations of valency theory, proving that sometimes, you just need to zap a few molecules to make something entirely new and exciting.
