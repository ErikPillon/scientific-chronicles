Before 'big data' was a thing, Herman Hollerith was already thinking big. This American inventor and statistician was faced with the monumental task of counting the entire U.S. population for the 1890 census, a job so tedious it nearly broke the previous system. Hollerith's brilliant solution? Punched cards! His electromechanical tabulating machine could process these cards at lightning speed, drastically cutting down the census time and saving a fortune. Essentially, he invented the original 'spreadsheet' long before computers were even a twinkle in anyone's eye. His company eventually became a cornerstone of IBM, proving that sometimes, the best way to count your chickens is with a very clever machine.
