Meet Georg von Békésy, the Hungarian-American genius who literally got to the bottom of how we hear! This biophysicist meticulously investigated the human ear, particularly the cochlea, unraveling the secrets of how sound waves are translated into nerve signals. His groundbreaking work earned him a Nobel Prize in 1961 and forever changed our understanding of auditory perception. So, next time you enjoy your favorite tune, give a nod to von Békésy for figuring out how your ear actually *hears* it.
