George W. Crane was the kind of psychologist who believed practical advice could change lives, and he proved it by reaching millions! This American academic and author became a household name through his long-running syndicated newspaper column, 'The Worry Clinic,' which offered common-sense solutions to everyday problems. He basically brought psychology out of the ivory tower and into people's living rooms, long before self-help gurus were a thing. With a knack for making complex ideas simple and actionable, Crane taught people how to manage stress, improve relationships, and boost productivity. He might not have discovered a new particle, but he certainly helped countless individuals navigate the 'particles' of daily life with more confidence.
