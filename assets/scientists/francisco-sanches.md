Meet Francisco Sanches, born around 1550, a Portuguese physician and philosopher who was basically the ultimate skeptic before skepticism was cool. He was tired of blindly accepting ancient medical and philosophical dogmas, shouting (metaphorically, we assume) 'Nil sciri!' (Nothing is known) at a world that thought it had all the answers. Sanches emphasized observation and reason over unquestioning faith in authority, a truly revolutionary idea for his time. His critical approach laid crucial groundwork for the scientific method, pushing thinkers to question, test, and prove. He was the intellectual equivalent of hitting the refresh button on an entire era's thinking, inspiring future generations to pursue knowledge through empirical investigation rather than dogma.
