Ever spoken into a microphone? Chances are, James West's work was behind it! This American acoustician is a true unsung hero of modern sound technology. Along with Gerhard Sessler, he co-invented the foil electret microphone in 1962. Before this, microphones were clunky and expensive. West's design was small, inexpensive, and incredibly sensitive, making it perfect for telephones, hearing aids, and eventually, virtually every electronic device that needs to pick up sound. It's estimated that 90% of microphones used today are based on his electret design. From your smartphone to professional studios, his innovation is literally everywhere, making our voices heard, quietly and efficiently.
