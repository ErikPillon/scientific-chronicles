Before he was half of a luxury car empire, Charles Stewart Rolls was a thrill-seeker with a need for speed. This dashing British aristocrat wasn't just passionate about cars; he was one of the earliest motor car dealers in Britain and famously co-founded Rolls-Royce with Henry Royce. But Charles wasn't content with just four wheels on the ground. He had an insatiable craving for the sky, becoming an avid balloonist and then one of Britain's first qualified pilots. He holds the rather unfortunate distinction of being the first Briton to die in an aviation accident in 1910, a stark reminder of the risks faced by these early pioneers. His legacy, however, lives on, embodying elegance and groundbreaking engineering, both on the road and in the air.
