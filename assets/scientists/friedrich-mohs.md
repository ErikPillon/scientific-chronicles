Friedrich Mohs was a mineralogist who scratched his way into history! In 1812, this German scientist developed what is now famously known as the Mohs scale of mineral hardness. Before Mohs, trying to describe how 'hard' a rock was felt a bit like guessing. He gave us a simple, comparative scale from 1 (hello, talc!) to 10 (sup, diamond!), based on which mineral could scratch another. It's so intuitive and practical that geologists, jewelers, and even kindergarteners still use it today. Mohs proved that sometimes the simplest ideas are the hardest to beat, making rock classification a real gem!
