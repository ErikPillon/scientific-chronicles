Charles Davis Hollister was no stranger to the deep blue, or rather, the deep, dark, mysterious ocean floor. This American oceanographer was a pioneer in understanding the geological processes of the abyssal plains, those vast, relatively unknown stretches beneath the waves. He co-authored *The Face of the Deep*, a seminal book that gave the world an unprecedented look at deep-sea photography and the strange creatures and landscapes found there. Hollister spent decades with the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, pushing the boundaries of deep-sea exploration and making groundbreaking discoveries about everything from sediment transport to radioactive waste disposal in the ocean. He showed us that the bottom of the sea is far from boring!
