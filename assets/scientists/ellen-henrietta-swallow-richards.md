Ellen Swallow Richards was a force of nature who basically invented modern sustainable living before 'sustainable' was even a buzzword. As the very first woman admitted to MIT (and probably made quite a few gentlemen splutter into their tea), she didn't just attend; she revolutionized. She applied scientific principles to everyday life, tackling issues like water quality, air pollution, and nutrition. She founded the field of 'home economics' (which was far more rigorous than just baking cookies; think applying chemistry to diet and engineering to household management) and was a pioneer in sanitary engineering. Richards believed that science could improve quality of life for everyone, turning homes into efficient, healthy environments. She proved that you could be a brilliant scientist and still be totally practical – a true domestic science goddess!
