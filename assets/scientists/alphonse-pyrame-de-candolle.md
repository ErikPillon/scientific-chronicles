Alphonse Pyrame de Candolle was a Swiss botanical heavyweight who didn't just study plants; he helped define the rules for how we classify and name them. Carrying on his famous father's legacy, he meticulously worked on the *Prodromus Systematis Naturalis Regni Vegetabilis*, a monumental catalog of plants. More importantly, he championed systematic rules for botanical nomenclature, essentially creating the "rulebook" for naming plants, which forms the basis of what botanists still use today. He was also a pioneer in phytogeography, studying how plants are distributed across the globe. He literally helped put plants on the map, both taxonomically and geographically!
